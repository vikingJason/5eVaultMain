I **love** the direction of **Sael'Loreth**. It immediately feels like an ancient Tyrellion name, and the apostrophe has a purpose rather than just looking fantasy-esque.

I'd make one small adjustment to the meaning to better align with the linguistic roots we've established.

### Breaking down the name

|Element|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**Sael**|Sacred, blessed, consecrated|
|**'**|A _Resonance Break_—a pause indicating the settlement's name was altered by a major magical event, such as a Heartstone awakening or the Great Shattering. Only a handful of Tyrellion cities bear this mark.|
|**Lor**|Wisdom, knowledge, memory|
|**-eth**|Ancient place, enduring settlement|

Rather than "Home of Ancient Wisdom," I'd translate it as:

> **"The Sacred Seat of Ancient Wisdom."**

or

> **"The Sacred Sanctuary of Ancient Wisdom."**

Both feel a little more ceremonial, like the name was _revealed_ rather than coined.

## The Resonance Break (')

I think you've accidentally stumbled onto something that could become uniquely Tyrellion.

Instead of treating the apostrophe as punctuation, make it **a written representation of a silent rune**.

The Tyrellion call it the **Resonance Break** (_Vael'Shyn_).

It signifies that the name changed because the land itself changed.

Examples:

- **Sael'Loreth** — A Heartstone awakened here.
- **Vael'Tharis** — The city's Heartstone harmonized with another across Calindor.
- **Syl'Vaen** — The forest absorbed powerful fey magic.
- **Thal'Zharen** — The Guardian City after its corruption by Zagra'gatra'lox.

Outsiders usually pronounce the apostrophe as a pause. Tyrellion don't pause—they subtly shift the pitch of the word, almost like singing a different note. It's one of the reasons non-Tyrellion can never quite pronounce their place names correctly.

## The City Itself

Given your established lore, I'd make **Sael'Loreth** one of the greatest cities of the old Aetherwing Dominion.

### Sael'Loreth

**The Sacred Seat of Ancient Wisdom**

Before the Great Shattering, Sael'Loreth was the spiritual and intellectual heart of the Tyrellion. It housed the **Grand Resonance Archive**, where crystal memories from across Calindor were preserved within vast Heartstone lattices. Here, Guardians were chosen, eggs were interpreted, and engineers worked alongside mystics to ensure that knowledge and technology remained in harmony.

The greatest achievement of Sael'Loreth was not a weapon or a flying citadel, but the **Living Archive**—a network of Heartstones capable of storing not only history, but emotion, memory, music, and even fragments of personality. The Tyrellion believed that wisdom was more than information; it was the lived experiences of countless generations resonating together.

When the Great Shattering occurred, much of the Living Archive fractured. Some memories were lost forever. Others still linger within the crystal halls, waiting for voices that know the ancient songs to awaken them once more.

I also have an idea that I think fits the Tyrellion perfectly:

Rather than every settlement ending in **-eth** or **-ion**, what if **every major Tyrellion city was named after one of the Seven Virtues of the Aetherwing Dominion**?

For example:

- **Sael'Loreth** — Wisdom
- **Thal'Vaerin** — Duty
- **Lyr'Saelis** — Harmony
- **Vael'Koryn** — Discovery
- **Oryn'Thalis** — Reflection
- **Kyr'Aestren** — Aspiration
- **Syl'Myrath** — Stewardship

Together, those names would subtly tell the story of what the Tyrellion valued as a civilization, and they'd give every ancient ruin an immediate sense of purpose before the players ever step inside. I think that would reinforce the idea that Tyrellion cities weren't just places—they were embodiments of ideals.
