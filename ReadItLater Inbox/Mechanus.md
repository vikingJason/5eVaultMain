# [Mechanus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus)

The **Clockwork Nirvana of Mechanus**, sometimes called **Nirvana**,[\[10\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP3e-127-10) was an [Outer Plane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Plane "Outer Plane") in the [Great Wheel cosmology](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Wheel_cosmology "Great Wheel cosmology") representing the alignment of [lawful neutral](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lawful_neutral "Lawful neutral")[\[8\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP1e-73-8) and home of the [modrons](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Modron "Modron").[\[1\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP1e-84-85-1)[\[2\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MMII1e-86-2)[\[11\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-DMG5e-p58-11)

“

Mechanus is a place of *rules*. Law and process. *Organization above all*. The denizens of this place disagree on how order is to be *maintained*, but in the end we *all* serve its *structure*. Without structure, we are *nothing*.

”

— A [parai](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Parai "Parai"), a creature native to Nirvana.[\[12\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-EABG11-12)

## Description\[[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus?veaction=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Description")\]

“

You are hereby exiled from the glory that is Mechanus! If you should ever return, even greater punishments shall be leveled against you, including but not limited to severe fines, imprisonment, hard labor, loss of limbs, loss of sight, [geas](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Geas "Geas") magic, modron cleaning, modron counting, legal file alphabetization...

”

— A judge (fortunately) banishing someone from Mechanus.[\[13\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-EBG3p18-13)

This plane was filled with an infinite number of country-sized circular interlocking gears which were habitable on one or both sides. These great flat wheels were at least 1,000 miles (1,600 kilometers) in diameter and had teeth that meshed at right angles, all turning slowly in synchronicity. Each disk had its own gravity that operated in a sphere circumscribing it exactly and pulling normal to the top and bottom surfaces.[\[1\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP1e-84-85-1) The void between the gears was filled with air, allowing easy flight. Outside a gravity sphere, objects would feel only very weak attraction to nearby disks.[\[1\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP1e-84-85-1)

A plane of Law and order, Nirvana had equal amounts of light and dark, heat and cold, and equal measures of the four elements. There was a place for everything and everything was in its place. Even individuals living here existed to fit into the greater scheme of things and thus achieve a perfect society. There was no pain, but the only pleasure was in successfully filling a role like a gear in a machine. Passion, fantasy, and illusion did not exist here, except for what might be brought in by visitors, and any who tried to foment individualism or stir passions for a cause soon found they were not well received.[\[1\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP1e-84-85-1)

Nirvana was connected to the [Prime Material Planes](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Prime_Material_Plane "Prime Material Plane") via the [Astral Plane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Plane "Astral Plane"), and to the [Outer Planes](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Planes "Outer Planes") of [Arcadia](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arcadia "Arcadia"), [Acheron](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Acheron "Acheron"), and [Concordant Opposition](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Concordant_Opposition "Concordant Opposition") by portals which appeared once every revolution of the gears they belonged to. The period of rotation depended on the size of the gear, but a typical period was twenty days. The portals stayed open for exactly one day. There was one portal to Concordant Opposition, located at the center of the featureless bottom side of a large gear, that was continually open.[\[1\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MotP1e-84-85-1)

## Inhabitants\[[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus?veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Inhabitants")\]

The only creatures native to Nirvana were the [modrons](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Modron "Modron"), a race of beings organized in a rigid caste system and ruled by [Primus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Primus "Primus"), the One and the Prime. The most common modrons were the monodrones,[\[2\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MMII1e-86-2) single-function drones used as common laborers, servants, or soldiers.[\[14\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-MMII1e-87-14)

## History\[[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus?veaction=edit&section=4 "Edit section: History")\]

After [Orcus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Orcus "Orcus") killed Primus and triggered an unexpected [Great Modron March](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Modron_March "Great Modron March"), he left the Energy Pool in [Regulus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Regulus "Regulus") to search for the *[Wand of Orcus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wand_of_Orcus "Wand of Orcus")*, leaving the way open for a [secundus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Secundus "Secundus") to be promoted into the new Primus. However, prolonged contact with the Pool caused Orcus's [evil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Evil "Evil") influence to taint a fraction of the modrons. One tainted secundus disputed the ascension of any one of the other three and rebelled, leaving Mechanus and taking almost a million modrons with him. The dissidents settled a modron colony in [Acheron](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Acheron "Acheron"), where they regrouped to later attempt to retake the plane. This schism crippled the modron race and paved the way for a [formian](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Formian "Formian") invasion of Mechanus.[\[36\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-Dr354-p36-36)

In the late 15th century [DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/DR "DR"), the [wild mage](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wild_mage "Wild mage") [Delina](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Delina "Delina") was sent to Mechanus by Martikay in order to search for a way to cure her from her wild magic surges. After interacting with several modrons, she was brought to trial by the [Fraternity of Order](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fraternity_of_Order "Fraternity of Order") at Regulus under charges of being an agent of chaos. She was found guilty and summarily sentenced to exile from the plane. The experience allowed Delina to better understand, rather than control, her wild magic.[\[37\]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus#cite_note-EBG3-37)
