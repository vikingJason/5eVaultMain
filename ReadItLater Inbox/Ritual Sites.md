# Ritual Sites | 10 Unique Rituals on a Mountainside+ Beholder Token



_"A ritual is a rather vague term. What is essentially meant by that word is the careful retracing of actions, energies, movements, and words to produce a result. More often than not, such rituals include an element of equal exchange. To gain, you must also give. Not all offerings are evil nor malevolent, but it is far simpler and more beneficial to offer blood rather than sacrifice something of your own..."_

### **1. Blood Ritual**

A dark ritual nears completion, the air heavy with the scent of blood. Tooth-like pillars rise in a jagged circle, their bases slick with crimson offerings. Ten towering obelisks pulse with power, their chains binding a coffin—whether a sacrifice or a sealed entity is uncertain. The cultists work frantically, their chanting reaching a fevered pitch as the final moments of the ritual approach. If the chains break, something ancient and terrible will be unleashed. If the ritual is interrupted, the backlash could be just as deadly.

### **2. Magic Crystal Ritual**

A swirling storm of unstable arcane energy dominates the ritual site, a massive crystalline formation at its center pulsing with raw magic. The runes of the ritual circle flicker and warp, their once-controlled pattern shifting unpredictably. Each moment brings a new surge of wild magic. The cultists struggle to maintain control, their leader desperately attempting to stabilize the ritual before the mountain itself is consumed by the chaos.

### **3. Nature Ritual**

Atop the mountain, four ancient stones stand as silent sentinels around a central slab, their surfaces covered in twisting vines. The scent of damp earth and blooming flowers pleasantly fills the air around you. A druidic circle gathers here, performing a sacred rite to awaken a slumbering force of nature. Whether their intentions are pure or misguided, the ritual is nearing completion, and the power they summon may not be willing to obey.

### **4. Massive Black Obelisk**

A towering black obelisk looms over the ritual site, its surface impossibly smooth, its purpose unfathomable. While completely black and smooth, you think you see forms appearing on it, yet disappear almost instantaneously. The closer one approaches, the stronger the pull—a whispering presence gnawing at the edges of the mind. As the ritual unfolds, visions of a lost civilization flicker into existence, and something from the past begins to stir, reaching across time to reclaim what was once its own.

### **5. Beholder Summoning**

Four orbs stand equidistant around a purplish-sphere of energy, their arcane light locking it in place. Within, a beholder thrashes, its eye stalks flickering with energy as it tests the weakening barrier. "YOU WON'T HOLD ME FOR MUCH LONGER, I WON'T SERVE LOWER BEINGS!", the beholder screeches. The mages maintaining the prison are faltering—whether from exhaustion or the whispers of the creature itself is unclear. If the barrier collapses, the aberration will be free to unleash its full wrath. But the ritual maintaining the seal is delicate, and tampering with it could have unpredictable consequences.

### **6. Giant Maw**

The ground here is no longer solid stone but slick, pulsing flesh, as if the mountain itself has begun to rot. A massive, gaping maw yawns open at the center of the site, viscous slime seeping from between jagged, uneven teeth. Cultists in tattered robes stand at the precipice, chanting as they push offerings—both living and dead—into the abyss. A thick, rancid mist rises from below, carrying with it the echoes of distant screams. Something in the darkness shifts. Something is awake.

### **7. Massive Pitch-Black Ravine**

A sheer, unnatural chasm splits the mountainside, the black void below seeming to swallow all light. There is no bottom, no echo when a stone is dropped, only an endless, suffocating nothingness. Those who stand too close hear whispers in a language they do not know but somehow understand. A stone platform seems to extend unnaturally in the middle of the chasm...almost urging you to take a leap.

### **8. Eldritch Ritual**

The ground pulses as if alive, veins of dark energy twisting across the ritual site. A gargantuan portal glowing with sickly purple and crimson light, and adorn with strange shadows that seem to jump from the light writhes like a living thing, tendrils of energy creeping outward. From the center, something emerges—tentacles, shifting forms, eyes that blink in unnatural patterns. The cultists chant in tongues not meant for mortal speech, their bodies contorted by the power flowing through them. Whatever is being called forth is neither god nor demon, but something beyond comprehension, and it is already taking root in the land itself.

### **9. Explosion Blast**

A crater dominates the mountainside, jagged rock and scorched earth marking the site of an arcane disaster. Broken ritual stones and shattered sigils lay scattered, their lingering magic sparking unpredictably. The bodies of the cultists remain, frozen in expressions of terror, their forms twisted by the raw power unleashed here. The air crackles with unstable energy, and something still lingers—a fragment of whatever force they attempted to summon, now trapped between worlds, searching for a way to finish what was started.

### **10. Haunted Ritual**

Seven stone sarcophagi form a perfect circle around a lone statue—a knight upon a rearing horse, sword raised high. A ghostly light flickers from within the tombs, drifting slowly toward the statue as if drawn by some unseen force. The ritual at work here is ancient, meant to bind the souls of fallen warriors into a single vessel. If the rite completes, the statue will awaken, no longer a mere carving of stone but an amalgamation of the dead, a spectral knight of unfathomable power. Whether it will serve as a protector or a curse remains unknown.
