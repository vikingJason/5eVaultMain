> _“You think you know shadow? I was born from it.”_



She is now **bound by a pact with a Shadow Fiend sealed within the Amber Temple**, serving as its assassin and soul collector.

Seraphine hunts those who meddle with the Fanes or seek the Amber Temple, seeing herself as the Temple’s “hand of balance.”

> **Hit Points** 90 (12d8 + 24)  
> **Speed** 30 ft
>

>

> **Saving Throws** Dex +7, Cha +6  
> **Skills** Stealth +10, Deception +6, Sleight of Hand +7, Perception +4, Arcana +4  
> **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft (Devil’s Sight), Passive Perception 14  
> **Languages** Common, Infernal, Abyssal  
> **Challenge** 6 (2,300 XP) **Proficiency Bonus** +3
> 
> ---
> 
> _**Fiendish Blessing.**_ When she reduces a creature to 0 HP, she gains 5 temporary HP.
> 
> _**Cunning Action.**_ She can Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.
> 
> _**Assassinate.**_ During the first round of combat, Seraphine has advantage on attack rolls against creatures that haven’t acted yet. Any hit against a surprised creature is a critical hit.
> 
> _**Shadow Step (Recharge 4–6).**_ As a bonus action, Seraphine can teleport up to 30 ft between dim light or darkness.
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Actions
> 
> **Multiattack.** Seraphine makes two attacks with her Shadow Dagger or one Eldritch Blast and one dagger strike.
> 
> **Shadow Dagger.** _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft or range 20/60 ft, one target. _Hit:_ 9 (1d8+5) piercing + 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.  
> On a critical hit, the target must make a DC 14 Con save or be Blinded until the end of its next turn.
> 
> **Eldritch Blast.** _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 120 ft, one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d10+3) force damage.
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Bonus Actions
> 
> **Hex (1/day).** Cast _Hex_ before attacking, adding 1d6 necrotic damage to her attacks.  
> **Cunning Action.** Dash, Hide, or Disengage.
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Reactions
> 
> **Uncanny Dodge.** Halve the damage from one attack that hits her.
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Spells (Warlock 2)
> 
> **Spell Save DC:** 14 | **+6 to hit**  
> **At-will:** _Eldritch Blast, Thaumaturgy_  
> **1st–2nd (2 slots):** _Armor of Agathys, Hex, Darkness_
> 
> _**Eldritch Invocations:**_ _Agonizing Blast, Devil’s Sight_
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Lair & Tactics
> 
> **Lair:** The ruins of a forgotten chapel near the slopes of Mount Ghakis, where dim light and shadows shift unnaturally.
> 
> **Opening Ambush:** Begins hidden (Stealth +10). Uses _Darkness_ centered on her dagger, relying on Devil’s Sight to fight in pitch black.  
> **Tactics:**
> 
> - Begins with _Hex_ → _Eldritch Blast_ or surprise dagger critical.
>     
> - Uses _Cunning Action (Hide)_ after every strike.
>     
> - Retreats with _Shadow Step_ into darkness or higher ground.
>     
> - If reduced below 20 HP, casts _Armor of Agathys_ and vanishes into shadow, possibly returning later.
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Flavor
> 
> **Physical:** Dusky crimson skin, long curling horns wrapped in silver thread, eyes glowing with a faint violet ember. Her cloak moves like ink in water.  
> **Voice:** Calm, low, with an unsettling melody — like she’s half-whispering to someone unseen.  
> _**“The shadows whisper truths the light never dares to.”**_
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Treasure / Hooks
> 
> - **Her Dagger, “Umbracall”:** (Requires attunement, rare) +1 weapon, deals +1d6 necrotic in dim light or darkness. Once per day, cast _Darkness_.
>     
> - **Fragment of Amber:** Glows faintly when near the Amber Temple; whispers promises of power.
>     
> - A **half-burned page** from her contract, naming the same patron that may be calling to one of your Warlocks or linked to the Fanes.
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Scaling Options
> 
> - **For stronger parties:** Add Mirror Image (via an Eldritch Invocation).
>     
> - **For weaker parties:** Remove Armor of Agathys or reduce HP to 70.
>     
> - **For narrative tension:** She can flee mid-fight and return as a recurring assassin or potential uneasy ally.
>
