### **3. Strahd’s Dinner—A Game of Shadows**

- **Strahd welcomes them as honored guests** in the grand dining hall.
- **Seraz sits at the table—pale, bound in arcane chains, visibly tortured.**
- Strahd is **not hostile**—instead, he is **charming, amused, playing the long game.**

#### **Strahd’s Dialogue (Calm, Almost Friendly)**

_"You have been lied to. Manipulated. Vladimir is lost to bitterness. Do you think he even remembers what he fights for?"_

**(Gestures to Seraz, giving a mocking sigh.)**  
_"I offered him the same thing I offer you—freedom. The right to leave Barovia and be done with all of this… but he is so **devoted** to his suffering. A shame, really."_

_"But you, dear guests, may still be spared. A simple agreement: renounce the mad ghosts of the past, swear **never** to take up arms against me, and I shall release Seraz to you unharmed. More than that—leave Barovia, **and I will let you go.**"_

> **Branching Outcomes:**
> 
> - If they **agree**, Strahd **releases Seraz but brands them cowards**.
> - If they **refuse or insult Strahd**, he darkens and delivers his **final warning.**

## **2. Seraz - Revenant-Touched Vampire Thrall**

Seraz was once a spy for the Order, **captured and broken** by Strahd. However, when he was forced to drink Strahd’s blood, something went **wrong**—a past soul within him resisted.

He is found **chained at the dinner**, visibly tortured, **fighting against Strahd’s will.**

### **Roleplay Mechanics**

- Seraz **attempts to resist Strahd’s commands** (rolling a contested **Wisdom save vs. Strahd’s Charisma**)
- If the party **frees** him, he becomes an ally but **dies shortly after warning them about the Fanes.**
- If he fails, **he turns on the party** in a tragic fight.

## **3. Argynvostholt Attack (Tactical Combat)**

|Enemy Type|Tactics|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**Vistani Scouts (x4)**|Archers using **silvered weapons**|Move in groups, pick off stragglers|
|**Vampire Knight (x1)**|Duelist, closes in fast|Paragon Actions keep pressure high|
|**Ghoul Assassins (x2)**|Climb walls, attack from above|Prioritize spellcasters|

#### **Encounter Gimmick: "Hold the Line"**

Vladimir is buying them time, but **if the fight lasts more than 3 rounds, reinforcements arrive.** The party must either:

1. **Retreat**, avoiding overwhelming forces.
2. **Find a creative way to collapse part of the manor** to cut off enemies.

## **4. Marcus’s Trial - Exorcism of the Vampyr Pact**

To be **ordained as a Silver Dragon Knight**, Marcus must undergo a **trial at the Abbey of St. Markovia.**

#### **Trial Gimmick: "Shadow Within"**

- The **Vampyr manifests as an illusion**, forcing Marcus to **relive** past choices.
- He **must choose between power and purity**, making **three Wisdom saving throws (DC 16)**:
    - **Success:** He resists the temptation.
    - **Fail:** He loses **spell slots temporarily** as the curse fights back.

## **5. The Fanes - Restoring Barovia’s Lost Power**

Strahd is actively **weakening the Fanes**, making **ritual sites battlefields**. Each site has:

- **Corrupted Druids** performing blood magic.
- **Shambling Horrors** (Undead Treants, Blighted Beasts).
- **A "Heart of Corruption"** that must be purified.
