### **The High Council of the Silver Dragons**

1. **Grandmaster of the Argent Scale**
    
    - The supreme leader, embodying the Order’s ideals. Presides over all councils and holds final authority in matters of war and justice.
        
2. **High Commander of the Dawn**
    
    - Second-in-command and military strategist. Oversees the Order’s armies and coordinates defenses against threats like Strahd.
        
3. **Lord/Lady Justiciar**
    
    - Chief arbiter of law and ethics. Judges internal disputes and ensures the Order’s code is upheld, even in dark times.
        
4. **Warden of the Ebon Keep**
    
    - Guardian of the Order’s primary stronghold. Manages fortifications and leads regional defenses in Barovia’s treacherous lands.
        
5. **Keeper of the Argent Vault**
    
    - Protector of sacred relics and treasurer. Safeguards magical artifacts and the Order’s historical treasures from corruption.
        
6. **Sentinel Prime**
    
    - Master of scouts and spies. Gathers intelligence on enemies (e.g., Strahd’s minions) and oversees covert operations.
        
7. **Herald of the Silver Dawn**
    
    - Diplomat and voice of hope. Liaises with allies (like the Keepers of the Feather) and inspires resistance against darkness.
        
8. **Master of the Forge**
    
    - Artificer and weaponsmith. Ensures the Order is armed with silvered weapons and blessed armaments to combat undead.
        
9. **Sergeant at Arms**
    
    - Chief enforcer of council security. Maintains order during meetings and leads the personal guard of the Grandmaster.
        
10. **Chronicler of the Codex**
    
    - Historian and lorekeeper. Records the Order’s deeds and preserves ancient knowledge about Barovia’s curses and Strahd’s weaknesses.

### **Eryon Drakov - The Blade of the Sanctum**  
- **Role**: Champion and enforcer of the Order’s sacred oaths.  
- **Appearance**: A tall, broad-shouldered knight with silver-plated armor adorned with dragon motifs. His face bears a jagged scar across his left cheek, a reminder of a battle with Strahd’s minions.  
- **Personality**: Stoic, disciplined, and fiercely loyal. Eryon is a man of few words, but his actions speak volumes. He believes in leading by example and expects the same level of dedication from his fellow knights.  
- **Motivation**: Eryon seeks to protect the sanctity of the Order and its sacred sites from corruption. He harbors a deep hatred for Strahd and his undead legions, driven by the loss of his family to a vampire spawn.  
- **Plot Hooks**:  
  - Eryon tasks the party with cleansing a desecrated shrine of the Order, which has been overrun by Strahd’s forces.  
  - He may suspect a traitor within the Order and secretly asks the party to investigate.  
  - Eryon’s scar is tied to a cursed weapon wielded by a vampire spawn; he seeks to destroy it before it falls into the wrong hands.  

---

### **Alaric Vale - The Chronicler of the Codex**  
- **Role**: Keeper of the Order’s history and lore.  
- **Appearance**: A slender, scholarly man in his late 40s, with silver-streaked hair and a pair of spectacles perched on his nose. He wears a robe over light armor, with a silver dragon pendant around his neck.  
- **Personality**: Wise, meticulous, and introspective. Alaric is a quiet observer who values knowledge above all else. He is deeply empathetic, often offering counsel to troubled knights.  
- **Motivation**: Alaric is determined to preserve the Order’s legacy, even as Barovia’s darkness threatens to erase it. He believes understanding the past is key to defeating Strahd.  
- **Plot Hooks**:  
  - Alaric enlists the party to recover a lost tome containing ancient secrets about Strahd’s weaknesses.  
  - He reveals a prophecy about the party’s role in Barovia’s fate, but its meaning is cryptic and requires interpretation.  
  - Alaric’s research uncovers a hidden connection between the Order and Strahd’s family, which could shake the knights’ faith.

### **Sergei von Zarovich - Sentinel Prime**  
- **Role**: Master of scouts, spies, and covert operations.  
- **Appearance**: A lean, agile man in his early 30s, with sharp features and piercing green eyes. He wears dark, lightweight armor and carries a silver dagger at his side. His demeanor is calm and calculating.  
- **Personality**: Cunning, resourceful, and secretive. Sergei is a master of deception, able to blend into the shadows and extract information with ease. He is fiercely protective of his agents and values loyalty above all.  
- **Motivation**: Sergei is driven by a desire to outwit Strahd at his own game. He believes the key to victory lies in intelligence and subterfuge, not brute force.  
- **Plot Hooks**:  
  - Sergei recruits the party for a covert mission to infiltrate Castle Ravenloft and gather intelligence on Strahd’s plans.  
  - He reveals that one of his agents has gone missing in Vallaki, and he needs the party to investigate.  
  - Sergei’s true identity as a distant relative of Strahd is revealed, causing tension within the Order.

### **Athun - Master of the Forge**  
- **Role**: Artificer and weaponsmith of the Order.  
- **Appearance**: A burly, muscular man in his 50s, with soot-stained hands and a thick beard. His workshop is filled with the clang of hammer on anvil, and he wears a heavy apron over his armor.  
- **Personality**: Gruff but kind-hearted, Elyndor is a perfectionist who takes immense pride in his craft. He has a dry sense of humor and a soft spot for underdogs.  
- **Motivation**: Elyndor is dedicated to arming the Order with the finest weapons and armor to combat Barovia’s horrors. He dreams of forging a weapon powerful enough to strike down Strahd himself.  
- **Plot Hooks**:  
  - Elyndor asks the party to retrieve rare materials (e.g., silver ore, dragon scales) to craft a legendary weapon.  
  - He reveals that one of his creations has been stolen by Strahd’s minions and must be recovered before it’s corrupted.  
  - Elyndor’s forge is haunted by the spirit of a fallen knight, and he needs the party’s help to lay it to rest.
