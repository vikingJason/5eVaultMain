## Summary

<p>
The Great Hall of the Amber Temple yawned before them, amber walls glimmering faintly like the skin of something long-dead and preserved. Above, a forty-foot statue loomed. Cowled. Faceless. The void where its face should be wasn’t absence, it was *presence*, a darkness that drank the light instead of simply lacking it.
</p>
<p>
And then the whispers began.
</p><p>
Not a single voice, not two, *hundreds*, layered and writhing over one another in languages none of them knew but all somehow *felt*. The words tugged at the mind like fishhooks through fog.
</p><p>
Marcus halted, hand raised, his voice low and urgent.
</p><p>
“They’re up there, balconies, arrow slits. The skulls shoot fireballs. We do **not** want to be in their line of sight.”
</p><p>
Aeli tilted her head, unconcerned, her tone dry.
</p><p>
“We’re in the Amber Temple. Everything wants us dead. New day, same problem.”
</p><p>
The whispers grew sharper, digging under their thoughts, until one broke through, a single, deliberate voice. *Close. Inside.*
</p><p>
The hall splintered into corridors, each lined with sealed amber and locked doors.
</p><p>
**The First Door**, Athun pushed it open and a wave of flying books and candlesticks erupted from the dark. The books struck like thrown bricks, the candlesticks like flung daggers. Athun slammed it shut, muttering curses. Aeli leaned against the wall, unimpressed.
</p><p>
“Someone’s either very protective of their reading list… or they just *really* hate visitors.”
</p><p>
**The Second Door**, Marcus pressed his ear against it. This time the voice was direct, silky, coiling into his skull like smoke.
</p><p>
"Free me, and I will give you power."
</p><p>
He jerked back, eyes wide. Athun frowned.
</p><p>
“Trapped beings offering gifts. That’s the fastest road to damnation I know.”
</p><p>
**The Third Door**, Silence. Lars approached. Four creatures stood inside, one-eyed horrors with too many teeth, watching without moving. Lars shut the door  with a *snap*.
</p><p>
They moved swiftly to the other side of the temple, finding witches hunched over another door, whispering  in tones both plaintive and threatening. When confronted, they hissed and the air around them warped, brooms rose from the ground, bristles crackling like spearpoints.
</p><p>
Aeli’s sun blade flared gold, cutting a broom in half mid-swing. She grinned.
</p><p>
“Who’s sweeping now?”
</p><p>
Marcus, finally wielding a greatsword worthy of the title, struck with brutal precision, his blade catching the dim light as it bit through a hag’s neck. Lars finished the last one with a mace strike that left silence in its wake.
</p><p>
The whispers fled. For the first time since they entered, the Temple was *quiet*.
</p><p>
Silence didn’t last.
</p><p>
Up a stairwell, Marcus opened a door, and two flaming skulls turned toward him, green fire burning in hollow sockets. He closed the door.
</p><p>
The door exploded inward as fire washed over them. Marcus barely dodged, twisting fate itself with a portent’s whisper to deflect the blast.
</p><p>
Lars charged, Hunter’s Mark blazing across his mace as it connected with sickening finality. One skull shattered in a burst of sparks, then the other. The last fled through a wall, leaving behind a charred corpse of a long-dead wizard. In its skeletal grasp, a staff, still humming faintly with magic.
</p><p>
A side chamber revealed something stranger: a twelve-foot scale model of Castle Ravenloft, unnervingly precise. Dust coated its spires. On a nearby table sat a wooden chest containing a thick tome and a sealed map case.
</p><p>
Aeli stared at the model, brows furrowed.
</p><p>
“Someone was watching him. Planning. But for what?”
</p><p>
The Temple was far from done with them. Behind its locked doors, voices still promised power, Morganas, Yrgar, Tarachamedes. Flaming skulls still patrolled the shadows. And somewhere, hidden in these halls, were the words that could command the Temple’s secrets… or unleash its nightmares.
</p><p>
Marcus closed the chest, his voice heavy.
</p><p>
“We find what we came for. Then we leave. Before this place decides we belong to it.”
</p><p>
The Amber Temple had been silent once. Now it listened. And in the darkness behind the whispers, something waited.
</p>

## Summary - Draft

### **Into the Amber Temple: A Descent into Whispering Shadows**  

The party staggered into the **Great Hall** of the Amber Temple, their nerves frayed from the climb up the mountain. Above them loomed a **40-foot statue of a faceless, cowled figure**, its visage a void of darkness. The air hummed with **indiscriminate whispers**, voices overlapping in a language just beyond comprehension.  

**Marcus**, still shaken from their last encounter with the temple’s **flaming skulls**, warned the others in a hushed, urgent tone:  
> *"They’re up there—balconies, windows. They shoot fireballs. We do* not *want to be in their line of sight."*  

**Aeli**, ever pragmatic, shrugged.  
> *"We’re in the Amber Temple. Everything tries to kill us here. What’s new?"*  

But the whispers grew louder, more insistent—until one voice broke through the cacophony.  

---

### **The Sealed Doors and the Voices Within**  

The party moved cautiously, testing doors along the amber-glazed corridors.  

1. **The Book-Throwing Room**  
   - Athun cracked open a door, only to be pelted by **flying books and candlesticks**.  
   - Aeli peered in, unimpressed. *"Someone’s protective. Or just really hates visitors."*  

2. **The Voice Behind the Locked Door**  
   - Marcus pressed his ear to another door—**Morganas** spoke directly into his mind.  
   > *"Free me, and I will grant you power."*  
   - Athun relayed the offer, skeptical. *"Trapped entities offering gifts? That’s never good."*  

3. **The Deathly Silent Chamber**  
   - Lars listened at a third door. **Nothing.**  
   - Aeli, ever curious, opened it anyway—revealing **four one-eyed horrors** staring blankly.  
   - The door shut *very* quickly.  

---

### **The Witches and the Battle of the Brooms**  

Their exploration led them to a trio of **hags**, hunched over a door, whispering *"Amber?"* like a plea. When disturbed, the hags turned, their green, wart-covered faces twisting in fury.  

- **Brooms animated**, sweeping aggressively at the party.  
- **Aeli** sliced one in half with her sun blade, grinning. *"Who’s sweeping now?"*  
- **Marcus**, wielding a **+2 greatsword** (finally!), cleaved through a hag with surprising efficiency.  
- **Lars**, ever the pragmatist, crushed the last witch’s skull with his mace.  

The whispers ceased. The temple fell silent.  

---

### **The Flaming Skull Ambush**  

Emboldened, the party ascended to the upper floor—only to walk straight into an ambush.  

- **Marcus** opened a door—**two flaming skulls** hovered inside, eyes glowing with emerald fire.  
- **Aeli** slammed the door shut. *"Nope. Not today."*  
- The skulls retaliated, blasting fire through the doorway. **Marcus barely dodged**, using a **Portent roll** to deflect a critical hit.  
- **Lars**, undeterred, charged in, **Hunter’s Mark** blazing. His mace shattered one skull, then the next.  

The last skull **fled**, phasing through a wall—leaving behind a **charred wizard’s corpse** clutching a **magical staff**.  

---

### **Secrets of the Model Room**  

In a side chamber, the party discovered:  
- A **12-foot-tall model of Castle Ravenloft**, eerily accurate.  
- A **wooden chest** containing a **tome** and **map case**—untouched, but promising answers.  

Aeli studied the model, frowning.  
> *"Someone was watching Strahd. Planning. But for what?"*  

---

### **Loose Ends and Lingering Threats**  

The temple was far from cleared.  
- **Sealed doors** still hid **Morganas, Yrgar, and Tarachamedes**—each offering gifts for freedom.  
- **Flaming skulls** lurked in the shadows, guarding something.  
- The **tome and map** might hold the command words needed to progress—or unleash something worse.  

As the party regrouped, Marcus sighed, rubbing his temples.  
> *"We need to find whatever we came here for. And then we need to leave. Before this place decides to keep us."*  

The Amber Temple had tested them. But the real trials—and the choices that could damn them—still lay ahead.  

---

### **Session Highlights & Memorable Lines**  
- **Athun**: *"I don’t want to die opening doors!"* (After the book-throwing incident.)  
- **Marcus**: *"Lars, you’re not shit! I respect you!"* (Post-skull annihilation.)  
- **Aeli**: *"This place is like a really bad Airbnb. No refunds, and the hosts want you dead."*  

**Next Steps:**  
- Decipher the **tome and map**.  
- Decide whether to **bargain with the trapped entities**.  
- Locate the **command words**—or risk becoming permanent residents of the Amber Temple.  

The whispers still echoed in their minds. The temple was listening. And it was hungry.
