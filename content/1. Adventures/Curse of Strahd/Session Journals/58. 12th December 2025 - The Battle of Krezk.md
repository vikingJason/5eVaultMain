## Summary - Draft

The session began with our heroes split. Lars, the stalwart ranger, stood alone before the towering gates of Krezk. His vigil was shattered by the thunderous approach of a gargantuan, walking tree—the Winter Splinter, a blight they had faced before on Yester Hill, now grown larger and more formidable. Its eyes glowed with a sinister green energy, and the earth shook with each step. With a defiant cry of “You shall not pass!” and a thrown torch, igniting the creature, Lars drew its wrath, triggering the battle.

From their vantage point, Marcus and Athun saw the colossal threat and Lars’s precarious position. They rushed to aid him, with Marcus ominously carrying his bucket of holy water. The battle was joined in earnest when Marcus, declaring “If we're going to piss it off, we're going to piss it off good,” unleashed a Fireball that engulfed the tree and set the very gates of Krezk ablaze. The creature recoiled, now wreathed in flames.

The Winter Splinter was not without strategy. It used its massive roots as whips, aiming to clear the walls. It struck Lars with tremendous force, hurling him from the ramparts and dealing additional fall damage. Athun, climbing the wall to dodge, was similarly swatted down. All the while, the blazing gate groaned under the creature’s relentless assault.

Lars, undaunted, scrambled up and hurled his greataxe into the beast’s side, marking it for the hunt. Marcus took to the skies with his cloak of flying, a bucket in hand, surveying the scene. In a moment of desperate experimentation, he poured the bucket of holy water onto the burning tree, hoping for a divine reaction. The result was merely the sizzling extinguishment of his own fiery handiwork. “It did not work the way I expected,” Marcus lamented.

The tree’s tactics evolved. It pinned Lars beneath a massive trunk-arm, then finally shattered the burning gates, breaching Krezk’s defences. Athun, rallying with his Giant’s Might, landed solid blows with his greatsword, but the creature’s retaliation was devastating. It slammed its roots into the earth, causing necrotic tendrils to erupt and snare the heroes. Athun was restrained and battered unconscious, falling to the ground.

At this dire moment, Aeli arrived, taking in the scene: Athun down, Lars pinned, Marcus grounded, and a burning, gargantuan tree blight standing victorious. She rushed to heal Athun, rousing him with a shout, and joined the fray with her Sun Sword.

The battle became a brutal war of attrition. Marcus, knocked from the sky by a volley of thorns, crashed to the earth. The tree unleashed a pulse of necrotic energy, frightening Aeli and Athun. Yet, the combined assault was wearing it down. Fireballs, axe blows, and radiant strikes from the Sun Sword chipped away at its form. Finally, with a mighty roar, Lars, still trapped beneath its limb, delivered the killing blows from within, causing the Winter Splinter to shudder and violently explode in a burst of splinters and dark energy.

Amid the wreckage, nestled in the roots, gleamed the source of its power: the green gemstone they had seen at Yester Hill, now securely woven into the creature’s body. “It learnt after the last time,” Aeli observed grimly.

Exhausted and wounded, the party retreated to the famed healing pool. The waters were refreshing, but held less potency than before. “That damn tree ruined everything,” Lars grumbled. Their respite was short-lived, for the complete silence of Krezk now seemed more ominous than ever.

Deciding to find shelter and investigate, they began searching houses. The first was a charnel house of carnage, furniture destroyed, blood and chunks of flesh splattered everywhere, with no bodies in sight. Investigation pointed clearly to werewolves, and specifically to smaller, child-sized paw prints. Athun deduced the savagery matched a werewolf’s strength, and the unlocked doors suggested the townsfolk had let something in.

A third house felt unnaturally warm, with smoke from the chimney. Inside, a seemingly normal scene was betrayed by a putrid, overwhelming stench that forced Marcus and Athun to retch. Lars traced the smell to a bedroom chest. Inside, he found a horrific “stew” of rotting human and werewolf parts. Disgusted, he piled furniture and set the house ablaze to purge the foulness.

Meanwhile, Aeli, following tracks, made the most disturbing find. In a cleared-out living room, a young werewolf was ritualistically displayed: splayed and nailed to the floor, gutted from neck to navel, with a perfect circle of salt drawn around it. Organs were missing. This was no frenzied kill; it was a deliberate, occult act. The timing matched the other attacks. “Someone’s been here before us,” Aeli reported, her voice thick with unease.



### Lore Revelations & Environmental Storytelling
*   The Gemstone’s Purpose: The green gemstone is confirmed as a power source for the druidic blights. The Winter Splinter had adapted to protect it better after Yesterhill, showing a malign intelligence behind these forces.
*   The Fate of Krezk: The town is a tomb. The werewolf children (three of an original six are still unaccounted for) appear to have been unleashed here, likely by the druids of Yester Hill or their allies. The townsfolk are almost certainly all dead, dismembered, and partially consumed.
*   A Deeper Evil: The ritualistic murder of the young werewolf introduces a new, chilling layer. This is not simple lycanthropic rage. Someone is harvesting werewolves and humans for an unknown purpose, employing magic (the salt circle) in a way that suggests a ritual to contain, purify, or transmute their essence. This ties directly to the darker, mystical arcs surrounding the druids and the fanes.
*   The Land’s Corruption: The healing pool’s diminished effect suggests the victory over the tree did not cleanse the land’s corruption; it may have been a symptom, not the cause.

### Loose Ends & Future Hooks
*   The Missing Children: Where are the remaining three werewolf children? Are they still in Krezk, or have they returned to the woods?
*   The Ritualist: Who performed the gutting ritual on the young werewolf, and for what purpose? This hints at a faction (a warlock, a hag, or a devoted cultist) with goals beyond the druids’ straightforward corruption.
*   The Gem’s Fate: The party now holds the second of the three stolen gemstones. Its magic is palpable, and its intended purpose for the druids’ rites remains a key campaign mystery.
*   Krezk’s Secrets: Is there truly no one left? What of the Abbey? The party’s search for a safe haven is now also an investigation into a mass murder and dark ritual.
*   The Vampire Angle: The briefly discussed theory that the vampires from the pond might have been harvesting werewolves adds another potential faction to the muddy moral landscape of Barovia.

The session closed on a note of grim victory and deepening horror. They had saved an empty town from a giant monster, only to find it was merely the aftermath of a far more intimate and sinister tragedy. The shadows in Krezk have secrets yet to tell.
