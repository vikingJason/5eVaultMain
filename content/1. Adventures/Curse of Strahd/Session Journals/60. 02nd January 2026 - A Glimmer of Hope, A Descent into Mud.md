## Summary
<p>
The morning sun rose over the winery like a defiance.
</p><p>
It should not have been there. Barovia did not allow such things. Yet the light came anyway, warm, steady, impossible, and it clung to the party as they stirred from rest. The gloom retreated, not from the land, but from them. Wherever they stood, the darkness gave ground.
</p><p>
Lars paused in the doorway, his hand resting on the frame. He stared out at the light in silence, a rare and unguarded look of pleasant surprise softening his features. Marcus leaned into the warmth without shame. Athun checked his gear with methodical calm. Aeli felt something lighter rise in her chest, hope, sharp and almost unfamiliar.
</p><p>
For a moment, Barovia blinked.
</p><p>
*“We’ve got a kingdom to save and a person to kill,”* Lars said at last.
</p><p>
It was not inspirational, but it was honest. The light did not argue.
</p><p>
The map guided them toward Berez.
</p><p>
They followed it south, and the journey passed with unsettling speed. The circle of sunlight travelled with them, sliding across dead fields and crooked trees, holding the fog at bay just long enough for their passage. The land watched, silent and resentful.
</p><p>
At the fork leading into the marshes, the light failed.
</p><p>
It did not fade gradually. It vanished, snuffed out the moment they crossed the threshold. Fog surged back in, thick and permanent, swallowing the road and the fragile optimism they had carried with them.
</p><p>
Berez waited.
</p><p>
The trail dissolved into sucking marshland beneath their boots.
</p><p>
What remained of Berez sagged into the mire, peasant cottages black with mildew, roofs collapsed, walls bowed and rotted through. Clouds of flies choked the air, their buzzing constant and hungry. The village had not been destroyed so much as *abandoned to decay*, left to sink inch by inch into the swamp.
</p><p>
To the east, something stood apart.
</p><p>
A hut, ramshackle, crooked, perched atop the enormous stump of a long-fallen tree. It loomed above the ruins, untouched by the slow drowning of the village. Iron cages flanked its doorway, each containing swarms of ravens. None of them moved. None of them made a sound.
</p><p>
As the others debated the meaning of the circles marked on their map, large and small, perhaps fanes, perhaps something worse, Lars grew restless. He began a careful, stealthy approach toward the hut.
</p><p>
Aeli remembered why they had come. [[The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind]]. Taken by [[Baba Lysaga]]. Servant of Strahd.
</p><p>
They followed.
</p><p>
Up close, the hut was worse.
</p><p>
Roots from the stump thrust upward like spider legs, warped and unnatural. Beneath the doorway floated an upside-down giant’s skull, suspended as though the swamp itself held it aloft. The ravens in their cages watched, silent and unmoving.
</p><p>
Lars hesitated, voicing mild concern about disturbing what might be a hermit.
</p><p>
“A polite word for him,” Aeli said.
</p><p>
Marcus studied the structure with clear interest, fire already dancing at his fingertips. “I have no problem with this,” he murmured.
</p><p>
Lars knocked.
</p><p>
There was no answer.
</p><p>
He pushed the door open.
</p><p>
Inside, the air reeked of blood and rot. A tub stained dark. Old furniture warped with age. In the center of the room stood a wooden crib, and within it lay an angelic child, untouched by the decay around it. From between the floorboards, green light seeped upward like a pulse.
</p><p>
The hut *lurched*.
</p><p>
Roots tore free from the mud. The entire structure rose, unfolding onto spidery legs with a grinding scream of wood and earth.
</p><p>
The house was alive.
</p><p>
Insects poured from the interior, swarming together until they formed a single shape. Baba Lysaga emerged from the buzzing mass, her body twisted, her eyes empty of anything but malice. She shrieked as she raised a clawed hand toward Lars.
</p><p>
“You’re here for the seed!” she screamed. “Kill them all!”
</p><p>
The hut obeyed.
</p><p>
Root-legs slammed down with crushing force. Athun, Marcus, and Lars were struck almost immediately, devastating blows that drove the breath from their lungs. Marcus staggered, dazed, struggling to keep his footing. Ireena could only watch in panic, frozen in place at Lars’s shouted command to stay back.
</p><p>
Chaos took hold.
</p><p>
Athun charged the nearest leg, turning doubt into violence. Steel rang against wood as he hacked again and again, committed fully to the simple truth that the hut had to fall.
</p><p>
Marcus grinned through the pain. “I’ll burn it to the ground.”
</p><p>
Fire bloomed as his *Fireball* tore into the structure, flames spreading hungrily across the dry wood.
</p><p>
Aeli and Lars climbed the shuddering hut itself, forcing their way toward Baba Lysaga. Sun blade and mace struck in tandem, their blows landing true against the hag’s twisted form.
</p><p>
She answered with poison.
</p><p>
A wave of her hand summoned a choking *Cloudkill*, thick and lethal. The fog swallowed the battlefield. Lars collapsed within it, coughing, then still.
</p><p>
Athun kept cutting.
</p><p>
Marcus saw the opening. Baba Lysaga’s focus was fixed on her spell. He raised his hand and released *Magic Missile*. The darts struck without mercy. Her concentration shattered. The poisonous cloud dissolved.
</p><p>
The fire did not. The hut burned as it fought.
</p><p>
Athun severed a leg at last, Baba Lysaga fell beneath a final barrage of blows, but the structure was already dying.
</p><p>
It collapsed into the marsh.
</p><p>
Flames surged outward as the magic unravelled. Those still atop the hut were caught in the blast. Lars and Athun were thrown clear, scorched and unconscious. Aeli leapt, landing hard but alive.
</p><p>
The swamp reclaimed the wreckage. Smoke drifted low over the water.
</p><p>
Aeli moved quickly, drawing on the blessed water from Krezk’s pool to revive Lars. A healing spell stabilized Athun. Nearby, Ireena stood rigid, magically paralyzed, her eyes wide and pleading. Lars lifted her without a word.
</p><p>
The remains of the hut still held danger. A chest, trapped with a glyph of warding, detonated when disturbed. Marcus fell unconscious in the blast.
</p><p>
Inside the chest, they found wealth:
1,300 gold pieces.
Five diamonds, each worth 500 gold.
An *Oil of Sharpness*.
+1 sling bullets.
Spell scrolls.
A set of pipes.
A polished bloodstone.
</p><p>
The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind was not among them.
</p><p>
But the Seed of the Swamp Fane was. Marcus and Athun found it underneath the rotting floorboards. Athun reached into gap, wrenching free the glowing green gemstone embedded within the stump, the Seed of the Swamp Fane.
</p><p>
With it, the truth stood clear. The fanes of Barovia had been corrupted. Bound. Mountain, Forest, Swamp. Strahd’s allies had done their work well.
</p><p>
Now the party held the means to undo it.
</p><p>
The land watched them again, silent and patient.
</p>

## Summary - Draft

The morning sun, a miraculous and steadfast companion, bathed the winery in its light as the party stirred. The oppressive gloom of Barovia had been pierced, if only in their immediate vicinity, by the divine blessing they carried. Lars stood in the doorway, simply staring, a rare, unguarded look of pleasant surprise on his features. Marcus basked in the warmth, Athun readied his gear, and Aeli felt a surge of chipper hope. The eternal night had been broken, if only for them. "We've got a kingdom to save and a person to kill," Lars declared, summarising their monumental task with characteristic bluntness.

Consulting their map, the party chose to investigate the marked location of Berez, a flooded village to the south. The journey was unnaturally swift, the circle of sunlight moving with them over the bleak landscape until they reached the fork leading into the marshes. As they crossed the threshold into Berez, the sunlight faded, swallowed once more by the thick, permanent fog that clung to this place. The hopeful mood evaporated.

Berez was a graveyard of a village. The trail dissolved into spongy, sucking marshland. Peasant cottages, black with mildew and rotted through, hunkered in the mire as if resigned to their fate. Clouds of hungry flies filled the stagnant air. To the east, a strange, ramshackle hut sat perched on the gigantic stump of a long-fallen tree, a stark contrast to the ruins around it. Flanking its doorway were iron cages filled with silent, watchful ravens.

While the others debated the purpose of the large and small circles on their map, theorizing they represented corrupted fanes of power, Lars grew impatient. He led a stealthy approach towards the anomalous hut. Aeli recalled their true purpose: to find the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, said to be taken by a woman named Baba Lysaga, a powerful and dark servant of Strahd.

Up close, the hut was even more sinister. The roots of the stump thrust up like spider legs, and an upside-down giant’s skull floated beneath the doorway. The ravens in their cages remained ominously quiet. Against Lars’s mild concern about disturbing a “poor hermit,” the party decided to investigate. “It’s a polite word for him,” Aeli quipped about Strahd, as Marcus eyed the flammable structure with palpable desire. “I have no problem with this,” he murmured, fire dancing at his fingertips.

Lars knocked. No answer. Pushing the door open, he saw a ghastly interior: bloody tub, old furniture, and in the center, a wooden crib holding an angelic child. Green light seeped from the floorboards. Before he could react, the entire hut *lurched*. The roots ripped free from the mud, and the structure rose on spidery legs, very much alive.

The battle was chaotic and brutal from the outset. As the hut animated, a swarm of insects inside coalesced into the form of [[Baba Lysaga]], a contorted hag whose eyes held nothing but pure evil. She immediately tried to shove Lars from the porch, shouting *“You’re here for the seed! Kill them all!”* to her monstrous home.

The walking house proved to be a terrifying opponent. Its giant root-legs whipped down with crushing force. Athun, Marcus, and Lars all suffered devastating blows in the first volley. Marcus was struck so hard he was left dazed, unable to react properly. Ireena could only watch in panic, following Lars’s order to stay back.

The party split their focus. Athun, embodying the phrase “when in doubt, hit the tree,” became a lumberjack, hacking relentlessly at the hut’s legs with his greatsword. Marcus, true to his impulses, grinned as he uttered, “I’ll burn it to the ground.” He engulfed a section of the hut in a roaring *Fireball*, which began to spread. Aeli and Lars climbed the shuddering structure to engage Baba Lysaga directly on her doorstep, their sun blade and maul striking true.

The hag proved a dangerous spellcaster. With a wave of her hand, she cast a *Cloudkill*, a thick, poisonous fog that enveloped the battlefield. Lars, caught in the cloud, choked and fell unconscious. As Athun continued his demolition, Marcus saw his chance. With Baba concentrating on her deadly fog, he cast *Magic Missile*. The unerring darts of force shattered her concentration, and the poisonous cloud dissipated.

The battle’s end was as dangerous as its beginning. The spreading fire from Marcus’s spells began to consume the hut. As Athun finally severed a leg and pulled the glowing green gemstone seed of the Swamp Fane from the stump, Baba Lysaga fell to a final barrage of attacks. With the hag dead, the mortally wounded hut collapsed into the marsh, but not before the expanding fireball engulfed those still on it. Lars and Athun were scorched into unconsciousness, while Aeli managed to leap to relative safety.

In the smouldering, waterlogged wreckage, the party recovered. Aeli used the blessed water from Krezk’s pool to revive Lars, and a healing spell stabilised Athun. They found Ireena magically paralyzed, unable to move, her eyes pleading. Lars carried her.

Searching the hut, they triggered a glyph of warding on a chest that blasted Marcus into unconsciousness. Within, they found a small fortune: 1,300 gp, five diamonds (500 gp each), an *Oil of Sharpness*, +1 sling bullets, spell scrolls, a set of pipes, and a polished bloodstone. Notably, the [[Holy Symbol of Ravenkind was not there.]]

The most critical find was the Seed of the Swamp Fane, now in their possession. This, coupled with the gem from Yester Hill, confirmed their theory: the fanes of Barovia, Mountain, Forest, and Swamp, had been corrupted and bound by Strahd’s allies. Restoring them was key to weakening the land’s dread lord.

### **Loose Ends & Future Hooks**

*   **The Primary Quest:** The **Holy Symbol of Ravenkind**, one of the artifacts to defeat Strahd, is still missing. Baba Lysaga did not have it. The search must continue elsewhere.
*   **Irina’s Condition:** The Burgomaster’s daughter remains magically paralyzed, a vulnerable burden and a constant reminder of the hag’s malice.
*   **The Restored Fane:** They possess the seed to reconsecrate the Swamp Fane, but the location of the actual shrine within the marshes is still unknown.
*   **The Third Fane:** The location of the final **Forest Fane** remains a mystery on their map.
*   **Strahd’s Wrath:** They have now slain one of his most powerful ancient servants. The vampire lord’s attention will be firmly upon them.

The sun still shone above them, a tiny island of light in the gloom, as they trudged back into the mire, their quest grown more complex and perilous than ever.
