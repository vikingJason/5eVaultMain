#### **Moonlit Club** (Uncommon)

- **Mechanics:** This +1 club deals an extra 1d4 radiant damage to undead. Once per day, as a bonus action, you can cause the club to emit dim moonlight in a 20-foot radius for 1 minute.
- **Description:** Made of pale, gnarled wood with faint carvings of wolves and stars, the club glows faintly under moonlight.
- **Lore:** Said to have been crafted from a sacred tree by the Keepers of the Feather, the club embodies the power of the moon to repel creatures of the night.

#### **Gravebreaker Mace** (Uncommon)

- **Mechanics:** A +1 mace that deals an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage to prone undead creatures.
- **Description:** This heavy weapon is studded with silver spikes and radiates a faint holy aura.
- **Lore:** Used by Barovian priests to destroy desecrated graves and the undead that crawled out of them.


#### Vampire’s Bane Bow (Uncommon)

- **Mechanics:** A +1 longbow that deals an additional 1d6 radiant damage to vampires and vampire spawn. Its string glows faintly in the dark.
- **Description:** The bow is made of yew wood, with silver inlays in the shape of a sunburst.
- **Lore:** A legendary weapon crafted by the Morninglord’s faithful, it was said to have slain a vampire lord centuries ago.


#### **Quiver of Blessed Arrows** (Rare)

- **Mechanics:** A quiver containing 10 silvered arrows. Each arrow deals an additional 1d8 radiant damage and is destroyed after hitting a target.
- **Description:** The arrows shimmer with a faint golden light.


#### **Hunter’s Gloves** (Rare)

- **Mechanics:** While wearing these gloves, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with ranged weapons and advantage on Survival checks to track undead.
- **Description:** Fingerless leather gloves that are faintly enchanted to smell like cloves.

#### ***Raven Cloak** (Uncommon)*

- ***Mechanics:** Grants resistance to necrotic damage. Once per day, you can cast Fly on yourself for 1 minutes.*
- ***Description:** A feathered cloak that seems to shift between shades of black and gray.*




#### **Map of Barovia (Uncommon)**

- **Mechanics:** A magical map that provides faint glowing markers for sanctuaries, danger zones, or locations of importance.
- **Description:** The map is hand-drawn and enchanted to adjust itself based on the user’s location.

#### **Witchhunter’s Hatchet (Uncommon)**

- **Mechanics:** A +1 handaxe that grants advantage on attack rolls against spellcasters or creatures concentrating on spells.
- **Description:** This hatchet has a rune-carved handle and a blade that hums faintly when magic is near.
- **Lore:** Used by a cult to slay witches, it was later stolen by Ezmerelda.


#### **Frostbane Longsword (Uncommon)**

- **Mechanics:** A +1 longsword deals an extra 1d4 cold damage. When struck, undead must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have their speed halved until the end of their next turn.
- **Description:** The blade is forged from a steel that resembles blue ice and perpetually cold to the touch.
- **Lore:** It is said to have been enchanted by a hermit who lived in the icy peaks of Barovia to defend against the undead.

### **3. Frostforged Hauberk** _(Uncommon)_

_Armor (chain shirt or chain mail)_

- This armor is crafted from enchanted ice that never melts. While wearing it, you have **resistance to cold damage**.
    
- If a creature within **5 feet** hits you with a melee attack, it takes **1d4 cold damage**.
    

### **4. Emberplate of the Phoenix** _(Uncommon, requires attunement)_

_Armor (scale mail or plate)_

- When you are reduced to **0 hit points**, a fiery pulse erupts from the armor, dealing **1d6 fire damage** to all creatures in a **10-foot radius**.
    
- This ability recharges **at dawn**.

### **5. Chameleon's Cloakmail** _(Uncommon, requires attunement)_

_Armor (any medium armor)_

- As an action, you can change the armor’s appearance to blend with your surroundings, granting you **advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks** for **1 hour**.
    
- This ability recharges after a **short or long rest**.
    




### **Uncommon Magic Armors**

1. **Gravekeeper’s Shroud (Studded Leather)**
    
    - You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.
        
    - Once per long rest, you can cast _detect undead_ as an action.
        
2. **Wraithbone Plate (Half Plate)**
    
    - Grants resistance to necrotic damage.
        
    - When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you gain temporary HP equal to your proficiency bonus.
        
3. ==**Fogbound Cloak (Cloak, Worn Over Any Armor)**==
    
    - Once per long rest, you can cast _misty step_ as a bonus action.
        
    - In dim light or darkness, creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to see you.
        
4. **Pale Knight’s Hauberk (Chain Mail)**
    
    - You do not suffer exhaustion from staying awake for 24 hours.
        
    - Undead that attempt to touch you feel an unnatural cold and must make a DC 13 Wisdom save or be unwilling to attack you until the end of their next turn.
        
5. **Bloodstained Brigandine (Breastplate)**
    
    - When you take slashing damage, you can use your reaction to deal 1d6 necrotic damage to the attacker.
        
    - You can spend a hit die to regain hit points as a bonus action once per long rest.
        
6. **Moonveil Vestments (Padded Armor)**
    
    - While wearing this armor, you leave no tracks and your footsteps make no sound.
        
    - Lycanthropes perceive you as one of their own unless you attack them.
        
7. **Dreadcarved Plate (Plate Armor)**
    
    - When you are reduced to 0 HP for the first time each day, you remain conscious until the end of your next turn before falling unconscious.
        
    - Your shadow moves unnaturally, flickering like a candle flame in dim light.
        
8. ==**Ghoulskin Jerkin (Hide Armor)**==
    
    - You have resistance to poison damage.
        
    - Once per long rest, you can touch a corpse and cast _speak with dead_ for up to one question.
        
9. ==**Specter’s Embrace (Ring Mail)**==
    
    - Attacks from incorporeal creatures (such as ghosts or wraiths) have disadvantage against you.
        
    - If you stand still for 1 minute, you can blend into the surroundings, gaining advantage on Stealth checks until you move.
        
10. ==**Ashen Guard (Scale Mail)**==
    
- Fire resistance.
    
- If reduced to 0 HP by fire damage, you explode into a cloud of smoke and ash, becoming stable but unconscious and leaving a smoldering silhouette behind.


1. ==**Veil of the Restless (Leather Armor)**==
    

- While wearing this armor, undead creatures cannot detect you by sight unless you attack or interact with them.
    
- Once per long rest, you can cast _disguise self_ to appear as a corpse or undead version of yourself.
    

12. **Ironbark Husk (Hide Armor)**
    

- Grants resistance to bludgeoning damage from nonmagical attacks.
    
- Once per long rest, you can use your reaction to turn your skin as hard as bark, granting +2 AC until the start of your next turn.
    

13. **Whispering Ward (Breastplate)**
    

- While wearing this armor, you hear distant whispers warning you of danger. You have advantage on Initiative rolls.
    
- If you roll a 1 on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, the whispers grow maddening, causing you to suffer disadvantage on your next roll.
    

14. **Sanguine Shell (Scale Mail)**
    

- When you deal melee damage, you regain hit points equal to half your proficiency bonus.
    
- If you are hit with a critical strike, the armor bleeds, causing you to take an additional 1d4 necrotic damage.
    

15. **Cursed Silvermail (Chain Shirt)**
    

- While wearing this armor, you have resistance to damage from shapechangers (such as werewolves).
    
- However, animals sense its cursed nature and react with hostility toward you.
    

16. **Eclipsed Aegis (Splint Mail)**
    

- While standing in dim light or darkness, you have resistance to radiant damage.
    
- When hit by a radiant attack, you glow with a ghostly aura for 1 minute, giving off dim light in a 10-foot radius.
    

17. **Hollowborn Carapace (Plate Armor)**
    

- When you roll a death saving throw, roll with advantage.
    
- If you are ever reduced to 0 HP by radiant damage, you fall unconscious but do not make death saving throws for 1 minute—either stabilizing or dying at the end of the duration.
    

18. **Shadowweave Robes (Worn as Armor, Counts as Padded Armor)**
    

- While in dim light or darkness, you can use a bonus action to teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.
    
- When standing in bright light, you have disadvantage on Stealth checks.
    

19. **Rimeforged Cuirass (Breastplate)**
    

- Grants resistance to cold damage.
    
- If you are reduced to 0 HP by cold damage, you are encased in ice instead of falling unconscious. While encased, you are stable and cannot take damage, but must be thawed or healed to return to normal.
    

20. **Coffinsteel Hauberk (Ring Mail)**
    

- When lying prone, you can appear as a corpse, requiring a DC 15 Investigation check to determine you are alive.
    
- Once per long rest, you can play dead for up to 10 minutes, remaining conscious but unmoving.
