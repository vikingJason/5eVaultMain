## 🧪 Potion of Healing

_Common | Vitality drawn from life, blood, and renewal_

### Core Components

- **Crimsonleaf or Bloodroot petals**  
    A hardy red-leafed plant that grows where blood has soaked into the soil (battlefields, gallows grounds).
- **Pure spring water**  
    Must be drawn at dawn; water taken after sunset weakens the potion.
- **Honey or sap**  
    Acts as a binding agent; Martikov wine-honey is especially prized.
- **A drop of fresh blood**  
    Voluntarily given during brewing (adds potency and intent).

## 🧪 Potion of Protection from Undead

_Uncommon | Warding and sanctification_

### Core Components

- **Powdered grave-silver or consecrated chalk**  
    Silver scraped from a burial token, wedding ring, or holy symbol.
- **Garlic oil**  
    Not culinary — distilled slowly until it becomes acrid and stinging.
- **Blessed incense ash**  
    Burned during a prayer or rite; Morninglord, Ezra, or Fane blessings all work differently.
- **Ash from an undead-creature’s remains**  
    Even a pinch from a burned zombie bone or wight fragment suffices.

### Barovian Flavor

- The potion smells like cold stone and smoke.
- While active, the drinker’s breath fogs unnaturally, even indoors.
- Undead feel _repelled_, not harmed — as if the drinker is briefly “out of phase” with death.

## 🧪 Potion of Lesser Restoration

_Uncommon | Purging corruption and imbalance_

### Core Components

- **Moonwort or Ghost Orchid**  
    Pale plants that grow where suffering lingered but hope remained (abbeys, old shrines).
- **Clear alcohol or distilled wine**  
    Acts as a solvent; Wizard of Wines vintages are ideal.
- **Salt blessed or washed in running water**  
    Used to draw out poison, disease, or affliction.
- **A focus of clarity**  
    One of the following:
    - A prayer spoken aloud during brewing
    - A handwritten confession dissolved in the liquid
    - A personal token willingly submerged (removed before drinking)

### Barovian Flavor

- Drinking the potion causes a brief wave of nausea or tears — the body _rejecting_ the affliction.
    - The potion works, but the memory of the pain lingers as a faint ache or dream.

## 🕯️ Optional Rule: Brewing in Barovia

If you want extra weight:

- Brewing **during daylight** improves success.
- Brewing near a **corrupted Fane** risks side effects.
- Brewing with **Strahd-aware components** (blood taken unwillingly, tainted water) may create _twisted_ variants.
