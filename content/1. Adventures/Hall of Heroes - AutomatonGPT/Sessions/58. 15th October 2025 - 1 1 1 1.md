**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 


Cedrick took Lullaby back to the Royal Kitchen to meet again with his mother, Lyra. Cedrick tried to sway his mother to help Lullaby with improving their skills in the kitchen.

Lyra, before agreeing, asked for a demonstration of Lullaby's skills.

Lullaby quickly got to work making their signature fruit juice. Their methods were quite unusual and impressive, before long the bustling kitchen was silent as everyone watched in awe.

Lyra, impressed, agreed to let Lullaby train, pairing them with Motorola, a distant cousin of Galax.

Cedrick went home and got busy with cleaning his workshop and performing maintenance on his steel defender, Short Stack.

Rinji and Achroteth went to the Royal Library, seeking knowledge that had been out of their reach for far too long.

The door to this trove of knowledge hid the grand scale of the impressive repository. The library was cylindrical, with a column in the middle, surrounded by a platform that would raise and lower to access the seven stories of bookshelves that were jam packed with books from floor to ceiling.

Celly greeted the brothers, assisting them locating the books for the information they sought.

Rinji scurried off, eager to learn more about the rune branded into his scales.

He spent the day reading, uncovering that the rune bore a striking resemblance to 'The Dragon's Eye',  an ancient constellation that was part of a cluster of constellations. These constellations were mystical in nature, their position in the night sky never moved, for generations, until one day when they were no longer visible to the naked eye.

These constellations were key to finding the Shimmering Cave.

As Rinji combed through this ancient tome, pages began to crumble in his fingers. Hurridly he kept pouring through the pages. He saw the mention of an ancient star chart that could help him locate The Dragon's Eye. The last known location of the star chart was in a Tyrellion observatory.

Before he could learn the location of the observatory, the page completely crumbled to dust. Not letting this hurdle defeat him he continued, but as he turned each page, the old paper disintegrated. Inscribed into the back of the book was an image of the author, Vraxxis, and a print date of over 1000 years ago.

Immediately the kobold turned to the troublesome elder that appeared only to him, "Why did you not tell me about my rune?"

"You did not ask," Vraxxis responded.

Rinji asked if he knew the location of observatory, which Vraxxis confirmed he did not. Rinji asked if he knew anything about the Guardians of the Shimmering Cave.

Vraxxis explained that all Fey know of  the Guardians, that whenever a Guardian fell another was called upon to take their place, and that it was an honor to be called to bear the burden. He went on the explain that he did not have the pleasure of ever meeting any of the guardians, until he met Rinji.

Filled me answers and even more questions, Rinji grew tired from the day of reading and returned to his quarters for the evening.

Achroteth moved determination to learn anything he could about [[Aurofann]] and [[Zagra’gatra’lox]], powerful Dragon's, that  had previouslly been revealed to him, he shared a direct lineage with.

Aurofann the Radiant, was a Crystal Dragon, known as The Herald of the Dawnlight. Aurofann was a descendant of [[Serakar]], and [[Zagra’gatra’lox]].

When the world fractured after the [[First War of the Gears]], [[Aurofann]] was said to have crafted a network of [[Heartstones]], radiant crystals placed within the roots of the world.

These Heartstones sing to one another beneath the soil, to stabilize its magic veins, anchoring magic and nature together.

But one network of seeds never sprouted to the surface.

This is what gave birth to a civilization deep below the surface, the [[Tyrelion]], who mastered biotechnomancy: the fusion of life and machine

Zagra’gatra’lox, The Skull Keeper was a Black Dragon, was decendant of [[Umbrazhul]], and ancestor of Aurofann. Zagra’gatra’lox was known for living a long life, spanning longer than any dragon before or since her.

She was known for her jealousy of Aurofann, that turned into a cruelty with severe consequences.

The Tyrellion stood tall and proud in their defiance of Zagra, with Aurofann as their patron. But Zagra unleashed her limitless fury, and it was said that Zagra was destroyed along with Tyrellion itself.

This sent Aurofann into despair, and it was rumoured that he went into self imposed solitary exile to live out the rest of their life.

Achroteth continued his research, focussing on the dragons mentioned in the lineage, Serakar and Umbrazhul 


****The Legend of the Crystal Dragon and the Black Dragon God***

Long ago, before Calindor became the demiplane it is today, a cosmic battle shook the very fabric of existence. The legend speaks of two powerful dragon gods: **Serakar, the Crystal Dragon God of Light and Harmony**, and **Umbrazhul, the Black Dragon God of Shadows and Destruction**. Their conflict would shape the fate of the realm, leading to the creation of the **Shimmering Cave** and the crystal veins that now bind Calindor’s reality.

Serakar and Umbrazhul were ancient rivals, embodiments of opposing forces. Serakar’s crystalline scales radiated pure, radiant energy, illuminating the cosmos with harmony and balance. Umbrazhul, on the other hand, was a being of endless shadow, a god who sought to consume and corrupt all creation, leaving a world of desolation and chaos.

The two gods clashed in an epic battle that spanned realms, their powers unleashing storms of magic that tore through dimensions and threatened the cosmos. The black dragon’s destructive powers began to warp reality itself, pulling parts of the Feywild and the Material Plane into a chaotic, twisted state. Knowing that Umbrazhul’s power would eventually destroy all things, Serakar realized there was only one way to save the world.

In a final, desperate move, Serakar drew upon the deepest reserves of his magic, channeling the essence of light and harmony. With a roar that shook the stars, the Crystal Dragon unleashed a spell so powerful that it crystallized reality itself. In one devastating act of self-sacrifice, Serakar merged his essence with Umbrazhul’s, creating a prison of eternal crystal that bound both gods together within a hidden cavern, the Shimmering Cave.

This **Crystal Prison** was intended to contain Umbrazhul’s destructive power for all eternity. However, the Black Dragon God’s strength was so great that the prison could not entirely hold him. Instead, it merely slowed him, like a dam that held back an unstoppable flood. From the prison, Umbrazhul’s dark power seeped slowly into the world, his shadowy essence twisting and mingling with Serakar’s magic. This blend of energies created the **crystal veins** that now spread across Calindor, forming a web of potent, volatile magic.

Over the ages, the **Guardians of the Crystal Veins** have understood their sacred purpose: to protect the crystal veins and prevent any force from tampering with the Shimmering Cave. Should the crystal prison ever be shattered, Umbrazhul would break free, unleashing his rage and darkness upon the world. Serakar’s power still lingers within the veins, acting as a protective force, but it requires the Guardians to keep the prison whole and stable.

Guardians across generations have passed down this duty, understanding that each vein represents a delicate balance of Serakar’s light and Umbrazhul’s darkness. Disturbing these veins risks awakening the Black Dragon God’s full power, while carefully tending to them keeps his destructive influence contained.

Found in The Codex Aurorium, written by an oracle of Serakar:

“When the Scales of Blood are born anew,
one half shall shimmer, the other decay.
Should the Balance falter,
the world will know the ruin of twin dawns.

It is said that one destined Guardian, the “True Champion,” will have the power to finally release Serakar’s essence from the crystal prison, cleansing the veins of Umbrazhul’s corruption.

Cedrick went to visit his mentor, Dorgle, at the local chapter of the Cogsmith Guild.

The Dwarf's office we a mess, open tomes, schematics, and journal entries were all scattered about haphazardly. Cedrick carefully stepped through the chaos over to Dorgle, whose attention was focused on an innocuous gear he held in his hand.

Suspicious that this Dorgle may be a Changeling, Cedrick asked for proof while producing a small knife. Dorgle run the blade over his palm, producing blood that satisfied Cedrick's curiosity. A drop of blood fell onto the gear, filling the intricate pattern etched into the gear. The pattern was unlike any other. Dorgle muttered something, Cedrick caught it, "Tyrelion".

This was not a subject that Cedrick was familiar with. After asking Dorgle, it appeared that his knowledge in this area was almost just a vague. He handed the gear to Cedrick , hoping that he might uncover more about the creators of such a finely made piece of technology.

Cedrick agreed, asking for information in return. The components that he needed to rebuild his father's legacy. Cedrick had uncovered the first puzzle but needed assistance.

His old mentor confirmed that Shadow Essence can only be obtained by either confronting a shadow creature or journeying to a place suffused with pure darkness. He offered the location of a crypt not far from the Capital that was rumoured to be haunted, but warned that this would test his resolve and combat abilities, as shadow creatures are elusive and dangerous.

Rinji focus was drawn to fulfilling more of his destiny. He searched high and low for any information, or even rumours, of a Polar bear sighting.

His mission took him through half of the taverns in Glitterfall before he came to a humble establishment with a single patron. A satyr with a grin like a knife-edge and a coat woven from dandelion fluff and mirror shards was regalling the empty common room with grandiose stories of incredible feats of his comrades.

When he noticed Rinji, he introduced himself as Tazielle, the ringleader of The Frostpetal Circus.

Rinji, hopeful, asked about the attractions the circus held. Tazielle confirmed that his menagerie was complimented with the most exquisite of Polar bears. Igrath was a Polar bear that stood larger than any other and whose fur shimmers brilliantly.

Tazielle agreed to take Rinji back to ask permission to wrestle Igrath, on the condition that Rinji perform in his show as part of the juggling act, where he would be juggled rather than the juggler.

Rinji agreed, and they made their way to the circus grounds, a few hours walk outside the city.

Rinji was escorted through the assorted of wagons and animal cages to Tazielle's caravan. The ring master pulled down a bottle of mead, the green colour catching the attention of the Kobold. The taste was sweet with nostalgia, instantly filling him with memories of his clan, growing up in the cave and partaking of the special sweet moss reserved for special occasions.

Tazielle offered that the mead was produced by the Moss Mead Guild, Rinji knew he had to seek them out. But that would have to wait. Rinji wanted to wrestle the bear.

Rinjin was pointed in the direction of a large enclosure and simple looking wagon. As Rinji approached he saw his target.

Igrath stood taller than any bear he had ever encountered. He studied the enclosure and saw evidence of the bears strength, metal bars that looked as though they were bent with ease, broken kegs with sand spilling onto the ground from being thrown around.

Igrath's fur shimmered as if made of tiny shards of mirrored glass.

As Rinji stared he was approached by a young goliath woman named Freyra Stoneshade. She showed genuine concern when Rinji requested to wrestle Igrath, but the pair retreated to Freya's wagon to discuss.
