## Key Events

**Lumday, 9th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 
<br><br>
Cedrick moved behind the bar in a blur of motion, his hands a well-practiced dance of bottles and glasses, pouring, cleaning, stacking. Dorgle, meanwhile, was less graceful. He slid into a seat beside Butch, Cassidee, and Martha, offering the universal peace offering of good company—buying the next round.
<br><br>
The reception was cool at first, the way one acknowledges a stranger who might have something interesting to say but probably doesn’t. But when Dorgle slapped a handful of gold on the bar and gestured for a fresh bottle—at full price—their gazes sharpened, their smiles widened.
<br><br>
Then the Fey Magic struck.
<br><br>
The air around Dorgle crackled with unseen forces, the way a storm feels before the first thunderclap. A pulse of invisible power slammed into him, sending him flying backward off his stool. He landed hard, blinking up at the ceiling as the room collectively paused to consider what had just happened. The regulars knew better than to tangle with Wild Magic. A few patrons pushed back their chairs, tossing coins on the counter as they hurried out the door.
<br><br>
That was the scene Achroteth and Lullaby walked into.
<br><br>
Martha lay slumped at the bar, passed out mid-drink. Cedrick poured another round, his expression distant, clearly contemplating some grand revelation about dishwashing efficiency.
<br><br>
Achroteth sighed. He had a gift for exasperated sighs, the kind that carried the weight of a hundred quiet frustrations. Without hesitation, he scooped up Martha in his arms and carried her upstairs, his steps steady and sure. He laid her down on a vacant bed, adjusting her position with the quiet care of a man who had carried many burdens in his time.
<br><br>
Back downstairs, he surveyed the room with the cold, assessing look of a scholar evaluating a particularly troublesome experiment. Then, with a curt nod, he set things into motion.
<br><br>
Lullaby sprinted into the kitchen and emerged moments later, brandishing a pot and pan like weapons of war. She beat them together in a rhythmic, merciless percussion that reverberated through the common room. The message was clear: time to go.
<br><br>
Butch and Cassidee, predictably, protested.
<br><br>
Dorgle, emboldened by the chaos, grabbed a drink of Firebreath and downed it in one go. The transformation was immediate—the illusion peeled away, revealing his true Dwarven form beneath.
<br><br>
Realizing that further mischief might push their luck, Butch and Cassidee took their revelry elsewhere, heading for The Fermentery. Dorgle and Cedrick exchanged a glance, the unspoken language of men looking for trouble and finding opportunity in equal measure. With grins and a shared sense of reckless abandon, they departed for The Fight Club.
<br><br>
Meanwhile, the bar was left in quieter hands. Lullaby gathered scattered mugs and upturned chairs while Achroteth sat down with his tome, his eyes tracing the unfamiliar weave of new spells, dissecting them with a scholar’s focus.
<br><br>
In the kitchen, something entirely different began to unfold.
<br><br>
Lullaby and Galax, two artists of vastly different mediums, found common ground over fruit and pulp. Galax watched, arms folded, as Lullaby demonstrated a method far more refined than the current technique of smashing fruit between heavy boards riddled with holes. The simplicity of it was almost insulting.
<br><br>
By the time they finished, Galax had been thoroughly converted. As thanks, he offered Lullaby an apprenticeship of sorts—a crash course in the basics of cooking. Lullaby, covered in juice, grinned at the offer. The two became so engrossed in their discussion of flavors and techniques that the rest of the world fell away, forgotten in the haze of citrus and spice.
<br><br>
Outside, the city churned with its usual brand of madness. But in the kitchen, for a little while at least, things made sense.
<br><br>
---
<br><br>
The Fight Club had changed.
<br><br>
Dorgle and Cedrick stepped through the entrance, expecting the familiar, surly Bugbear to greet them. Instead, an Automaton stood guard—cold metal where there should have been fur, glowing eyes where there should have been a glare. More of them moved about the club, performing menial tasks with mechanical efficiency. Each one wore a small red cap, a detail that sent an uneasy ripple through the atmosphere.
<br><br>
But the arena was alive, buzzing with the energy of combat and the feverish roar of the crowd. All eyes were locked on the pit below, where a fight was reaching its brutal conclusion.
<br><br>
Bax, wiry and desperate, squared off against Securee—the very same Bugbear who had once manned the door. There was no contest. Bax staggered, barely managing to keep his footing before Securee delivered a crushing blow. The crowd erupted. Blood sprayed across the sand.
<br><br>
Dorgle and Cedrick exchanged a look, an understanding passing between them without words. This was their kind of night. Without hesitation, they signed up for ranked matches.
<br><br>
Dorgle went first. His opponent was Sorrow, a Tiefling who fought with a dancer’s grace, twin daggers flashing like slivers of moonlight. She was fast, deadly. Dangerous. But Dorgle was a wall of iron. He took the blows that landed, gritting his teeth through the pain. Then he struck back, hard.
<br><br>
The fight was brief. Sorrow faltered, Dorgle pressed forward, and in the end, the Dwarf stood victorious.
<br><br>
Cedrick, watching from the side, let his confidence ride the momentum. With a smirk, he placed a bet on himself before even knowing who he would face. It didn’t matter.
<br><br>
Then he stepped into the ring and saw Bax standing across from him. A slow grin spread across Cedrick’s face. This would be over quickly.
<br><br>
The crowd barely had time to register the start of the fight before it was done. Bax barely moved. Cedrick struck once, twice—precise, devastating. A flawless victory.
<br><br>
The cheers came, but Cedrick barely heard them.
<br><br>



## Summary


While [[Cedrick]] was busy running around and taking care of the bar, [[Dorgle]] joined Butch, Cassidee and Martha at the end of the bar.

His attempt to engage them was met with an indifferent welcome, but that quickly changed when he offered to buy the next bottle, at full price. The Wild Magic surged, slapping [[Dorgle]] with a force of Fey magic, pushing him from the stool he sat upon.

These antics caused a stir with some of the patrons who quickly left as [[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Achroteth/Achroteth]] and [[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Lullaby/Lullaby]] arrived. The sight before them was almost unbelievable. Martha passed out at the bar, Cedrick pouring drinks and contemplating different ways to make cleaning the cups easier.

[[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Achroteth/Achroteth]] took charge of the situation, he carried Martha upstairs and gently laid her down in one of the empty rooms so that she could rest.

As he came back downstairs his eyes scanned the room and gave a silent but determined nod. [[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Lullaby/Lullaby]] dashed into the kitchen and grabbed a pot and pan, taking it out into the common room and began to beat on it like a drum to announce to the few remaining patrons that it was time to leave.

The protests came from [[Butch and Cassidee]], who were still enjoying their libations. [[Dorgle]] egged them on, downing a drink of [Firebreath] that caused his appearance to change back to his regular Dwarven self.

Before they could do more damage, [[Butch and Cassidee]] left and headed to the [[Brewery - The Fermentery|The Fermentery]]. Cedrick and [[Dorgle]], looking for some action and enteratinment, left [[The Troll's Tollbooth]] and headed to [[The Fight Club]]. [[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Lullaby/Lullaby]] helped with cleaning up the bar while [[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Achroteth/Achroteth]] sat and read over his tome, studying the new spells he had learned.

After their hard work, [[content/1. Adventures/Campaigns/Hall of Heroes/01. Adventures/Lullaby/Lullaby]] and [[Galax]] bonded over making juice. They should the chef different methods for extracting the sweet nectar and making delicious concoctions, a welcome reprieve from the current method of trying to smash the fruit between large boards with holes drilled into them. As thanks, Galax offered to teach Lullaby some of the basics with cooking, the pair oblivious to anything else going on outside of the kitchen.

---

At [[The Fight Club]] [[Dorgle]] and [[Cedrick]] noticed some changes they weren't prepared for. Instead of the burly Bugbear to greet them at the door, it was an Automaton that stood guard. More automatons were spotted running around and the club, performing  tasks and wearing small red caps.

The arena was bustling, the fight that was underway had drawn the attention of the spectators. [[Bax]] was fighting, or trying to at least, the Bugbear that had previously stood guard. [[Bax]] was no match, the crowd cheered for [[Securee]] as he struck his final blow.

This spurred on Dorgle and Cedrick, who signed up to participate in ranked matches. [[Dorgle]] was up first, matched against Sorrow, a Tiefling who moved with deadly grace and wielded dual daggers. The fight was tough but brief, the Dwarf besting his opponent.

During his preparation, [[Cedrick]]'s confidence drove him to make a bet on himself against his still unknown opponent. When he stepped into the ring, he gave a subtle smile to see that he was going to engage [[Bax]]. The combat was over in mere seconds, with [[Cedrick]] securing a flawless win against [[Bax]].
