## Summary

**Waterday, 11th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 

Dread lingered in the air like a bad smell.

Finroth had consolidated even more power, through magical manipulation and foul play.

Dorgle had a plan that he kept from the others, hiding it behind wanting to partake in the days festivities. 

Lullaby and Rinji left together, hoping to find out more information about the cooking competition.

The others headed off to speak with [[Lilareth]]. Not wanting to raise suspicions they decided to call on her at her residence. 

As they went their separate ways they were greeted by a town alive with excitement. People passed by offering smiles and hearty hellos. The sounds of laughter and joy echoed throughout town. The smells of freshly baked goods filled each breath and was so strong they could taste it.

---


Dorgle passed stalls where vendors had set up tables to proffer their wares. One table caught their eye. An Automaton manned a booth that sold costumes, for children and adults alike, of great quality. The booth was also giving away masks which Dorgle could not resist.

---

Rinji and Lullaby stopped by the main stage where a Sprite Duo named Willow and Wisp where performing in front of the crowd that had gathered.

The mischievous sprites perform dazzling aerial acrobatics using enchanted ribbons, creating intricate light trails in the air.

Rinji helped himself to some popcorn while they stopped to watch the show. Vraxxis was there, unseen to all but Rinji. Their banter raised concerns with Lullaby who could not understand if Rinji speaking to himself was a sign he was going crazy or was just an odd Kobold behaviour they were unfamiliar with.

The wisps began to prepare for their final act, the crowd enjoying the show. Except for one person. They were wearing a mask, standing alone in the crowd. They booed, trying to interrupt the performers concentration.

The crowd silently stood by, unsure what to do. The performers continued like true entertiners do. The stage was set for the last feat, their most daring of all. As they attempted it time felt like it slowed to a crawl as the detractor in the crowd threw their bag of popcorn at the stage.

Willow and Wisp crashed to floor, picked themselves up and took their exit.

The crowd parted as Automatons security arrested the masked hoodlum.

Rinji and Lullaby moved along to the next attraction.

---

As the Scribe answered the door to greet her guests, it was clear to Cedrick, Fawn, and Achroteth, that she was under the same spell as Althea. Achroteth cast Dispel Magic on her, immediately releasing Lilareth from the magic grip that had compelled her.

Achroteth explained the magic of the land to Lilareth and they discussed the situation with the dissolution of the council.

---

Dorgle wasted no time seeking out his target. Even with the fanfare of the festival in full swing this morning, it did not take him long to find Finroth. He was standing in the main square, stopping to shake hands and exchange pleasantries with the villagers that had gathered.

The mayor was escorted by Automaton guard-bots. Even though they were crafted to look less threatening, Dorgle knew that these were precision made and deadly.

Dorgle threw caution to the wind, approaching the Finroth with a plan. He wanted to be appointed as Sheriff. Even though it was just last night that he was informed that the position of Sheriff was appointed by the Capital, he knew that this would get Finroth’s attention.

But something about the mayor’s demeanour was different. The confidence overlapped by an air of arrogance now. Dorgle calculated the outcomes of his actions, his approach was silent, or so he thought.

Finroth somehow knew what Dorgle was planning, and turned to greet him. Dorgle offered his service as Sheriff. Finroth scoffed, taunting Dorgle, "Why would I need someone to be Sheriff? Why would I need you?"

Never one to back down, Dorgle lunged but was immediately grappled by Automatons.

The townsfolk screamed and began to scatter as the Automatons began to drag Dorgle away.

Lullaby and Rinji arrived at the main square, moving quickly to aid Dorgle. Seeing the situation as dire, they followed closely, not letting the Dwarf out of their sight.

The other party members headed back to the festival, spotting Rinji and Lullaby chasing after Dorgle being dragged away by automatons.

Trying something to help, Achroteth reached out from a distance and tried to Dispel Magic on Finroth's necklace. Fawn intervened, her commanding presence and words of authority de-escalating the situation.

She demanded answers, an explanation, and a stop to the hostilities. Finroth smiled and nodded, not in compliance, it was calculated.

Dorgle fought off the automatons as they power down and released him.

Finroth took control of the conversation, he smirked as he explained that it was Dorgle who attacked him, in front of many witnesses.

He agreed to discuss this further with Fawn back at the council chambers privately. Fawn agrees, and they all make their way to Town Hall.

Achroteth suggested that Fawn not go alone, that Rinji and he should join the princess while invisible.
