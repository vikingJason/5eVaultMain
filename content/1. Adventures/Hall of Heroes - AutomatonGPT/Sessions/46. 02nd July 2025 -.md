## Summary

**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 

The others arrive and Dorgle attacks Finroth. I call out “Finroth you tyrant, this is not natural. Let's settle this one on one.”

He keeps walking away and we notice the people in masks are now moving with purpose towards people who aren't wearing masks.

“You will not be able to goad me. Nature causes suffering, I will bring peace.”

Rinji talks to Baxbot and it seems to free him from the mind control. The other automatons just pummel Bax, completely destroying him. Rinji runs in and grabs the core, I try to stop him but he grabs it anyway. I grab Baxbot’s head, arm, leg and a few other small bits.

Grabbing the body of Bax, I Wild Shape into a Giant Horned Ram (black fur with white spots and large steel spiral horns), Lullaby and Rinji climb on and we make our way through the streets. They decide that we should go and save the others at the Troll’s Tollbooth and I change direction.

As we arrive, the area is eerily quiet and the door is unlocked. As I am waiting outside, Gallax comes charging out and almost runs into me. I am not able to convince him to stay and he runs off. I then try to hide around the corner and see Dorgle carrying Althea and then Fawn come running in.

Fawn walks outside and heals Althea. Everyone leaves the inn and we follow the Quicklings through town. We begin to hear cheering and laughter intermittently then we hear the heavy footsteps of automatons.

We get out of town and as we leave Dorgle tells us about the voice that was telling him to do things.

We made our way towards the Quicklings den.
