	- they use G.L.U.E. to re-attach the leaf. (Gleaming Luminescent Unification Elixir)

- if the party are unfriendly, they Light Rangers remain polite and answer any basic questions:
	- Meridian Prime is home to many ancient Fey creatures, considered extinct or almost extinct. These Fey are more akin to beasts then they are the current Fey.
	- [[Keeper Nessa]] is the head Warden, she makes sure that all creatures are properly cared for.
	- [[Magistrate Corvin]] is the person in charge of Meridian Prime.

	- if asked, they reveal:
		- a few days ago something weird happened, the ancient Fey all seemed to experience a shared nightmare
		- after the nightmare some of the creature changed, became more aggressive.
	- if pushed further:
		- the creatures have started attacking people.
		- thankfully no one has yet been killed.
	- if asked about the observatory, they heard something about an underground facility but don't know it's location.
		- [[Archivist Moss]] knows more about it, he should be at the outpost.
