## Key Events

**Lumday, 9th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 


## Summary

The carriages arrive. Finroth and the council arrive to greet the delegation. As the automatons start to unpack the chairs and tables, the delegation seems unsure.

Rinji is performing for the crowd with his new drum. I am enjoying a Lullatail and just relaxing.

Fawn and an unknown female elf arrived. Fawn introduces her as Celly, and Lullaby goes on a rant about her being Fawn’s wife or fiance or person to marriage.

Fawn and Celly head upstairs (Celly mentions the kiss that she gave up to the Quicklings), they spend the night together.

The next morning I look out the window and cast Skywrite “Petalwood Unity Gala”, I come downstairs and ask Cedric to look at the images I found in Finroth’s “spellbook”.

The spells seem to be of Mass Suggestion and Mass Charm Person.

Fast forward to the opening of the gala, we follow the group, and as the town council and Fawn head up onstage, we wait off stage with the others from the capital and listen to the speeches.

As the applause begins to slow, a voice calls out drunkenly “Boo!” and Finroth calls the automatons to grab him.
