## Summary

**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 


Achroteth spent the next day researching the Champion of Shimmering Cave. It seems to not have been classified yet and is kept on level 7 with the other yet to be classified books.

  

I take notes and make a full summary of the book.

Protecting the crystal veins that lie deep beneath Calindor are potectors known as **Guardians**. Historically, the Guardian has been any creature drawn to the crystal's magic by fate, duty, or circumstance.

However the tail-end of the timelines indicate that the Guardian has purely been of Fey origin.

The are tales of past Guardians passed down through generations of the various Fey groups each one shrouded in myth and casting doubt on the validity of their stories but there are three that share commonalities across the collected stories.

- **The Fireborne Keeper**: A Guardian who wielded flames directly from the veins, sacrificing their life to contain a fire that threatened all of Calindor.
- **The Spirit Shepherd**: This Guardian was known for guiding spirits near the veins and supposedly merged with the crystals, becoming an ethereal guide for future Guardians.
- **The Shadow Sentinel**: A mysterious Guardian who could manipulate shadows and stabilize disruptions in the veins. Their legacy warns of betrayal, as they were reportedly abandoned by allies who sought the cave’s power for themselves.

##### **Guardian’s Destiny and Prophecy**

One of Calindor’s greatest Fey guarded prophecies foretells the rise of a Guardian bound by fate, known as the True Champion, with the power to purify the veins, not merely balancing Umbrazhul and Serakar, but choosing which essence will endure.

However, this destiny is uncertain, as some fear that “unweaving” the crystals might lead to the realm’s unraveling. 

Some say the purification would release Serakar’s light, severing Umbrazhul’s influence forever.
Others whisper it could shatter the divine symmetry, ending both dragons’ dominion, and perhaps Calindor itself.

The Champion may ultimately face the choice of sacrificing their freedom to protect the crystals or risking all of Calindor in a final, transformative act.

The illustrations of the True Champion in the book all bear a striking resemblance to Rinji.

The True Champion must undergo labors and trials to prove their worthiness, with their power over the crystals growing as they progress.

#### **Factions and Conflicting Forces**

Several factions seek control of the crystal veins and the Shimmering Cave, each with their own goals and beliefs.

1. Groups of rebellious Fey creatures believes that controlling the Shimmering Cave will free them from the Eladrin rulers of Calindor. They see the crystals as a key to overthrowing oppressive rulers and returning Calindor to its Fey origins.
2. Radical sects that worships the crystal veins as sacred and seeks to have the Champion “unweave” them, transforming Calindor into a godlike realm, even if it means the destruction of mortal life.
3. Eladrin that view the crystals as vital to maintaining order. They support the prophecy of the Guardian but fear the prophecy’s fulfillment, as some believe the “True Champion” will disrupt the crystal system entirely, possibly destroying Calindor.
