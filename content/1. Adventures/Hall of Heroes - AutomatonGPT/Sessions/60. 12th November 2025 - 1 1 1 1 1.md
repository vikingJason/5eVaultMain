## Summary

**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 



Rinji returned to dinner.

Informed his friends that he put on a great show for the crowds at the circus.

In actual truth, Orlac the Magnificent was so nervous that his hands were sweating and he could not maintain proper grip, throwing Rinji into the crowd or dropping him completely.

The crowd loved the show regardless.

Rinji tried to press his luck and negotiate better compensation but Tazielle declined and relieved him of his obligation.

  

At dinner they disucssed plans for the next day.

Invitations to a "secret" coronation were extended and recinded repeatedly by Skwik.

  

Lullaby came back to his room to find a package on his bed.

Concerned he sought help from Acroteth.

Achroteth stopped an attendant who could not provide any clarity on the origin of the package, suggesting they seek out Ness who always knows what is going on in the castle.

  

Ness shared that the package was from Mo'toe'role.

Inside was a chefs jacket, and a set of chef's knives with different fruit etched into the blades.

The next morning they awoke and headed down into the sewers. The manhole cover were automatically opened by a swarm of rats.

  

Inside the dark, dank sewer, Skwik led the group to a large ancient confluence deep beneath the capital where every sewer tunnel meets. The space was cavernous, for a rat, a cathedral of rot and running water — lit by hundreds of guttering candles stuck into rusted pipes and floating on bits of flotsam.

  

A throne of twisted scrap metal sat upon an open sluice.

  

On the walls were 6 banners, each with a spoon emblazoned on a backdrop of different colours.

Green: Verminciani

Blue: 

Red:

Yellow: 

Orange:

The banner behind the throne was a concert of all 5 colours.

  

The 5 heads of the families each produced a spoon, with bites taken out, turning them into keys.

They used the keys to close sluice gates, preventing any further water flowing into the chamber. The central pool began to drain, revealing a large collection of copper pieces and other trinkets.

  

Pebbles was escorted to the throne.

  

Skwik produced the crown he had aquired for the occasion.

  

Verminciani, a stickler for tradition, held out his hands for the crown.

Gnawbone produced a sceptre.

Chewfang produced a long regal coat.

Slinktail anointed Queen Pebbles with sewer water.

Grizzlefur recited the ancient oaths.

  

Skwik was anointed Protector of Queen Pebbles, receiving the Vermin’s Bite, a dagger with a curved blade of fused rat teeth, each sharpened to a cruel point and set into a dark resin.

  

Verminciani was appointed Regent in Pebbles absence.

  

Cedrick, Lullaby, and Rinji had no idea what was going on, unable to understand the squeaks from the rats unlike Achrotheth and Skwik.

  

The rat party began.

Rat caught the eye of Rinji, giving him a knowing look while gnawing a popcorn kernel.

  

The group left, exiting from a manhole close to the Spectral Vanquishers.

  

Cedrick knocked, greeted by Rae Stanzwick, the stumpier of the trio, with a set of lenses attached to a head band and moving without intervention.

  

Cedrick was invited inside, to complete the transaction to borrow the spirit trap. He read the find print of the contract, noting that there a considerable amount of liability waivers.

Cedrick asked how to use the trap, Rae explained that the gjost needed to be weakened, and the trapped placed underneath, warning that it was not a good idea for anyone living to be caught in the radius.

  

Cedrick agreed and exited the huilding he mistook for a business, but was a residence, triggering a wild magic surge.

  

Lullaby and Achroteth watched as Rinji, Cedrick, and Skwik were overcome with uncontrollable laughter.

  

When the laighter subsided, Cedrick examined the simple looking trap he had just paid for, placing it into his bag of holding.

  

The moment he drew the drawstring closed he realised he had a massive a significant miscalculation.
