## What happened to Petalwood
- Althea has been appointed interim Mayor
- Rorick has been appointed interim Sheriff
- The council has began redrafting a new Town Charter, aimed at preventing ting anything like this happen again
- Elections have been scheduled to occur in 2 weeks time
- 

### After 1 week
- Fey have began taking up residence in Petalwood, so far this has been a peaceful process
- Some residents of the town deny Finroth, unable to face the reality of what he had done while they were either complicit or ignorant
- Others in town believe it important to talk about it, so that they remember, and learn from.the experience
- 


### After 2 weeks
- Althea is elected Mayor
- Rorick is confirmed as Sheriff (Pending the crown's endorsement - does Fawn vouch for him)
- The Town council structure is changed, reduced to only 3 positions, that share power - Mayor, Sheriff, Purser, with term limits
- An Assembly of the Hive is formed, a group of 6 elected memebers, half must be Fey, that have the power to advise and if necessary, challenge the rulings of the council
- Automatons have been distributed equitably amongst the townsfolk, no new Automatons will be ordered
- Some villagers are unhappy that Automatons remain in town, but understand that breaking the contract would bankrupt Petalwood
