## Key Events

**Moonday, 8th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 

The chamber was drowned in darkness. A living, breathing abyss.

Lullaby waved a hand in front of their face—nothing. No flicker of movement, no silhouette. Not even Achroteth’s star-forged form could cut through it. The darkness wasn’t just an absence of light. It was a presence.

Fawn clenched her jaw, reaching inward, summoning the radiance of her divine armor.

Nothing. Even the magic recoiled. The room had been swallowed whole. They were blind. 

And Seralith… Seralith was hunting.

In turn they struck out where they thought Seralith might be—a sweep of a blade, a burst of energy, aimed at the sound of shifting cloth and breath.

But the attempts proved futile.

Then a sudden, blinding surge The void collapsed in on itself and the room was whole again. Moonlight streamed through the ceiling cracks, shadows retreating like a tide pulling away from shore.

And in that light Dorgle stood before the altar, jaw tight, stance firm. "I will give you what you want," he called, voice steady, "if you let them leave."

The words hung in the air. A pact. A price. A sacrifice.

But maybe, just maybe, it was exactly the opening they needed.

Achroteth moved quickly. He sprinted to one of the standing mirrors, angling it, bending the light. A beam shot across the room, touching the edge of a mosaic carved into the wall.

Fawn’s mind snapped into focus. The Moonstone. Only one marked by Sehanine could take it from the Heart of the Temple. She ran. Her fingers closed around the precious artifact before Dorgle could.

A heartbeat later, Seralith’s eyes locked onto her.  He saw it. And he wanted it.

Cedrick darted to another mirror, hands steady, shifting it toward the next mosaic. The phases of the moon. They had to be activated in the right order.

Seralith snarled, lunging forward.

Necrotic energy churned in his hands, black mist slithering across his fingers before he let it loose. Fawn twisted, barely dodging the wave of decay. Dorgle took the brunt of another blast, his skin paling as death magic curled through his veins.

Cedrick’s mirror locked into place.

Achroteth adjusted another.

The pieces were falling into place.

Lullaby darted across the chamber. They skidded to a stop before the final mirror, braced themselves and tilted it just so.

The sanctum shuddered. Moonlight poured through the fractures in the ceiling, streaming through the mirrors, bouncing across mosaics. Power surged, filling the chamber, flooding the darkness with something pure.

Seralith screamed. His form wavered. The divine radiance cut through him like a blade of celestial steel.

Taking advantage of the opportunity, their attentions turned to defeating Seralith. Achroteth raised a hand. A star ignited at his fingertips, a single point of light. Then it streaked forward. A brilliant, searing flash. Seralith’s body shuddered, convulsed, then faded.

The shadows that had once clung to him vanished. And then—he was gone.  The chamber was silent. Only the moonlight remained.

Fawn lingered.

The ritual chamber lay still, emptied of the battle’s fury, yet something in its air remained heavy. A presence, an echo of faith twisted into something unrecognizable. Her gaze drifted to the columns.

Madness.

Scrawled across the stone in uneven, erratic script, the thoughts of a mind unravelling. Yet amid the tangled words, patterns emerged. Truths, or perhaps lies masquerading as wisdom.

- **Worship is Power.** Devotion is the only true measure of worth; claim it at all costs.
- **Faith is a Weapon.** Turn the faith of others against them, for the devout are the easiest to manipulate.
- **The Light Betrays.** Beauty, kindness, and grace are hollow masks; true strength lies in shadows and deception.

A final phrase stood alone, deliberate, as if set apart for a reason.

**Find the Veil of Devotion.**

A command. A directive. A warning.

Fawn exhaled, the weight of the words settling into her bones. Then, as she turned to leave, Elysandrial, the celestial presence flickered at the chamber’s edge, a smile soft as candlelight, a nod like the shifting of the stars. Then she faded away.

Achroteth’s voice cut through the moment, bringing Fawn back to the present. “The headdress,” he reminded her. “The one in the storage room.” A possibility. A tether to something greater.

With renewed purpose, they returned. Cedrick examined the artifact with careful hands, tracing the silver filigree and glimmering gemstones woven into the delicate circlet. The magic within pulsed faintly beneath his fingertips—old, powerful, celestial.

"The Starlight Diadem," he murmured. "A relic, yes. But not the Veil of Devotion." So the mystery remained.

They stepped beyond the temple’s threshold. The forest stirred with the hush of impending dawn. Golden light pierced through the canopy, soft and dappled, painting shifting patterns across the ground.

But something was missing. The Guardian Wisps were nowhere to be seen. The reflecting pool in the courtyard remained undisturbed, its surface still mirroring constellations that did not belong to this sky.

And the butterfly wings, he ones that had lingered at the edges of their vision, subtle but ever-present, were gone.

They reached the carriage.

Butch and Cassidee were still inebriated, their laughter lilting, careless, as if the night’s horrors had not touched them.

The journey back was smooth.

And in the quiet, Fawn studied the Sacred Moonstone.

The relic rested in her hands, a crescent of milky-white gemstone, its surface adorned with delicate carvings of swirling starlit skies and lunar phases. Along its curve, tiny silver inlays formed constellations, shimmering faintly as if reflecting a sky unseen.

But there was more.

When bathed in moonlight, the carvings shifted. Moved. Fleeting glimpses of hidden truths and distant realms whispered across its surface, teasing at knowledge just beyond comprehension.

A key.

A guide.

A power.

And whoever held it… would never look at the night sky the same way again.


## Summary

**Moonday, 8th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 


The ritual chamber was still awash in a magical darkness, Lullaby not able to see their hand waving in front of their own face. Achroteth, still in his starry form, provided no illumination within the room. Fawn tried to draw on her gifts to make her armour glow with magical light, but even that was no match for the sheer force of the darkness.

Accepting the situation, they attempted to attack Seralith aiming at where they thought the fallen cleric had slithered to, but their attempts  proves to be futile.

Suddenly the darkness disappeared and the room was once again filled with light. 

 Taking a moment to adjust their eyes to see Dorgle approach the altar and call out “I will give you what you want, if you let them leave”, willing to sacrifice himself for the safety of his companions.

But this may have just been the diversion that that was needed. Achroteth ran to one of the mirrors around the chamber and pointed it at one of the large mosaics on the wall.

Fawn, knowing that only someone blessed with the mark of Sehanine may take the Moonstone from the Heart of the Temple, quickly snatched it up before Dorgle got the chance. This drew the attention of Seralith, his eyes following the precious relic. 

Cedrick bolts towards another mirror, ready to angle it the next mosaic in the right order of the phases of the moon.

Seralith launched his attacks at Dorgle and Fawn, necrotic energy spewing from his hands.

As Cedrick moves his mirror, Achroteth angled a third.

Lullaby scurried across the room to move the final mirror. The magic lanterns around the sanctum began to dim, as the moonbeams bursting through the ceiling expanded and flooded the room with moonlight, severely weakening Seralith.

With the mosaics lit, they all turned their attention to defeating the devotee of Kalistyr. Achroteth delivers the final blow, a flash of light that streaked towards Seralith and faded the shadowy figure away.

Fawn took a moment to take one last look within the chamber before they headed back to Brother Varras. Scrawled on the columns seemed to be the ramblings of someone gone mad. But key phrases kept repeating throughout the gibberish of words.

- **Worship is Power.** Devotion is the only true measure of worth; claim it at all costs.
- **Faith is a Weapon.** Turn the faith of others against them, for the devout are the easiest to manipulate.
- **The Light Betrays.** Beauty, kindness, and grace are hollow masks; true strength lies in shadows and deception.

One other phrase stood out, a single sentence, a command, a reminder, "Find the Veil of Devotion."

Leaving the room, Elysandrial gave a warm smile and gentle nod of approval before fading away.

Achroteth reminded Fawn about the head dress they saw earlier in the storage room, sending them back there with purpose. Cedric spent some moments studying the head ware and learning that it was the Starlight Diadem, helpful nonetheless but not the Veil of Devotion.

They exited the temple, spying the rays of sunlight sneak through the leaves of the trees overhead, signalling the impending dawn. The Guardian Wisps were nowhere to be seen. The reflecting pool in the centre of the courtyard still reflected constellations that were not in the sky above. And the butterfly wings that they had become accustomed to disappeared.

They found their way to the carriage, Butch and Cassidee still inebriated. The journey back was a smooth one, Fawn had time to study the Sacred Moonstone they had come looking for.

The holy relic was an intricately carved, milky-white gemstone in the shape of a crescent moon, its surface adorned with delicate etchings of swirling starlit skies and lunar phases. Embedded along its outer curve were tiny silver inlays resembling constellations, which shimmer faintly as if reflecting a night sky. When bathed in moonlight, the carvings seem to shift and dance, revealing fleeting glimpses of hidden truths and distant realms.

But the moonstone also gave some powerful benefits to whomever held it.
