## Summary

**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 

Cedrick heard a slight hum originating from his bag. The sound evolved into a frantic crescendo of crackling and buzzing energy.

A bright explosion of mystic purple light emanated from Cedrick’s bag of holding, blinding everyone caught within the blast radius.

The party each felt an invisible hand press firmly upon their chest, the coldness sending chills down their spines.

Cedrick felt something off, his body invaded by the invisible hand.

The light subsided, and the party stood in the street staring at each other wondering what had just happened.

The people in the streets of Glitterfall all shared the same look of disbelief mixed with fear.

Achroteth and Lullaby shared their experience, while Cedrick sheepishly withheld the details of the hand reaching into his chest.

Cedrick was checking the ghost trap and co firmed that it was still in perfect working order when Aegon Sparkler, Rae Stanzwick, and Petran Venkross rushed from [[The Spectral Vanquishers]] residence, excitedly they asked Cedrick what he had done.

The Spectral Vanquishers confirmed that the ghosts, spooks, and spectres jailed within the trap had been released however they specific details of how many seemed to change with each exclamation.

Cedrick’s guilt began to bubble in his stomach, he wondered to himself, "what had he just done?"

[[Skwik]] however proposed that this was but a profitable business opportunity for the trio, that they would be able to charge again to recapture these ghouls.

This appeased Aegon, Rae, and Petran.

Wasting no time, Cedrick led Achrotheth, Lullaby, and Skwik towards the Tyrellion crypt.

The half day journey felt like no time had passed, travelling through open fields, then [[The ... Forest]] with the sounds of birds and other forest creatures providing a tranquil soundtrack for the adventurers.

Cedrick took lead and was first to notice up ahead was a group of unusually large trees.

These trees formed a perimeter around a clearing that contained a rock formation. Upon closer inspection, the rock formation was an optical illusion. It was not one rock, but many rocks all spaced apart, but arranged in a way that hid a cave entrance adorned with blackened vines and steps leading down into a dark lair.

The smaller rocks were arranged like headstones, satisfying Cedrick that this was the location of the long forgotten Tyrellion crypt.

Skwik surveyed the area, noticing that no animal tracks seemed to venture past the tree line. He encountered a deer, and drew upon his arcane gifts to communicate with it. The deer explained that the cave was dark, not just because there was no light, but the blackened vines surrounding the entrance proved there was no food fit for their consumption.

Skwik offered to dissuade any hunters in the area if they came upon any, but the deer smirked preferring that Skwik let them come through. It was at the moment that 15 other deer appeared from their hiding spots, all carefully camouflaged as if waiting to turn the predators unknowingly into the prey.

Cedrick quickly descended the stairs into the crypt, Achroteth, Skwik, and Lullaby quickly followed.

They were met with a large chamber, filled with collapsed statues of armored figures standing guard along the walls.

A cracked console-like structure hums faintly across from the entrance. Cedrick and Lullaby headed over to investigate it, while Achroteth studied the statues and Skwik snuck off to an adjoining room.

Cedrick and Lullaby noticed that the console had 25 buttons arranged in a 5 x 5 grid. Each button was illuminated with a warm yellow light except for one, which was red.

Cedrick could not decipher the runes on the buttons, but instinctively knew that somehow this was important, or at the very least a new technology to uncover and reverse engineer.

Lullaby found some stone tablets tossed aside near the console, the words inscribed showing the effects of age a d erosion.

Cedrick tried earnestly but had no reference point to begin interpreting them, but Lullaby unlocked a forgotten memory and the words came to life for him.



Investigation DC 15: Reveals symbols referring to “Heartstones” — crystalline anchors of soul energy.

Trap: When tampered with, a burst of necrotic energy lashes out (DEX save DC 15 or 4d6 necrotic damage).
