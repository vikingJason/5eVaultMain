## Summary

**Waterday, 11th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 


Fawn and Achroteth entered the council chambers to meet with Finroth.

Finroth and Fawn engaged in a political discourse, Finroth admitted that the council was too bloated and stuck in ceremony and believed that for the good of the town it needed to be trimmed down. As a gesture of his intentions to only do what was good for Petalwood he offered to step down once things had settled.

Achroteth silently contemplated exercising caution however Fawn found Finroth’s explanations agreeable.

As the pair left the chambers, Fawn updated the others and they each went their separate ways.

Achroteth found a place to lie down, stair up at the clouds in the sky and relax. It didnt take long to notice Dorgle at a table talking to Glendel.

Lullaby followed him to discuss what happened in the council chamber. After their curiosity was satisfied, they left.

The mail server arrives and handed Achroteth an e-mail.

> [!quote ]
> To My Branchbound Kin,
> 
> The veins bleed shadows. What once sang with light now shudders in silence. Beneath your feet, the world is withering—not in bark or leaf, but in the crystal bones of our mother.
> 
> I have seen it, in dreams not my own. The pulse of the ley is stilled. The Grove of Heartroot yet remains — untouched, unspoiled — and it is there the old rite must be performed.
> 
> You know it, though you do not remember learning. It was woven into your soul with root and ritual. The Rite of Verdant Resonance.
> 
> But be warned: the balance cannot be restored by effort alone. It demands a gift beyond price. A soul rooted to the land must bind themselves to the ley.
> 
> The stars whisper of a Glimmering Eclipse in four dawns' time. Go swiftly. And choose wisely.
> 
> May the Song of the Soil carry you home.



Achroteth pondered for a moment. The Rite of Resonance, he knew it would reset the laylines but it required the willing sacrifice of a powerful magic being of the land, possibly the Dryad.

---

Fawn walked the grounds of the Unity Festival with Celly and Ness. The princess's companions wearing masks that seemed to become suddenly popular. But Fawn noticed that while wearing the masks, those she held dear to her heart were different, their behaviour odd.

Celly and Ness seemed unusually attached to the masks, unwilling to remove them. Fawn knew foul play was afoot. She wrestled the mask off Celly's face and break it.

When that happened, all the automatons in the area snap to look at her for a moment. Too quick for Fawn to catch it, her attention focussed on Celly who had become motionless. Fawn then casts a spell to remove any magical interference and marched back to The Troll’s Tollbooth.

The princess has not spotted her ally, Achroteth whonwas laying down and watching it all unfold, get up and follow them.

---

Fawn wanting answers made her way to Town Hall, just as a session was concluding. The council and Finroth emerged with pomp and ceremony to announce and that the council has been reinstated and that they are actively working to remove beauracratic red tape. 

The council stood their, smiles across their face that didnt sit right. To the more intuitive, it was easy to tell that they obviously under some kind of mind control.

Achrotheth, from a concealed position, dug his heels into the dirt and try to coax the magic of the laylines to cast Dispel Magic across the whole council.

As he closed his eyes to cast the spell, the druid fell into a trance. His consciousness was transported to a void where a pair of gargantuan dragons are intertwined, a white and black dragon of ancient and immense power. They both look at him, their vision is piercing. 

Suddenly he is snapped back to reality, observing that his attempts to cast the spell worked for Althea and [[Lilareth]].

The party headed to the tavern, with Althea following closely behind.

With everyone gathered in the common room of The Troll’s Tollbooth, Fawn and Achroteth work together to remove Ness’s mask and the magic that is coercing her.
