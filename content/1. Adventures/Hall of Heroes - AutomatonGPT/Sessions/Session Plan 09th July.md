Thank you! Based on your answers, here’s a complete **3-phase climactic encounter** for the **final confrontation with Finroth**, the manipulative architect of greed, betrayal, and mechanical domination—wrapped in the guise of unity and inclusion.

---

> [!warning]  
> **Unity Fractured: The Fall of Finroth**  
> A 3-Phase Boss Encounter for 6 Level 5 PCs  
> Setting: Petalwood’s Unity Festival

---

## 🧩 OVERVIEW

- **Setting:** The Unity Festival in full swing—bunting, magical lights, and townsfolk gathered in the main square.
    
- **Twist:** The Automatons, meant to represent unity, suddenly begin to **"maintain order"**—i.e., **violently suppress** Fey guests and dissenters.
    
- **Finroth:** Calm, smiling, casting spells through enchanted speaker-crystals, controlling the festival like a play.
    
- **Themes:** Control vs. Chaos, Truth vs. Perception, Sacrifice, and Legacy.
    

---

## 🎭 PHASE 1: **The Puppetmaster’s Smile**

**Location:** Festival Plaza (main square); joyful, colorful, packed with townsfolk and Fey guests.

**Goal:** Survive the ambush and expose Finroth's manipulation to the public.

**Events:**

- **Finroth appears atop the festival dais**, giving a speech of unity.
    
- **He casts _Mass Suggestion_** ("Let the guardians restore order!")—turning parts of the crowd into his unwitting allies.
    
- **Automaton Soldiers activate**, targeting Fey guests and outspoken citizens.
    
- **Quickling diplomat tries to flee**, a Dryad is captured by soldiers.
    

**Combat:**

- 4x **Clockwork Legionnaires** (CR 2-3)
    
- 1x **Enforcer Automaton** (CR 3, riot-shield + stun baton style)
    

**Tactical Notes:**

- Hostile NPCs under _Mass Suggestion_ complicate the battlefield—players must **avoid collateral damage**.
    
- PCs can **make persuasion checks** to rouse allies or **dispel the charm**.
    

**Moral Choice:**

- Save **Celly** (Fawn’s love) as she’s cornered by an Enforcer.
    
- Or protect **the Dryad dignitary**, risking a blow to Fey-kingdom relations if she’s harmed.
    

---

## 🌀 PHASE 2: **Winding Gears, Winding Lies**

**Location Change:** The town’s central **Clocktower**, now revealed as Finroth’s secret lair—an arcane, gear-filled sanctum.

**Trigger:** Once Automatons fall, Finroth retreats, shouting, _“They’ll never understand your worth like I do!”_

**Goal:** Ascend the crumbling clocktower, disable the arcane machine, and confront Finroth before he finishes his ritual.

**Events:**

- As they ascend:
    
    - Gears grind chaotically.
        
    - **Magic pulses in waves** (wild magic zones).
        
    - **Time-distortion effects** (PCs act twice or lose turns).
        
- The machine is powered by **Fey essence** and **coin-blooded steel**.
    

**Combat:**

- 2x **Arc-Welded Sentinels** (custom bizarre constructs with elemental cores)
    
- Hazard: **Moving gears, random slow/haste effects**
    

**Skill Challenge:** Disable magical conduits powering the immortality ritual (Arcana, Athletics, Sleight of Hand).

**Moral Choice:**

- **Bax** is trapped in a containment pod—used as a “test subject.” Saving him slows their ascent, allowing Finroth to finish preparations.
    

---

## ☄️ PHASE 3: **Unity’s Lie: Finroth Ascended**

**Location:** Rooftop of the clocktower, under starlight and festival lanterns.  
**Sky breaks open**—cosmic Fey portals flicker; a **Fey comet** crosses the sky (the “Celestial Accord”).

**Finroth now fused** with his own machine—an elegant **Humanoid-Construct Hybrid**, gold-plated, filled with burning greed.

**Finroth’s Theme:**  
He believes **unity is obedience**, **love is currency**, and **freedom is chaos**.

---

### 🌟 BOSS FIGHT: Finroth, the Gilded Lie

**Abilities:**

- _Mass Charm Person_, _Mass Suggestion_, _Counterspell_
    
- **Greed Pulse (Recharge 5-6):** Forces PCs to choose between an ally or advantage (e.g., +2 to hit, but an ally takes damage).
    
- **Coinlash Tendrils:** Whip-like tendrils of enchanted gold from his back.
    
- Legendary Action: **Guilt Mirror** – Reflects a PC’s failed saving throw onto an ally.
    

**Allies:**

- 1x **Sentinel of Order** (Giant Automaton with arcane-stabilizers)
    
- Reinforcements of charmed townsfolk unless pacified in Phase 1.
    

---

### 💔 FINAL MORAL CHOICE:

**During the battle**, the machine begins to overload.

- **The Dryads rush to stabilize it**, but **Ness** is caught inside the Fey-essence feedback loop.
    

> 🧠 The Players must decide:

- **Destroy the machine quickly**, saving the dignitaries but killing Ness.
    
- Or **delay**, saving Ness, but letting the Fey diplomatic accord collapse in chaos.
    

This will affect **future Fey involvement** and perhaps the return of an angry Fey Court later.

---

## 🎁 OPTIONAL RESOLUTIONS

- If the players **free the charmed townsfolk**, the crowd may turn against Finroth and **delay reinforcements**.
    
- If Celly was saved, she can help **reach Fawn**, who can **banish Fey energies** from the machine.
    
- If Bax was saved, he may sacrifice himself to **hold the machine’s collapse**, giving the players an edge.

Excellent! Here are **Lair Actions**, **Tactical Notes**, and **Custom Items** for **Finroth, the Gilded Architect**, to elevate your climactic encounter into something legendary, emotional, and tactical.

---

## 🌀 **Lair Actions** — _The Unity Spire_

Finroth has modified the central Unity Spire with arcane-tech enhancements, giving him control over the battlefield. On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), Finroth takes a lair action. He can’t use the same effect two rounds in a row.

### 🛡️ **1. Arcane Overload**

Arcane circuits in the walls hum violently. Finroth chooses a 10-ft-radius zone within 120 ft. It surges with energy until initiative count 20 next round. The first creature to cast a spell inside the zone must roll a d6:

- 1–2: The spell fails, slot wasted.
    
- 3–4: Spell is cast normally.
    
- 5–6: The spell is empowered — creatures affected by it make saves at disadvantage or take +1d8 damage.
    

### 🪞 **2. Holo-Doubles**

An array of illusion projectors activates. Until the next round, all creatures Finroth designates gain **1 illusory duplicate** (as _mirror image_). They pop if hit, but don’t use the spell’s mechanics.

### ⚙️ **3. Recall the Machines**

Finroth summons aid from his workshop's fail-safes. One inactive Automaton Sentinel reactivates at 1/2 HP and joins the fight under his control.

### 🌪️ **4. Spatial Fold**

A shimmering gate appears. Finroth teleports up to 60 feet to any unoccupied space in the lair. Creatures within 10 feet of his arrival point must make a DC 15 Dex save or be pushed 10 ft and knocked prone.

---

## 🧠 **Tactical Notes**

Finroth is not a brute-force villain. He is manipulative, elusive, and theatrical. His battle strategy is designed around control, deception, and spectacle.

### 📌 **Pre-Battle Preparation**

- Casts _mirror image_ or _greater invisibility_ during speeches.
    
- Uses _mass suggestion_ during festival to sway the crowd: e.g., "You must not interfere — this is for your salvation!"
    
- His automatons guard points of interest (VIPs, fey, mechanisms).
    

### 🧠 **Phase Breakdown**

- **Phase 1: Crowd Control** – Uses _mass charm person_ and _hypnotic pattern_ to delay interference. Focuses on splitting the party and activating lair defenses.
    
- **Phase 2: Reveal & Regroup** – Once wounded (below 50%), his arm splits open to reveal a second magical core. Switches to aggressive tactics: _dominate person_, _wall of force_ to isolate PCs, uses “Command Automaton” to pin casters.
    
- **Phase 3: Final Form** – Activates Arcane Conductor, initiates lair-wide overload (Area pulses, danger zones). May attempt to flee if truly losing, threatening to detonate the Unity Spire or sacrifice someone beloved.
    

### ❤️ **Moral Choice Point**

He grabs either:

- Ness, Celly, or Bax
    
- A Fey Dignitary  
    He uses _mass suggestion_ on civilians: "They'll destroy all you've built! Stand with me or let the kingdom fall."  
    Players must choose: save the loved one or stop Finroth’s transformation ritual.

- While attuned, you gain advantage on Concentration checks.

> ⚠️ Removing the core causes a cascade failure — the spire may collapse or a planar gate may crack open...

Here are **six strategic options** you can seed into your game as hints, informants, or discoveries to give your players multiple approaches to take Finroth down. These add variety, encourage roleplay, and support different playstyles—stealth, combat, social, magical, and inventive thinking:

---

### 🔧 1. **Bax-bot Rebuild Side Quest**

**Hint:** Bax’s scattered parts contain secret anti-Automaton protocols—if reassembled, he can emit a short-range burst to disable or reprogram nearby Automatons.

- The pieces must be reassembled and powered using Quickling crystals or rare components.
    
- Bax has partial memory logs revealing weak points in Finroth's control systems or surveillance network.
    
- Possible new ability: _Baxburst Pulse_ – 1/day, disables Automatons within 30 ft for 1 round unless they succeed a CON save (DC 14).
    

---

### 🌿 2. **Dryad Alliance**

**Hint:** The forest Dryads are planning to retaliate after being displaced by Finroth’s machines, but they need help entering the town safely.

- Players can broker an alliance between the Dryads and Fey Quicklings, creating a flanking forest-force.
    
- The Dryads have access to powerful _plant growth_ and _ensnaring vines_ magic that can hinder Automatons in town streets.
    
- Could unlock a special Fey-druidic ritual to _seal Finroth’s power conduit_ (takes 10 rounds during final battle).
    

---

### 🔮 3. **Sabotage the Central Power Conduit**

**Hint:** Finroth draws his Automaton control magic from an arcane lattice beneath Petalwood (once a Fey convergence point).

- Using arcana or engineering skill, players learn of _four core pylons_ that relay the control signal.
    
- Each one disabled (physically or magically) increases the Automaton failure chance by 10%.
    
- Dismantling all four causes mass disarray among the Automatons, or turns them against Finroth with a CHA check (DC 18).
    

---

### 🕯️ 4. **Infiltrate Through the Dreaming**

**Hint:** A Sehanine-worshipping dreamweaver offers to guide one character into Finroth’s subconscious—where his true fears and secrets are hidden.

- Through this dream-quest, the character learns _Finroth’s original mortal identity_ and something he deeply regrets (e.g., the loss of a sibling or betrayal of a mentor).
    
- They gain a token of his guilt (a dream-sigil or name), which can be used in conversation or magic to weaken his will (e.g., impose disadvantage on saving throws vs being charmed/frightened).
    
- Also grants 1 insight into his tactics, lair, or item.
    

---

### 🗝️ 5. **Automaton Defectors**

**Hint:** A pair of Automatons, secretly programmed by their original inventor (not Finroth), have developed a form of free will.

- These Automatons are hiding among the townsfolk or hiding with the Fey.
    
- If found and convinced, they can guide the party past security wards and unlock restricted areas.
    
- They can also jam Finroth’s signal for 1 minute once per day, allowing stealth or high-impact combat turns.
    

---

### 👑 6. **Fawn’s Dilemma: The Crown’s Leverage**

**Hint:** Finroth intends to blackmail Fawn into betraying the capital in exchange for the safety of Ness and Celly.

- Fawn learns she can contact her mother (the Queen) through a magical artifact, but doing so alerts Finroth’s scryers unless warded by Fey magic.
    
- Players can help craft a counter-message: _a public Fey broadcast that outs Finroth’s plans during the festival_, risking early combat but potentially rallying the townsfolk.
    
- Can be used to divide loyalties and cause chaos among Finroth’s supporters.
    

---

## 🧠 DM Tips: How to Introduce These

- Drop clues during short rests (e.g., "Bax’s eyes flicker with a strange glow as if… waiting.")
    
- Use NPCs like Celly, Ness, the Quicklings, or even Fey spirits to hint at solutions.
    
- Give players **too many** options—let them discuss, divide tasks, or gamble on outcomes. This creates ownership.
    
- Each option should offer a partial edge—not total victory—to reward smart combination (e.g., sabotage + Baxburst + public broadcast = epic finale).
