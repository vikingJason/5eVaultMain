## Summary

**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 

We chat with the Quicklings and we organise our plan.

2 points of attack
Dorgle, Rinji the Red Caps and half the Quicklings. They will head down main street, Quicklings will clear the people with masks, so the others can make a big distraction and attack automatons on the way to town hall.
Cedric, Lullaby, myself and the other half of the Quicklings. We will move through the town stealthfully towards the town hall.

Cedric works on the crystal bomb, is able to get a prototype that works but not for long.

The Quicklings start to panic and we find that someone who seems to be a wounded Celly. As she approaches, I demand she reveal her true self and when she plays dumb, after I call the real Celly forward, I cast Moonbeam on her and she reveals her true Changeling form. She tells us that Finroth is planning on stealing Fawn’s mind. Dorgle then steps forward and kills the Changeling.

We then decide to march back towards town and camp when we get tired as to not avoid exhustion.
