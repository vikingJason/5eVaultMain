## Summary

**Galeday, 10th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 


The events of the past few hours hung in the air like a bad smell that no one wanted to acknowledge out loud. Fawn headed upstairs to get changed. The party prepare to go find Calarel, quiet and inconspicuously, not wanting to draw attention until they were fully aware of what they were dealing with.

Achroteth stepped outside, quickly noticing that there were automatons at guard every 40 feet.

The druid ducked behind the building, hiding in the shadows and cast Animal Messenger to find Calarel. The bird of prey that answered the call flew off into the sky to try and find the Royal Archivist, acting as nature's sattelite.

Achrotheth asked Fawn to find something that bonged to Calarel. A backup plan forming in his mind. She came back quickly, with a shirt in hand. The dragonborn turned into a Mastiff, using the beasts keen sense of smell to pick up the trail while Rinji to kept an eye on the bird in the sky.

Achroteth, Fawn, Rinji, Dorgle and Cedrick followed the scent to town hall while Lullaby stayed behind to study the documents recovered from Elrohir's office.

As the group arrived they found a small gathering of town council members and fey dignitaries engaged in an intimate social event.

The visitors spread out.

Fawn headed to the Quicklings, discovering that they have made a different deal with Finroth to give them each citizen's memory of their child's first birth. They thanked Fawn for facilitating the new partnership.

Achroteth found [[Laurelia]] the Dryad who shared that she can detect the chaos in town. She went on to warn that it is very unusual and will cause problems, explaining that there seems to be a dam in the natural flow of the water and someone is carving additional pathways for the mana to flow.

Perturbed, Achroteth went over to speak with Althea, who  agreed to sneak him into Finroth’s office. When her key did not work, the delegate of the Whispering Court pulled out a lock pick and swiftly opened the door. Inside, she gave a look over the spellbook and took it as evidence. 

With everything in order, the group all head back to the Trolls Tollbooth.

---

Lullaby poured over the documents recovered from Elrohir's office, hoping to uncover anything that would help them put the puzzle together.

They read through the letters, status reports meant for Calarel. They warned of an unseen danger, untrust towards the Council's decision to contract the use of Automatons. The warnings intensified, with each letter they read.

Lullaby's concern grew, the dates and the information captured did not match the reports that Calarel had shared with them prior to their journey.

Determined, Lullaby continued. They studied the map, a map of Petalwood with marks, locations circled, crosses near others. Some of the points marked were locations they had visited, often to help with some problem or another. But why, what did it mean.

Faintly written in the border of the map were a series of numbers. 

Cedrick reviewed the ledger, his time spent with his Dorgle guildmaster gave him some knowledge of how these things would work.

His investigation was thorough, and what he uncovered was peculiar. He consulted the map, then back to the ledger, the map, and then back to the ledger.

He revealed what he learned. The ledger was the registration for all businesses within Petalwood. This alone was normal, but it was what Cedrick revealed next that caused concern. Most, if not all of the registrations could be traced back to incorporated entities, their registration numbers etched on the map.

These corporations were set up in a way to try and distance them all from the holding company that owned these subsidiaries, EFT Inc.

Elara Finroth Tilvenar Inc.

The mayor.

He didn't just lead Petalwood. He owned it.

Without hesitation, Lullaby knew they had something important so they made copies of everything.

The party returned to The Troll's Tollbooth with Calarel as Lullaby finished their work.

Fawn tells them about Finroth touching his necklace. Lullaby revealed what they had uncovered.

Fawn is told by Calarel that she sent them a letter a few days ago, but Fawn protested knowing it was not her that sent it.

Althea joined them at the Inn. They shared with the evidence they found, giving her a copy to present to the Council to try and remove Finroth from his position.

Althea warned the visitors that it would take a unanimous decision to remove Finroth, but was optimistic that the evidence now was too damming and would be enough to convince the others.

**Waterday, 11th Of Emberleaf, Cycle 6880 CCE** 

Dorgle left early to send a letter.

As he returned Achrotheth intercepted him, wishing to speak to privately. The Dragonborn interrogated the Dwarf about the changelings and that he shoul not be so ignorant, educating him that not all dragons were inherently evil.

Dorgle, in a moment of vulnerability and trust, revealed his first memories to Achroteth. He explained that he was only three weeks old, that the first memories he had were waking in some kind of labrotory surrounded by other things that looked like him.

He shared the harrowing story of how the Dorgles tried to kill him, but he had been able to defeat them, watching in horror as their bodies transformed into those Changeling creatures upon their death. From then on Dorgle knew one absolute truth,  Dorgles were Changelings, and Changelings were bad.

What he didn't know was why the Changelings had created him.

They returned to join the others at their usual table.

Althea knocked on the door and was let her in. Her demanour was different, her smile seemed forced. He disposition more agreeable than usual.

She greeted the party exhanging pleasantried before delivering the update they had all been patiently waiting to hear.

"The council unanimously voted to extend Finroth’s power and disband the council."

Silence.

They weren't sure they had heard correctly.

Sensing something was off, Achroteth use his magic to dispell any magic that may be affecting their Ally.

The smile was wiped from Althea's face.

She looked distraught, coming to terms with weight of her magically coerced actions.

"Wha... what have we done?"
