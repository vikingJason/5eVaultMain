
## 🐀 **Gnawfang, the Vermin’s Bite**

> _“When you hold it close, you can hear them gnashing in the dark — all the little mouths that never stopped eating.”_  
> — Grizzlefur elder to a would-be assassin

---

**Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement by a ranger or warlock)**

This dagger’s blade is **a curve of fused rat teeth**, each sharpened to a cruel point and set into a dark resin that seems to pulse faintly with life. When drawn, it releases a faint chittering sound — the echoes of the countless vermin whose teeth birthed it. Its handle is wrapped in sewer silk and bound with sinew, slightly damp to the touch.

---

### **Stats**

- **Damage:** 1d4 piercing
    
- **Properties:** Finesse, light, thrown (range 20/60 ft.)
    
- **Magic Bonus:** +1 to attack and damage rolls
    
- **Sentience:** Semi-sentient (whispers to its wielder in dreams)
    

---

### **Special Abilities**

**🩸 Swarmbond (1/short rest):**  
When you strike a creature with _Gnawfang_, you may summon a **Spectral Rat Swarm** that bursts from the wound.

- The swarm occupies a 5-foot space adjacent to the target.
    
- It lasts for **1 minute** or until it drops to 0 HP.
    
- The swarm acts immediately after your turn, attacking the same target you struck.
    
- It uses the _Swarm of Rats_ stat block, but deals necrotic damage instead of piercing.
    

**🕳 Vermin’s Step:**  
While attuned, you gain advantage on **Stealth checks** made in dim light or darkness, and you can squeeze through spaces as though you were one size smaller — your body briefly flickering with spectral rat forms.

**🦴 Gnawing Curse:**  
If you kill a creature with Gnawfang, its teeth briefly sprout tiny rat teeth that chatter as it dies. The dagger absorbs part of its essence —  you regain **1d4 hit points**, or one expended **warlock spell slot of 1st level**, whichever you choose (once per long rest).

---

### **Quirk & Corruption**

The dagger _hungers_. If you go **three days without drawing blood** with it, your sleep is plagued by whispers of gnawing and crawling things.  
Fail a DC 12 Wisdom save, and you awake to find small rats nesting near you — or worse, **one of your own teeth missing**, added to the blade.

---

### **Appearance (Optional Evolution)**

As the wielder bonds with it, Gnawfang changes:

- **Stage I:** Faint chittering and glimmering teeth.
    
- **Stage II:** The hilt grows faint whiskers; faint rat eyes open along the blade.
    
- **Stage III:** When drawn, rats within 30 feet pause and bow their heads — a silent salute to their new Rat King.
