# 👑 The Collector, Prince of Neverenough

## True Identity

A Fey prince of Calindor.

Once beloved beyond reason.

His parents:

- indulged every whim
- gifted him impossible treasures
- shielded him from consequence

When they died:

- nobody told him “no”
- nobody taught him grief
- nobody took away the keys to the kingdom

So he continued collecting.

Toys. Stories. People. Moments. Anything that made him feel less alone.

---

# 💎 Connection to the Crystals Beneath Calindor

This is excellent because it ties:

- Fey whimsy
- emotional instability
- magical infrastructure
- environmental consequences

The Fey prince is psychically linked to the crystal lattice beneath Calindor.

His emotions unconsciously affect:

- storms
- seasonal shifts
- auroras
- earthquakes
- strange weather anomalies

---

# 🌩 Emotional Weather Table

Use this subtly throughout the arc.

|Emotion|Effect|
|---|---|
|Excited|Static electricity, glowing rain|
|Upset|Violent winds|
|Lonely|Heavy fog|
|Angry|Thunderstorms, hail, crystal tremors|
|Distracted|Weather changes every few minutes|
|Sad|Soft snowfall regardless of season|

This means: 👉 The party can literally feel his moods before meeting him.

# 🧸 THE LAIR REWORK

## “The Palace of Wonderful Things”

Not a museum.

Not a vault.

A gigantic impossible toyshop built by someone who never emotionally matured.

Think:

- FAO Schwarz built by ancient Fey royalty
- Willy Wonka with abandonment issues
- Spirited Away meets Home Alone

---

# 🎠 VISUAL LANGUAGE OF THE LAIR

Everything should feel:

- oversized
- playful
- slightly broken
- emotionally charged

Examples:

- Corridors made of dollhouses
- Toy trains delivering dangerous artifacts
- Plush creatures acting as servants
- Jack-in-the-box sentries
- Shelves that rearrange based on interest

---

# 🌲 APPROACH TO THE CROSSING (Updated)

Instead of eerie mystery:

The forest slowly becomes _playground logic._

The party notices:

- Hopscotch grids carved into stone
- Gigantic marbles embedded in the earth
- Trees with swing sets hanging impossibly high

The crossing itself is:

## The Broken Music Box Gate

A colossal music box half-swallowed by roots.

Its ballerina no longer spins properly.

When activated:

- The tune slows unnaturally
- The forest folds open like cardboard scenery

# 🏰 ROOM-BY-ROOM REWORK

---

# ROOM 1 — The Welcome Parade

## Description

A winding toyshop entrance where:

- wind-up toys march endlessly
- dolls wave in synchronization
- stuffed animals applaud arrivals

A massive sign overhead:

> “NEW FRIENDS MUST PLAY NICELY.”

---

## Mechanic

Anyone acting hostile:

- toy soldiers begin following them
- the lair marks them as “mean”

---

# ROOM 2 — The Impossible Toy Aisles

## Description

Towering shelves vanish into darkness above.

Contents include:

- swords for giants
- bottled lightning
- clocks that tick backwards
- dragon puppets breathing real smoke

Toy trains race overhead carrying artifacts between rooms.

---

## Fey Puzzle

The aisles rearrange depending on:

- what players desire most

The greedy become lost fastest.

---

# ROOM 3 — The Nursery Storm

## Description

A giant indoor nursery where weather manifests emotionally.

Current mood: ⚡ Angry.

Rainclouds drift near the ceiling. Thunder cracks indoors. Toys float during emotional surges.

---

## Discovery

Signs of Ash Cult fighting:

- burned teddy bears
- shattered toy automatons
- cultists crushed beneath giant blocks

The cult is learning:

> the lair itself reacts emotionally.

---

# ROOM 4 — The Birthday Hall

## Description

An endless birthday party frozen in time.

Cake untouched. Candles still lit. Music looping slightly wrong.

Illusory Fey guests repeat:

> “Best birthday ever…”

---

## The Rule

Players must participate briefly:

- wear hats
- accept cake
- play games

Refusal causes:

- the room to become deeply offended

---

# ROOM 5 — The Playroom War

## Description

The Ash Cult and animated toys are actively battling.

Examples:

- hobby horses trampling cultists
- dolls biting ankles
- cultists setting plush mountains ablaze

This becomes: ⚔️ a dynamic battlefield

---

## Key Reveal

Cult notes reveal:

- The Seed is tied to ancient “Pathway Engines”
- Borfaldor contains a nexus beneath the city

---

# ROOM 6 — The Prince’s Bedroom

## Description

A massive child’s bedroom the size of a cathedral.

Features:

- blanket forts as tall as castles
- moon-and-star mobiles orbiting the ceiling
- mountains of toys

At the center: The Collector’s throne made from stacked toyboxes.

---

# 👑 THE COLLECTOR (FINAL VERSION)

## Appearance

A beautiful Fey prince:

- velvet royal pajamas
- crystal crown slightly crooked
- tear stains beneath his eyes
- carrying a toy sceptre containing terrifying power

Despite appearing youthful: his eyes are ancient.

---

## Personality

Veruca Salt + lonely god-child.

He expects:

- praise
- gifts
- entertainment
- agreement

When denied: ⚡ weather destabilises instantly.

---

## Example Dialogue

> “You can’t take it. It’s _mine._”

> “Everything interesting belongs to me eventually.”

> “Mother said sharing was for ordinary people.”

> “Why does everyone leave when I get upset?”

---

# 🎶 THE SEED MOMENT

The Seed isn’t just knowledge.

It’s:

- a song archive
- emotional memory storage
- Tyrellion navigation harmonics

When Lullaby sings:

The Prince freezes.

The storm stops.

For the first time in centuries: the toyshop becomes quiet.

---

# 🧠 IMPORTANT EMOTIONAL REVEAL

The Prince doesn’t hoard objects because he’s greedy.

He hoards because:

> he’s terrified of losing things again.

That’s the heart of the encounter.

Not: “defeat the Fey prince.”

But:

> “handle the emotions of someone powerful enough to accidentally cause magical disasters.”

---

# 🌩 ENDING OPTIONS

## Peaceful

The party:

- comforts
- distracts
- bargains

He allows them to borrow the Seed.

---

## Chaotic

He tantrums.

The lair begins collapsing into surreal toy chaos.

Storm erupts across Calindor.

---

## Dangerous

Ash Cult attacks directly.

The Prince panics.

Weather catastrophe begins outside.

Now the party must stop:

- cultists
- magical collapse
- emotional meltdown

simultaneously.

---

This version gives you:

- whimsy
- emotional depth
- strong visual identity
- a memorable recurring NPC
- natural ties to the crystal network and Borfaldor

And honestly? Your players will probably adopt him instead of kill him. Which is exactly what you want from Fey nobility.
