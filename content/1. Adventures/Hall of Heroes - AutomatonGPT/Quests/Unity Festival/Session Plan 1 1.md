# **"The Grand Illusion"**  
### **A 3-Phase Encounter for 6 x Level 5 PCs**  
**Theme**: A battle of wits, charm, and brute force against Finroth, the expelled mage who’s turned Petalwood into his puppet theater. The party must choose between saving beloved locals or preserving a fragile fey alliance—all while dodging charmed crowds and his deadly **Automaton Sentinels**.  

---

## **Phase 1: The Masquerade Ball (Social/Stealth)**  
**Location**: Finroth’s estate (during his "victory speech" at the festival’s climax).  
**Goal**: Expose Finroth’s lies *without* triggering a riot.  

### **Challenges**  
- **Charmed Crowd**: Finroth uses *Mass Suggestion* to make the crowd chant his name ("*All hail the Grand Architect!*"). Anyone who speaks against him is jeered or dragged off by automatons.  
- **Automaton Sentinels**:  
  - **Humanoid Drones**: Disguised as "festival guards" (AC 16, immune to charm, *Enforcer’s Strike*: 2d6+3 bludgeoning + grapple).  
  - **Bizarre Sentinel**: A **Clockwork Juggernaut** (reskinned Animated Armor) with a mounted *firework launcher* (15-ft cone, 3d6 fire, Dex save).  

### **Moral Choice (Early)**  
The party spots:  
- **Ness & Bax** being taken away by automatons ("for questioning").  
- **Dryads & Quicklings** trapped in a gilded cage (Finroth plans to sell them to a rival kingdom).  
**Decision**: Save the locals *or* the fey—but not both in time.  

---

## **Phase 2: The Puppet’s Revolt (Skill Challenge/Combat)**  
**Location**: Town square (festival in chaos—collapsing stages, runaway prize livestock).  
**Goal**: Disrupt Finroth’s **control crystal** (held in his cane) while avoiding civilian casualties.  

### **Twist: The Automatons Gain Free Will**  
- A dying dryad’s magic surges, overloading some automatons. They *switch sides* but are **unstable** (explode when reduced to 0 HP: 1d8 force damage in 5 ft).  
- Finroth **charms Celly** (Fawn’s love interest) into attacking the party (*"They’re here to ruin our future!"*).  

### **Tactical Opportunities**  
- **Firework Stall**: Lure enemies near it, then shoot it (15-ft explosion, Dex save or 4d6 fire).  
- **Prize Livestock**: Release the **Giant Goat** (it charges automatons for 2d6 damage).  
- **Fey Cage**: Shattering it frees the Quicklings, who zip around blinding enemies (grants Advantage on one attack per round).  

---

## **Phase 3: Finroth’s Last Trick (Boss Fight + Moral Dilemma)**  
**Location**: Finroth’s crumbling estate balcony (overlooking the screaming crowd).  

### **Finroth’s Tactics**  
- **Phase 1 (Control)**: Hides behind his **Juggernaut Sentinel**, using *Mass Charm Person* on NPCs to throw themselves at the party ("*Stop them!*").  
- **Phase 2 (Desperation)**: Smashes his **control crystal** to overload all automatons (they gain +2 damage but lose immunity to charm).  
- **Phase 3 (Escape)**: Attempts to teleport away—*unless* the party destroys his **spellbook pendant** (AC 18, 15 HP).  

### **Final Choice**  
Finroth’s dying/captured **automaton general** reveals:  
- **Option 1**: The automatons *can be freed*, but doing so will erase their minds (killing Ness/Bax’s captured friends).  
- **Option 2**: Restore them to factory settings—saving lives, but leaving them as slaves.  

---

### **Epilogue Hooks**  
- If the fey were saved: The Quicklings gift the party a **Moonlit Whistle** (summons a fey steed 1/day).  
- If locals were saved: The council offers them **Petalwood’s Crest** (grants *Heroes’ Feast* once/week).  
- If Finroth escapes: His **spellbook** contains notes on "immortality via soul-transfer"—*into an automaton body*.

> ### **Automaton Sentinel**  
> *Medium Construct, Lawful Neutral*  
> **Armor Class** 16 (reinforced plating)  
> **Hit Points** 60 (8d8 + 24)  
> **Speed** 30 ft.  
> 
> | STR  | DEX  | CON  | INT  | WIS  | CHA  |
> |------|------|------|------|------|------|
> | 18 (+4)| 12 (+1)| 16 (+3)| 6 (-2)| 10 (+0)| 5 (-3)|  
> 
> **Damage Resistances** Poison, Psychic  
> **Damage Immunities** Necrotic, Charmed, Exhaustion  
> **Condition Immunities** Frightened, Paralyzed, Unconscious  
> **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10  
> **Languages** Understands Common but cannot speak  
> 
> ### **Abilities**  
> **Overclocked (Optional).** If Finroth breaks his **control crystal**, the sentinel gains +2 to damage rolls but loses immunity to charm.  
> 
> **Command Link.** If Finroth uses his bonus action to issue a command, the sentinel can make one additional attack that turn.  
> 
> **Self-Destruct Protocol.** When reduced to 0 HP, the sentinel explodes in a 5-ft radius. Each creature in the area must make a **DC 13 Dexterity save**, taking **2d6 fire + 2d6 force damage** on a failure (half on success).  
> 
> ### **Actions**  
> **Multiattack.** The sentinel makes two **Enforcer’s Strike** attacks.  
> 
> **Enforcer’s Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
> *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is **grappled** (escape DC 14) if it is Medium or smaller.  
> 
> **Arcane Pulse (Recharge 5–6).** The sentinel emits a shockwave in a 10-ft radius. Each creature in the area must make a **DC 14 Constitution save**, taking **3d6 force damage** and being **pushed 5 feet** on a failure (half damage and no push on success).

### **How to Use Them in Combat**  
- **Phase 1 (Defensive):** Sentinels act as bodyguards, grappling PCs to protect Finroth.  
- **Phase 2 (Overclocked):** After the crystal breaks, they deal more damage but can be turned against Finroth with *Charm Monster* or similar.  
- **Phase 3 (Desperate):** Finroth orders them to self-destruct near PCs.  

**Optional Weakness:** A PC can make a **DC 15 Arcana check** to hack a sentinel’s command link, forcing it to obey a single command ("Attack Finroth!").
