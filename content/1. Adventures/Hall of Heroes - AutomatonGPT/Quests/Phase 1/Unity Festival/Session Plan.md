Here's an exciting 3-phased climactic encounter for your six level 5 PCs designed to challenge them tactically, weave in story elements they've encountered, and present a meaningful moral choice. This climactic showdown takes place in Finroth's estate and his workshops in Petalwood, culminating in a tense negotiation or battle with the mayor and his Automatons.

---

## Phase 1: Estate Courtyard Skirmish — The Outer Gate

**Setup:** The PCs arrive at the Mayor’s estate gate under stormy skies. Finroth’s Automatons patrol heavily here, including two Automaton Sentinels and several smaller Automaton Scouts. Finroth watches from the balcony of his manor, directing the Automatons via his gauntlet.

**Enemies:**

- 2 Automaton Sentinels (see stats below)
- 4 Automaton Scouts (see stats below)

**Tactical Tips:** The yard is cluttered with scrap metal and partial automatons — PCs can use this for cover or potentially sabotage to slow reinforcements. The iron gate is locked but breaks under sustained attack or via a Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools) check.

**Goal:** Break through the courtyard defenses to enter the manor. Finroth taunts the PCs but doesn’t engage directly yet.

---

## Phase 2: Workshop Ambush — Sabotage and Control

**Setup:** Once inside, PCs enter Finroth’s workshops — a large clockwork-filled room with arcane machines whirring and humming. Finroth activates traps and defensive layout to delay them.

**Enemies:**

- 1 Automaton Sentinel (reinforced model, more HP, see stat changes below)
- 2 Automaton Scouts
- Arcane trap effects (magical pulse damage, binding runes)

**Mechanics:**

- PCs can attempt Intelligence (Arcana) or Dexterity checks to disable traps or sabotage machines.
- Sabotage attempts require one full round action per machine with failure triggering trap effects.
- Finroth can use a lair action to summon 1d4 Automaton Scouts from concealed gates.

**Goal:** Reach Finroth’s inner chamber. PCs can choose to try and disable his control gauntlet on his mechanical throne for a potential edge in Phase 3.

---

## Phase 3: Final Confrontation — Finroth’s Gambit

**Setup:** Finroth awaits in his grand chamber. Two Automaton Sentinels flank him. He is determined to preserve his control over Petalwood and will unleash full power.

**Enemies:**

- Finroth (see detailed stat block below)
- 2 Automaton Sentinels

**Combat and Moral Choice:** Mid-fight, PCs see two important captives held magically bound in protective cages nearby:

- **Elrohir, the Missing Sheriff** (trusted NPC)
- **Two Fey Dignitaries** visiting Petalwood who sought peace between realms

Finroth vows to kill the captives if the PCs don’t surrender control or leave now.

**Moral Quandary:**

- Attempting to free the captives mid-battle will require an action and leave the PCs vulnerable to attack. Doing so will grant a powerful morale boost (+1d6 temporary HP for one round) to each PC but require the party to divide their focus.
- Leaving captives or delaying risks them being executed but allows the PCs to focus fully on Finroth and Automatons.

---

## Stat Blocks Highlights

### Finroth, Mayor-Manipulator (CR 6)

- HP 120, AC 16
- Spellcasting (9th level caster): lightning bolt, hold person, counterspell, greater invisibility
- Special: While active, grants +1 to attack/damage rolls for all Automatons within 60 ft
- Can command Automatons as bonus action
- Legendary Actions: Quick Command (move Automaton), Arcane Shield (gain advantage on saving throw)

> [!NOTE]
> ### Automaton Sentinel (CR 3 each)
> 
> - HP 70, AC 17
> - Melee claw attack: +6 to hit, 2d8+4 slashing + 1d6 lightning damage
> - Multiattack: two claws attacks
> - Shockwave Pulse (Recharge 5-6): 20 ft cone, DC 14 Dex save or take 3d8 lightning damage and be knocked prone

> [!NOTE]
> ### Automaton Scout (CR 1/2)
> 
> - HP 22, AC 15
> - Ranged pulse attack: +4 to hit, 1d8+2 lightning damage
> - Nimble: can disengage as bonus action

## Suggested Narrative/Dialogue for Phase 3 Moral Choice

As the battle rages, Finroth snarls:  
“Fools! You want my empire? Then you’ll burn for it! My prisoners won’t survive your recklessness… Surrender, and perhaps I’ll spare them.”  
The cages imprisoning the Sheriff and Fey dignitaries crackle with magical energy, fragile but deadly. Your moment to decide is fleeting.
