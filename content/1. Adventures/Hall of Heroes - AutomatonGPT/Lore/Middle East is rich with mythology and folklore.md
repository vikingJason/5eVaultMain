## **Creatures from the Middle East**

### 1. **Ifrit (Infernal Flames)**

Inspired by Arabian mythology, Ifrit are powerful fire spirits that dwell in volcanic regions of the Feywild. They are surrounded by flames and radiate an aura of searing heat.

- **Traits:** Immune to fire, cast fire-based spells, and can summon magma eruptions.
- **Lore:** Ifrits were once celestial beings who fell from grace, cursed to forever burn with the flames of their pride.
- **Plot Hook:** An Ifrit offers the players a boon in exchange for extinguishing its eternal flames—if they can survive its fiery domain.

---

### 2. **Simurgh (Eternal Wisdom)**

Inspired by Persian mythology, the Simurgh is a massive, mystical bird with shimmering, rainbow-colored feathers. It is ancient, wise, and deeply connected to the balance of nature.

- **Traits:** Immune to psychic damage, heals allies with its song, and can summon storms with its wings.
- **Lore:** The Simurgh is said to have seen the world’s beginning and holds knowledge of all things.
- **Plot Hook:** The players seek the Simurgh’s wisdom, but it demands a test of their courage and virtue before revealing its secrets.

---

### 3. **Nasnas (Half-Body Fey)**

Based on Arabian folklore, Nasnas are eerie creatures with only half a body: one eye, one arm, one leg, and half a torso. Despite their appearance, they are unnervingly agile and cunning.

- **Traits:** Can leap great distances, cast illusions to disorient foes, and use psychic attacks to manipulate fear.
- **Lore:** Nasnas are created from the broken remnants of failed magical experiments, wandering the Feywild in search of completion.
- **Plot Hook:** A Nasnas approaches the players, offering help in exchange for finding its missing "other half."

---

### 4. **Huma (Phoenix of the Winds)**

Inspired by Persian and Sufi traditions, the Huma is a phoenix-like bird that never lands on the ground, soaring endlessly through the skies of the Feywild.

- **Traits:** Radiates healing magic, can teleport through wind, and is immune to non-magical weapons.
- **Lore:** It is said that seeing the Huma brings great fortune, but capturing it brings calamity.
- **Plot Hook:** A desperate Fey king has captured a Huma, disrupting the balance of the skies. The players must decide whether to free it or exploit its powers.

---

### 5. **Alraune (Living Flowers)**

Inspired by ancient Mesopotamian beliefs in plant spirits, Alraune are humanoid Fey who emerge from enchanted flowers. They are beautiful but deadly, luring intruders into traps with their sweet scent.

- **Traits:** Charm enemies with their fragrance, summon thorny vines to attack, and drain vitality from foes.
- **Lore:** Alraune are believed to be guardians of sacred groves, embodying the Feywild’s floral magic.
- **Plot Hook:** A grove of Alraune has turned hostile, their flowers corrupted by a dark force.

---

## **Myths and Lore from the Middle East**

### 1. **The Garden of Eternal Night**

Inspired by tales of hidden paradises, this Feywild garden is cloaked in perpetual twilight, with glowing plants and shimmering stars overhead.

- **Myth:** It is said that the garden was created by a moon deity as a sanctuary for weary travelers. Those who harm the garden are cursed to wander the Feywild forever.
- **Plot Hook:** The players must retrieve a rare flower from the garden without upsetting its delicate balance.

---

### 2. **The Djinn’s Bargain**

Inspired by Arabian stories of Djinn, these powerful spirits roam the Feywild, offering deals to those brave (or foolish) enough to bargain with them.

- **Myth:** Djinn are neither good nor evil but are bound by their word. However, their deals often have unintended consequences.
- **Plot Hook:** A Djinn has cursed a Fey village after a broken promise. The players must fulfill the original bargain or negotiate a new one.

---

### 3. **The River of Stars**

Inspired by Mesopotamian and Persian cosmology, this Feywild river is made of liquid starlight. It flows through the night sky, carrying fragments of celestial magic.

- **Myth:** Drinking from the River of Stars grants visions of the future but blinds the drinker to the present.
- **Plot Hook:** A dying Fey has stolen starlight from the river, causing the night sky to dim. The players must return it to restore balance.

---

### 4. **The Mirror of Truth**

Inspired by Sufi traditions, the Feywild holds a magical mirror that reveals one’s true self, including hidden desires, fears, and flaws.

- **Myth:** It is said that the mirror was created by a wise sage who wanted to teach mortals the value of self-awareness. However, those who cannot accept their reflection are consumed by the mirror.
- **Plot Hook:** The players must use the mirror to uncover a hidden truth but risk exposing their own secrets.

## **Creatures from the Middle East (Expanded)**

### 1. **Ghilan (Desert Predators)**

Inspired by the Ghoul legends of Arabian folklore, Ghilan are shape-shifting desert predators that lure victims with illusions of water and shelter.

- **Traits:** Immune to exhaustion, can mimic voices, and ambush prey with burrowing attacks.
- **Lore:** Ghilan are cursed Fey who were once greedy mortals, doomed to roam the deserts in search of sustenance.
- **Plot Hook:** A Ghilan has begun hunting travelers along a major Feywild trade route, threatening the flow of goods and information.

---

### 2. **Marid (Oceanic Titans)**

Inspired by the Marid Djinn, these are massive, aquatic Fey with control over tides and storms. They are proud and difficult to bargain with.

- **Traits:** Immune to water-based damage, can summon whirlpools, and use thunderous roars to stun enemies.
- **Lore:** Marids are believed to have been born from the tears of a water goddess. They despise arrogance in mortals.
- **Plot Hook:** A Marid has flooded a Feywild village, demanding an apology from a local leader who insulted it.

---

## **Myths and Lore (Expanded)**

### 1. **The Seven Stars’ Dance**

Inspired by the Pleiades myth from Mesopotamia, the Feywild stars are said to be living beings who perform a celestial dance to maintain balance in the heavens.

- **Myth:** The stars once descended to the Feywild to join a mortal festival, but they were tricked into staying too long and became bound to the sky.
- **Plot Hook:** A star has fallen from the sky, disrupting the celestial dance. The players must guide it back to its place.

---

### 2. **The Flame of Knowledge**

Inspired by Zoroastrian fire-worship traditions, this is an eternal flame that burns in the heart of the Feywild.

- **Myth:** The flame grants great knowledge but only to those who approach it with pure intentions.
- **Plot Hook:** A rival faction seeks to extinguish the flame to gain dominance in the Feywild.

---
