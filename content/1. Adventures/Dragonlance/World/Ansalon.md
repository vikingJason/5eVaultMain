# Overview

Ansalon is the largest and most influential continent on the world of [[Krynn]]. It is a land of ancient kingdoms, legendary heroes, forgotten gods, and looming darkness.

For over three centuries, the peoples of Ansalon have struggled to rebuild following the devastation of the Cataclysm. Nations have risen and fallen, empires have crumbled, and entire cultures have been transformed by the loss of divine guidance.

Now, as the true gods begin to return and whispers of dragons spread across the land once more, Ansalon stands at the edge of a new age.

Many believe peace has finally come.

They are wrong.

# History

## The Age of Dreams

In the earliest days of Krynn, the gods walked closely beside mortals. Great kingdoms rose, magic flourished, and dragons soared through the skies.

Many of the continent's oldest ruins date from this era.

## The Age of Might

The Age of Might saw the rise of powerful nations and heroic figures, including the legendary knight **Vinas Solamnus**, founder of the [[Knights of Solamnia]].

Civilization spread across Ansalon, and the great orders of knighthood, wizardry, and faith took shape.

## The Cataclysm

In response to the arrogance and corruption of the [[Kingpriest of Istar]], the gods unleashed divine judgment upon Krynn.

A fiery mountain struck the world.

Entire nations vanished.

Coastlines shifted.

Millions died.

The event became known as the Cataclysm.

It forever changed Ansalon.

## The Age of Despair

The centuries following the Cataclysm became known as the Age of Despair.

Faith faded.

The true gods vanished from the world.

Old alliances fractured.

Many nations struggled simply to survive.

It is during this age that the events of the War of the Lance begin.

# Geography

Ansalon stretches thousands of miles from frozen tundra to scorching deserts.

Its diverse landscapes include:

- Vast plains
- Ancient forests
- Rugged mountain ranges
- Arid deserts
- Fertile river valleys
- Frozen wastelands

Travel across the continent can take months and often requires crossing hostile territory.

The continent is home to many races and cultures.

The most widespread people on the continent.

### Elves

- Qualinesti
- Kagonesti

### Dwarves

Primarily centered around [[Thorbardin]].

### Kender

Curious wanderers famous for their fearlessness and adventurous nature.

### Gnomes

Inventors and tinkerers centered around Mount Nevermind.

### Minotaurs

Seafaring warriors with their own growing empire.

# Organizations

## [[Knights of Solamnia]]

The greatest knightly order on Krynn.

Their influence remains strong despite centuries of declining prestige.

## [[Mages of High Sorcery]]

Guardians of arcane magic and servants of the three moons.

Their members are respected, feared, and often misunderstood.

## [[Dragon Armies]]

A rapidly growing military force operating throughout eastern Ansalon.

Most people know little about them.

That is beginning to change.
