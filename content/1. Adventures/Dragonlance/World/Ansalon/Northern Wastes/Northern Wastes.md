# Overview

The Northern Wastes are among the most inhospitable lands in all of [[Ansalon]]. Stretching across the frozen and barren northern reaches of the continent, the region is a place of savage beauty, brutal survival, and ancient secrets.

To most civilized nations, the Northern Wastes are little more than a blank spot on the map—a land of nomads, monsters, and endless cold. Yet hidden among its windswept plains and jagged mountains are powerful tribes, forgotten ruins, and the growing heart of a threat that few in the south fully understand.

For it is here that the [[Dragon Armies]] have established their greatest strongholds and where the servants of [[Takhisis]] prepare for war.

---

# History

## Before the Cataclysm

The Northern Wastes were never heavily settled by the great civilizations of Ansalon.

Instead, the region belonged to wandering tribes, ogre kingdoms, and isolated communities that survived through hunting, raiding, and trade.

Ancient ruins scattered throughout the wastes hint at forgotten civilizations that predate recorded history.

## After the Cataclysm

The Cataclysm had little direct impact on the region.

Life was already harsh, and the tribes adapted quickly to the changing world.

As southern kingdoms struggled to recover, the people of the Northern Wastes continued their traditional way of life largely undisturbed.

## Rise of the Dragon Armies

In recent years, powerful forces loyal to [[Takhisis]] have begun uniting tribes and monster clans beneath a single banner.

What once seemed impossible has become reality.

The Dragon Armies now recruit warriors, train soldiers, and gather resources throughout the region.

Many southern leaders remain unaware of the scale of these preparations.

# Culture

The people of the Northern Wastes value:

- Strength
- Endurance
- Courage
- Loyalty to tribe
- Survival above all else

Life in the wastes is unforgiving.

Weak leaders rarely survive long.

Stories are passed through oral traditions, and many tribes maintain ancient customs dating back centuries.

While outsiders often view the tribes as barbarians, most possess complex traditions and strong communal bonds.

---

# Government

There is no centralized government.

Authority rests with:

- Tribal chieftains
- Clan elders
- Warlords
- Religious leaders

Alliances between tribes are temporary and often based upon necessity.

The Dragon Armies have begun changing this by providing a common cause and centralized leadership.

---

# Military

Every adult in the Northern Wastes is expected to contribute to the survival of their community.

Most warriors are skilled in:

- Hunting
- Mounted combat
- Survival
- Ambush tactics
- Wilderness warfare

The region's greatest military strength lies in its people’s ability to endure conditions that would cripple armies from the south.

Dragon Army commanders have successfully leveraged these strengths by recruiting heavily from local populations.

---

# Factions

## Wastelander Tribes

The oldest inhabitants of the region.

Many tribes remain independent.

Others have pledged loyalty to the Dragon Armies.

## Ogre Clans

Several powerful ogre clans dominate portions of the wastes.

Their allegiances shift frequently.

## Goblin and Hobgoblin Tribes

Numerous goblinoid communities inhabit the badlands and mountain regions.

Many have become important recruits for the Dragon Armies.

## [[Dragon Armies]]

The true power behind many recent changes.

The Northern Wastes serve as one of their primary recruiting and staging areas.

---

# Religion

Religious beliefs vary widely between tribes.

Common forms of worship include:

- Ancestor spirits
- Nature spirits
- Tribal totems
- Takhisis
- Sargonnas

Shamans and spiritual leaders often hold considerable influence within their communities.

# Current Events

### Unusual Unity

Traditionally hostile tribes have begun cooperating with one another.

### Increased Recruitment

Dragon Army agents travel openly through parts of the region.

### Southern Rumours

Stories spread of coming war and great dragons returning to Krynn.

### Military Preparations

Large stockpiles of weapons and supplies are being assembled in remote strongholds.

> [!warning] GM Only

### Birthplace of the Dragon Armies

Many of the Dragon Armies' earliest successes were built upon alliances forged in the Northern Wastes.

### Hidden Strongholds

Numerous military camps remain completely unknown to southern nations.

### Ancient Dragon Lore

The wastes contain relics and knowledge connected to dragons that predate the Cataclysm.

The Dragon Armies exert far greater control over the region than most outsiders realize.

Entire tribes now answer directly to Dragon Army commanders.
