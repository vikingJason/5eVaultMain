# Overview

Estwilde lies east of [[Solamnia]] and has long been regarded as one of the most unstable regions of [[Ansalon]]. Once a collection of thriving settlements connected by trade and shared culture, centuries of hardship, political division, and neglect transformed the nation into a patchwork of independent city-states and competing warlords.

Today, Estwilde serves as the heartland of the rising [[Dragon Armies]]. Entire communities have fallen under their control, while others have been destroyed or absorbed into the growing military machine.

To most people in Solamnia, Estwilde is a distant and troubled land. To those who have fled its borders, it is the first battlefield of a war that threatens all of Ansalon.

---

# History

## Before the Cataclysm

Estwilde was once a prosperous agricultural region with strong trade ties to neighboring nations.

Its fertile plains supported numerous settlements and local rulers, though political unity was always difficult to maintain.

## After the Cataclysm

The devastation of the Cataclysm shattered much of the region's infrastructure.

Trade routes collapsed.

Governments failed.

Banditry and warfare became commonplace.

Over the centuries, local rulers and warlords competed for territory while the people endured constant instability.

## Rise of the Dragon Armies

The chaos of Estwilde created the perfect environment for the servants of [[Takhisis]].

The Dragon Armies offered:

- Security
- Food
- Military protection
- Purpose

Many communities joined willingly.

Others were conquered.

By the beginning of the War of the Lance, much of Estwilde had become the foundation upon which the Dragon Armies built their power.

# Geography

Estwilde consists primarily of:

- Open grasslands
- Rolling hills
- Sparse forests
- Isolated settlements
- Ancient ruins

The region's geography makes it ideal for moving large armies across great distances.

Many old roads remain from before the Cataclysm, though most have fallen into disrepair.

---

# Culture

Estwilde lacks a unified cultural identity.

Instead, culture varies significantly between settlements and tribes.

Common traits include:

- Self-reliance
- Pragmatism
- Distrust of outsiders
- Respect for strength
- Adaptability

Years of conflict have hardened many communities.

The average citizen is far more concerned with survival than politics.

---

# Government

There is no true central government.

Authority is held by:

- Local rulers
- Military leaders
- Tribal chiefs
- Dragon Army commanders

Many settlements govern themselves independently, though most are now subject to Dragon Army influence or direct control.

---

# Military

Historically, Estwilde maintained only local militias and mercenary forces.

The arrival of the Dragon Armies transformed the region into a military staging ground.

Military assets include:

- Recruitment camps
- Training grounds
- Supply depots
- Occupied settlements
- Monster auxiliaries

Entire villages have been converted into support networks for the war effort.

---

# Factions

## [[Dragon Armies]]

The dominant power within the region.

Most major settlements now answer directly or indirectly to Dragon Army leadership.

## Local Warlords

Several independent rulers continue to resist outside control.

Some oppose the Dragon Armies.

Others cooperate for personal gain.

## Mercenary Companies

Numerous mercenary bands operate throughout Estwilde.

Loyalty often goes to the highest bidder.

---

# Religion

Faith in Estwilde is fragmented.

Temples are uncommon outside larger settlements.

Commonly worshipped deities include:

- [[Takhisis]]
- [[Sargonnas]]
- [[Hiddukel]]
- [[Chemosh]]

Many communities maintain local traditions and folk beliefs alongside formal religion.
