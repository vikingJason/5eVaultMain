# Overview

Solamnia is one of the oldest and most influential nations on the continent of [[Ansalon]]. Known for its vast fields, fertile farmlands, and traditions of honour and chivalry, it is the homeland of the legendary [[Knights of Solamnia]].

For centuries Solamnia stood as a beacon of order and justice. However, in the years following the Cataclysm, public faith in both the gods and the knighthood diminished. Many citizens now view the Knights as relics of a bygone age, more concerned with tradition than protecting the people.

Despite these growing doubts, Solamnia remains one of the strongest nations in Ansalon. Its strategic location, agricultural wealth, and military traditions make it a critical target for the ambitions of the Dragon Armies.

---

# History

## The Age of Might

Solamnia was founded by the legendary hero **Vinas Solamnus**, whose leadership united much of western Ansalon under a common banner. From his teachings arose the Orders of the Knights of Solamnia and their famous virtues:

- Loyalty
- Courage
- Honour

For centuries the nation prospered under the protection of the Knights.

## The Cataclysm

The fiery mountain that struck the world shattered kingdoms and reshaped entire coastlines. Though Solamnia survived, its influence waned dramatically.

Many blamed the Knights for failing to prevent the disaster, and distrust of both the Orders and the gods spread throughout the land.

## The Present Day

The nation remains stable but politically divided. Rumours from the east speak of armies gathering in [[Estwilde]], refugees fleeing their homes, and dragons sighted once again in the skies.

Most citizens dismiss these stories as exaggerations.

The Knights do not.

# Culture

Solamnic culture places great value on:

- Honour
- Duty
- Family
- Hospitality
- Personal reputation

While not every citizen follows the strict ideals of the Knights, many aspects of daily life still reflect Solamnia's chivalric traditions.

Travellers are generally welcomed, provided they respect local customs.

The nation is particularly famous for:

- Horse breeding
- Military traditions
- Knightly tournaments
- Agricultural festivals

---

# Government

Solamnia is governed through a mixture of local lords, noble families, and knightly influence.

Although the Knights possess tremendous prestige, they do not directly rule the nation.

Political authority is divided among:

- Local governors
- Noble houses
- Military commanders
- Knightly Orders

This system often leads to disagreements over how best to respond to emerging threats.
