# Overview

Khur is a vast and harsh land of deserts, rocky plateaus, and endless horizons located in southeastern [[Ansalon]]. Home to fiercely independent tribes and caravan routes that span the continent, Khur is a realm where survival depends upon strength, adaptability, and loyalty to one's clan.

To outsiders, Khur often appears wild and lawless. In truth, the Khurish people follow ancient traditions and complex social customs that govern every aspect of life.

Though distant from the heartlands of [[Solamnia]], Khur occupies a strategically important position. Its trade routes, warriors, and resources have attracted the attention of the rising [[Dragon Armies]], who seek allies among the tribes.

---

# History

## Ancient Origins

The tribes of Khur trace their ancestry back thousands of years.

Long before the rise of Solamnia, nomadic peoples crossed these deserts, establishing caravan routes and tribal territories that endure to this day.

Many Khurish traditions predate the Cataclysm and preserve oral histories lost elsewhere in Ansalon.

## The Cataclysm

While Khur escaped the worst physical devastation, the collapse of trade networks and neighboring kingdoms brought hardship throughout the region.

Many tribes became increasingly isolated.

Others adapted by controlling the valuable trade routes that survived.

## The Age of Despair

Khur remains largely independent, though internal rivalries frequently erupt into conflict.

The rise of the Dragon Armies threatens to upset the delicate balance between the tribes, as ambitious leaders consider alliances that could reshape the future of the region.

# Geography

Khur is dominated by harsh landscapes:

- Vast deserts
- Rocky mesas
- Dry grasslands
- Hidden oases
- Mountain passes

Water is precious.

Settlements often cluster around reliable wells, rivers, or oasis networks.

Travel through Khur can be dangerous due to:

- Sandstorms
- Extreme temperatures
- Raiders
- Scarcity of supplies

# Culture

The people of Khur value:

- Personal honor
- Hospitality
- Tribal loyalty
- Self-sufficiency
- Courage

A guest offered shelter is traditionally protected by their host, even at great personal cost.

Stories, songs, and oral histories are central to Khurish culture. Many tribal elders serve as both historians and spiritual leaders.

Competition between tribes is common, but outside threats often unite them.

---

# Government

There is no single ruler of Khur.

Instead, authority is divided among numerous tribes and clans.

Powerful chieftains govern their people independently while occasionally gathering in councils to discuss matters affecting the region as a whole.

Political power depends upon:

- Reputation
- Military strength
- Wealth
- Alliances
- Ancestral legitimacy

These shifting alliances make Khur politically unpredictable.

---

# Military

Most tribes maintain skilled warriors accustomed to surviving in the desert.

Their strengths include:

- Mounted combat
- Ambush tactics
- Desert navigation
- Rapid raids
- Guerrilla warfare

While individual tribes may lack the resources of larger kingdoms, they can be formidable opponents within their own territory.

Large-scale military cooperation remains uncommon.

---

# Factions

## Khurish Tribes

The dominant political force in the region.

Each tribe maintains its own traditions, territory, and leadership.

## Merchant Clans

Powerful trading families control caravan routes connecting Khur to the rest of Ansalon.

Their wealth often rivals that of tribal leaders.

## [[Dragon Armies]]

Agents of the Dragon Armies have begun courting tribal leaders, offering wealth, power, and protection.

Some tribes have accepted these offers.

Others remain skeptical.

---

# Religion

Religious practice varies greatly between tribes.

Common faiths include:

- Branchala, god of music and inspiration
- Zeboim, goddess of the sea and storms
- Chislev, goddess of nature

Many tribes also honor:

- Ancestor spirits
- Tribal heroes
- Local sacred sites

Formal temples are rare outside major settlements.
