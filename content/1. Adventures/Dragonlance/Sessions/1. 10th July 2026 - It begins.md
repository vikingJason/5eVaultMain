## 🕒 Key Events
- The party, each drawn by personal connection to [[Ispin Greenshield]], converges on the road to [[Vogler]] for his memorial.
- A terrified young farmer, [[Rhys]], flags them down, begging for help after his supply wagon was ambushed.
- The party investigates the wreckage and engages four scaly, reptilian humanoids who are looting the corpses of [[Knights of Solamnia]].
- A brutal combat ensues; [[Caeden Nia'lo]] is critically wounded and knocked unconscious, while [[Sylvia]] and others are placed in perilous positions.
- The party defeats the creatures, who crumble to stone upon death, releasing a foul, rotting gas.
- The group tends to their wounds, buries the fallen knights by the roadside, and salvages supplies, including a mysterious locked chest.
- The party resolves to continue their journey to [[Vogler]], escorting [[Rhys]] and the recovered goods.

## 🧭 Summary

The road to [[Vogler]] was supposed to be a somber journey of remembrance, but fate had other plans. The party—each drawn by the memory of the gentle soul [[Ispin Greenshield]]—found themselves walking the same path, their shared grief a silent bond. Elara, a young woman still raw with loss, confided that Ispin had been a father figure to her and her brother, while Sylvia and Caeden spoke of the profound, though shorter, impact he had on their lives. The mysterious and withdrawn Silas remained an enigma, offering little of his own connection to the deceased.

Their contemplative mood was shattered by a frantic scream. A young farmer, trembling with terror, came stumbling towards them. His name was [[Rhys]], and he spoke of a nightmare: his supply wagon, carrying provisions for a soldier's camp, had been attacked. The soldiers escorting it lay dead, and strange, reptilian creatures were picking through the wreckage. Without hesitation, the party moved to investigate.

Crouching low and moving through the treeline, they spotted four figures with dark cloaks, scaly wings, and sharp snouts, looting the bodies of the fallen [[Knights of Solamnia]]. The creatures, having caught the party's scent, turned and charged, initiating a vicious skirmish. Silas, ever the archer, loosed a volley of arrows, but his attacks were wild and his form sloppy, resulting in a pulled muscle that enfeebled him. Caeden, the stoic guardian, charged forward, his sword and shield at the ready. He managed to taunt one of the creatures, drawing its ire away from the others, but was soon caught in the crushing jaws of two of them. The damage was immense, and Caeden fell, unconscious and bleeding. The battle raged on, a chaotic melee of splintering wood, flashing steel, and arcane magic. Sylvia’s spells, though potent, often missed their mark in the heat of the fray, and she found herself pinned beneath a falling, petrified foe. Elara’s magic, more subtle, was a lifeline; her mystical tree absorbed a critical blow meant for Caeden, and her healing magic eventually stabilized him, pulling him back from the brink of death.

The tide of battle turned as the party coordinated their efforts. With a final, desperate strike, the last of the creatures fell. As it crumpled to the ground, its body began to turn to stone, releasing a noxious cloud of gas that smelled of rotten eggs. The gas dissipated, and the stone crumbled to dust, leaving only the grim evidence of the fight. With the battle won, the party took stock of their losses. The fallen knights were a sobering sight. At Sylvia’s urging, they took the time to dig shallow graves and bury the soldiers with honor, placing their longswords as makeshift grave markers. They salvaged what they could from the wrecked wagon: eight days of food and water, and a small, intriguing chest, which Silas claimed for safekeeping. The farmer [[Rhys]] was shaken but grateful, and they all pressed on, their pace sobered by the day's events. The journey to [[Vogler]] was no longer just a memorial; it was a testament to the dangers that now stalked the land.
