## 🧭 Summary
The thirty minutes preceding the end of [[Vogler]] were spent in a frantic, suffocating silence. High above the village on the ruins of [[Thornwall Keep]], [[Silas]] looked down at the "Gnome Flinger," the eccentric contraption that had recently hurled him through the sky. He searched for rocks, hoping to turn the tinker’s masterpiece into a weapon of desperation, but the silence of the keep was absolute. The gnomes were gone, and the machine was a dormant giant, too complex for a lone ranger to calibrate in the face of an oncoming storm. 

Down at the village gates, the air tasted of salt and iron. [[Sylvia]] moved with a grim, practiced efficiency, scattering bags of caltrops across the dirt path. Behind her, [[Caeden]] took a whetstone to his gear, his hands steady as he performed a quick [[Repair]] on his battered shield. He knew the timber of the gate wouldn't hold forever; it was merely a screen against the inevitable. [[Elara]], ever the voice for those who could not speak, knelt in the dirt. She reached out to the small creatures of the woods—the birds and the burrowing things she had tended to—and, through [[Atlas]], guided them toward the safety of the docks. Even the animals sensed the shifting of the world.

The peace shattered with the sound of frantic hooves. An [[Ironclad Regiment]] scout appeared on the road, his horse lathered in foam, screaming a warning that the "Dragon Queen’s" reach was already upon them. Before he could reach the threshold, a hooded figure launched itself from the eastern cliffs like a predatory bird. The scout was slammed to the earth, and in the ensuing struggle, a jagged blade found his vitals.

Panic, sharp and cold, seized the gatehouse. [[Silas]] loosed an arrow from the heights, the shaft finding purchase in the attacker’s chest, while [[Sylvia]] hurled a stone from her sling. [[Caeden]], driven by a soldier’s instinct to leave no man behind, charged out through the gate to save the scout. In the chaos, he forgot the very defenses [[Sylvia]] had laid; his boots crunched down onto the sharp metal caltrops. Gritting his teeth against the searing pain and the sudden flow of blood, he dragged the scout's lifeless body back behind the gates just as the shadows on the cliffs multiplied.

[[Becklin Uth-Vaharan]] and [[Cudgel Iron-Smile]] arrived at the gate with the remnants of the militia. The veteran knight looked at the party, her eyes reflecting the orange glow of the rising sun. She ordered them to the docks to oversee the final evacuation, insisting that the [[Ironclad Regiment]] would hold the line. "Ispin would have protected the people," she reminded them, a final command wrapped in the memory of their departed friend. 

The argument was cut short by a sight that defied the logic of the old world. Along the northern cliffs, hundreds of hooded figures stepped into the void. They did not fall. Great, leathery wings unfurled from beneath their cloaks, catching the wind as they glided down into the streets of [[Vogler]]. The invasion was no longer at the gates; it was in the sky, in the alleys, and on the rooftops.

The party retreated toward the city center, finding the village already beginning to burn. Near the market, they encountered a scene of cruel slaughter. Reptilian soldiers—creatures with scales like steel and hissing, bifurcated tongues—were toying with elderly villagers. [[Caeden]] threw himself into the fray, his shield becoming a bulwark for the weak. [[Elara]] unleashed the fury of the storm, her [[Galeblast]] throwing the invaders back, while [[Atlas]] tore at scaled throats with a ferocity that matched his mistress’s resolve.

The combat was a messy, desperate affair. [[Silas]] abandoned his bow for his blade, carving through the reptilian infantry. [[Sylvia]], attempting to provide cover fire, suffered a momentary lapse in the smoke; a stone from her sling went wide, striking poor [[Atlas]] instead of his quarry. Despite the fumbles and the exhaustion, the party, aided by a timely intervention from [[Sergeant Sturm]], managed to put the small band of invaders to the sword.

But there was no time for victory. As the last reptilian soldier fell, a new sound drowned out the crackle of burning thatch. It was a roar that vibrated in the marrow of their bones. From the direction of the wharf, the word was carried on the wind by screaming survivors: *Dragon.*

A massive, black-scaled beast, flanked by the vanguard of the [[Dragon Army]], had reached the water. The escape route was being cut off. The defense of [[Vogler]] had reached its final, most desperate hour.
