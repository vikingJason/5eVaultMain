## 🕒 Key Events
- The party heals seven wounded militia members on High Hill using a combination of Medicine and Natural Medicine.
- Cudgel Iron-Smile captures the mercenary Dayhorn, who is taken to the Brass Crab for questioning.
- Sylvia and Elara interrogate Dayhorn, learning of Gragonis’s mysterious employer and the hoard of gold used for the betrayal.
- The party investigates the mercenary camp, finding Gragonis’s empty gold chest and strange reptilian tracks heading west.

## 🧭 Summary
The metallic tang of blood and the scent of ozone lingered over [[High Hill]] as the dust of the skirmish finally settled. The [[Ironclad Regiment]] had been broken, and the hulking brute [[Gragonis]] lay dead, but the victory felt hollow. [[Caeden]] stood amidst the carnage, his breath coming in ragged hitches, while [[Sylvia]] and [[Elara]] immediately moved toward the fallen. Seven members of the [[Vogler]] militia remained on the field, their bodies broken by the mercenaries' sudden treachery.

[[Sylvia]] worked with practiced, albeit grumbling, efficiency, her healer’s kit open as she stabilized the wounded. Beside her, [[Elara]] bypassed traditional sutures for the wisdom of the wild, her [[Natural Medicine]] allowing her to treat gashes with the same efficacy as a trained surgeon. While they worked, [[Cudgel Iron-Smile]] emerged from the brush, dragging a bruised and bound mercenary by the collar.

The captive, a man named [[Dayhorn]], was brought back to the [[Brass Crab]] under heavy guard. The atmosphere in the village had shifted from festive to funereal. Inside the familiar warmth of the taproom, [[Sylvia]] took the lead in the interrogation. She abandoned her usual acerbic wit for a calculated blend of empathy and threat, holding [[Dayhorn]]’s hand while [[Elara]] loomed behind, casting a subtle spell of [[Fear]] that left the man trembling. 

Under the pressure of [[Sylvia]]'s expert diplomacy and the terrifying prospect of a "mayor’s rope," [[Dayhorn]] broke. He revealed that [[Gragonis]] had been meeting a mysterious benefactor in the woods to the west, returning with enough gold to buy the loyalty of the regiment’s most ruthless members. The plan was a coup: kill [[Cudgel]], take the regiment, and raid [[Vogler]]. He mentioned a lieutenant named [[Jeyev]] who might still be loyal to [[Cudgel]], still out at the camp.

Urgency spurred the party back into the wild. As they raced toward the mercenary camp, [[Elara]] paused briefly at the corpse of [[Gragonis]]. She discovered a red silk coin pouch emblazoned with a disturbing spiral symbol—a sigil [[Sylvia]] recognized as being tied to the ancient, malevolent gods of [[Krynn]]. Near the body, they found a second set of tracks: peculiar, reptilian prints that headed west, distinct from the human boots of the mercenaries.

The mercenary camp was a ghost town. They found [[Gragonis]]’s personal tent, which contained a smashed-open chest. Inside, strangely, were several oversized dresses—a bizarre detail that offered more questions than answers. The gold was gone. Following a fresh trail of hoofprints, the party tracked the movements back toward [[Vogler]], fearing the worst.

They arrived back at the [[Brass Crab]] to find [[Cudgel]] being tended to by the healer [[Yalm]]. Moments later, the heavy rhythm of hooves announced the arrival of [[Jeyev]] and a handful of loyalists. [[Jeyev]] looked as though he had seen a ghost. He reported that while scouting the valley to the west, he had found an army. Not a raiding party, but a professional force of over a thousand soldiers under red banners. He spoke of hooded figures with wings and a sea of red tents.

The revelation sparked a heated debate within the village council. [[Mayor Raven Uthvoglar]] and [[Becklin Uth-Vaharan]] were joined by the pompous [[Lord Bakaris]], who immediately suggested using the town’s coffers to buy their way out of the conflict. He viewed the encroaching army as a business negotiation, oblivious to the existential threat they posed. 

The debate was silenced by a frantic villager. A messenger had arrived at the gates.

The party moved to the entrance of [[Vogler]], positioning themselves protectively around [[Mayor Raven]]. Standing beneath the archway was a rider clad in black and red scale mail, his armor bearing the same spiral symbol found on [[Gragonis]]’s pouch. He offered no greeting, only a rolled parchment. 

The letter was brief and final. It demanded the immediate surrender of [[Vogler]] to the forces of the "Dragon Queen." The message ended with three chilling words: *Refuse and die.*
