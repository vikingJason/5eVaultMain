## 🕒 Key Events
- The party engages the "Steel Dragon" siege engine at the Vogler wharf.
- Caeden forces open the rear hatch of the machine, revealing a Draconian crew.
- A frantic combat occurs inside the machine; Silas snipes the crew while Caeden fights in the hold.
- The Steel Dragon malfunctions and explodes, knocking Caeden unconscious and inflicting heavy damage on the wharf.
- A black-scaled Ogre commander and Draconian reinforcements arrive to cut off the evacuation.

## 🧭 Summary
The wharf of [[Vogler]] was a choking tapestry of black smoke and the copper tang of blood. High above, the sun struggled to pierce the haze, but the true heat came from the shoreline. What the party had initially mistaken for a living dragon—a nightmare of black scales and jagged wings—revealed its true, horrific nature as it drew closer. It was a machine of war, a "Steel Dragon" siege engine that clanked with the rhythmic thrum of a furnace and hissed with pressurized steam.

[[Caeden]] didn't hesitate. While the rest of the party reeled from the sight of the metallic monstrosity, the soldier charged. He crossed the thirty feet of salt-slicked timber in a blur of iron and resolve, raising his shield as the machine’s maw opened. A torrent of flame erupted, a sixty-foot cone of liquid fire that turned the docks into a kiln. [[Sylvia]] was caught in the periphery, the heat stealing the air from her lungs as she collapsed, while [[Atlas]] howled in the heat.

From the heights, [[Silas]] loosed arrows that skittered off the machine’s plating, but [[Elara]] found a different weakness. She summoned an [[Electric Arc]], the lightning dancing across the iron hide of the beast. The conductivity of the machine proved to be its undoing; the bolt surged through the frame, causing the internal mechanisms to screech in protest.

Recognizing the thing for a vessel rather than a creature, [[Caeden]] circled to the rear. He discovered a circular hatch, a pressure valve meant to regulate the furnace within. With a Herculean effort—an exhibition of raw strength that bordered on the supernatural—he wrenched the handle. The hatch buckled and flew open, revealing the cramped, sweltering interior. Inside, four reptilian soldiers—[[Draconians]]—manned the joysticks and bellows.

The battle turned into a frantic, internal slaughter. [[Silas]] moved to a vantage point, sniping through the open hatch with surgical precision, his arrows finding the soft tissue between scaled joints. Inside, [[Caeden]] hacked at the crew, his blade catching the orange glow of the furnace. But the [[Draconians]] fought with the desperation of cornered rats. A jagged blade found a gap in [[Caeden]]'s armor, sending him into the darkness of unconsciousness.

[[Sylvia]], revived by a potion from [[Silas]], limped toward the fray. She saw the machine beginning to shudder; the furnace was glowing white-hot, and the metallic shrieks were now deafening. She dragged [[Caeden]]’s limp body away just as the Steel Dragon reached its breaking point. The explosion was a symphony of shrapnel and fire. The wharf groaned under the force, and the machine disintegrated, taking its crew and the last of [[Caeden]]’s strength with it. He hit the wood, dying and broken, for the second time that morning.

As the smoke cleared, the peace was momentary. From the ruins of a nearby inn, a ten-foot brute in black scale armor emerged, gorging himself on a fistful of wriggling fish before discarding them with a roar. This ogre-commander, flanked by fresh [[Draconians]], saw the battered party and the escaping villagers. 

The defense of the docks became a messy, tactical scramble. [[Elara]] poured the last of her primal magic into [[Caeden]], calling him back from the threshold of death. [[Silas]], realizing the ogre’s massive reach was their greatest threat, sprinted forward with an unconventional gambit. In a blur of movement, he used a "Dirty Trick" to tie the brute’s own bootlaces together. The ogre, confused and clumsy, stumbled, allowing [[Caeden]] to sweep its legs and send the giant crashing to the pier.

The tide turned on the docks. [[Sylvia]], standing between the invaders and the last boat, caught a [[Draconian]] off-balance. With a desperate shove, she sent the creature plummeting into the churning river water. [[Silas]] and [[Caeden]] coordinated their strikes, the ranger’s arrows softening the ogre before the soldier delivered a crushing blow to the fallen commander.

The last of the villagers were boarding. [[Mayor Raven Uthvoglar]] and [[Darret Highwater]] stood on the gunwales, shouting for the party to move. [[Atlas]] was already safe on the deck. As the party prepared to leap for the boat, a new figure appeared through the smog—lathered in sweat and grime, wearing the colors of the [[Ironclad Regiment]]. It was [[Jeyev]], the lieutenant they had feared lost. He clutched his helmet, his eyes wide with the news of what had happened at the gates.

The village of [[Vogler]] was gone, consumed by fire and iron, but the survivors had a single boat and a long river journey ahead to the safety of [[Kalaman]].
