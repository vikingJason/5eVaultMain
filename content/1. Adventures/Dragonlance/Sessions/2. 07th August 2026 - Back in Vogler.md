## 🕒 Key Events
- The party arrives in Vogler and scouts the village, learning of lizardfolk sightings from local fishermen.
- The group meets Becklin Uth-Vaharan and Cudgel Iron-Smile at the Brass Crab.
- Ispin Greenshield’s funeral is held at the docks; his body is sent down the Vingard River.
- The wake commences at the Brass Crab with the party sharing stories of Ispin’s life.
- Bakaris the Younger heckles the mourners and is magically charmed by Sylvia into compliance.

## 🧭 Summary
The scent of the [[Vingard River]]—a thick, brackish mix of mud and scales—greeted the party long before the village of [[Vogler]] came into view. For [[Elara]], [[Sylvia]], [[Caeden]], and [[Silas]], the journey had been marked by blood and the strange, stoney remains of reptilian attackers, but as they descended the wooded cliffs, the violence of the road felt worlds away. [[Vogler]] was a town built on the bones of the past. An incomplete stone bridge, a relic of the age before the Cataclysm, jutted into the water like a broken finger, while the modest wooden homes of the village clustered around a central green.

The mood in town was jarringly festive. The annual [[Kingfisher Festival]] was underway, celebrating a historic victory of the [[Knights of Solamnia]]. While the party carried the weight of [[Ispin Greenshield]]’s death, the locals were busy preparing for games and reenactments. Upon arrival, the group split to find their bearings. [[Sylvia]], ever the social firebrand, immediately tracked down [[Sergeant Sturm]], a local militia leader who seemed more interested in preening for her than discussing the "dragon people" the party had encountered on the road. Her brother, [[Caeden]], took a more practical approach, wandering the docks to speak with a fisherman named John. From him, [[Caeden]] learned a sobering truth: the "lizardfolk" the party fought weren't an isolated incident. Cloaked raiders had been sighted along the river, though the village leadership seemed content to ignore them in favor of the festival.

Meanwhile, [[Elara]] sought solace in the greenery of the village, visiting a garden she had once tended with Ispin. The memory of her mentor hung heavy in the air, a silent reminder of why they had come. [[Silas]], typically withdrawn, drifted through the village circle, observing the common folk as they gathered under the ancient trees.

As the sun climbed, the party reunited at the [[Brass Crab]], the village’s premier inn and Ispin’s favorite haunt. The doors were initially closed for preparations, but once they opened, the atmosphere shifted to one of somber remembrance. They were met by [[Becklin Uth-Vaharan]], the Castellan of [[Thornwall Keep]], and her companion [[Cudgel Iron-Smile]], the formidable leader of the [[Ironclad Regiment]] mercenary company. Both women had adventured with Ispin in their youth and spoke of him with a fierce, nostalgic love. [[Becklin]], clad in the ornate armor of a Solamnic Knight, lamented the growing distrust the common folk held for her order—a bitterness that had lingered since the Cataclysm.

The funeral itself was held on the docks. Ispin’s body, wrapped in a simple shroud, was placed in a small boat. There were no grand orations, only the soft music of a lone performer and the gentle lap of the river. [[Becklin]] echoed Ispin’s own wishes: "Tales, not tears." As the boat drifted down the [[Vingard River]] and out of sight, a heavy silence fell over the gathered crowd, broken only by the invitation to return to the inn to share stories.

The wake at the [[Brass Crab]] was a raucous affair, exactly as Ispin would have wanted. [[Becklin]] began with a tale of a gnome’s pet weasel and a disastrously aimed artillery launch. [[Caeden]] took his turn, though the ale had begun to muddle his tongue. He recounted a confusing encounter with a "blue food befuddled old man" who disappeared before dawn—a story that, despite his clumsy delivery, drew warm laughs from the crowd. [[Sylvia]] followed with a much more successful performance, vividly describing a sea battle where a chamber pot was catapulted into an enemy captain’s quarters to end a chase. Even [[Elara]] spoke, her voice thick with emotion as she credited Ispin for the life she and her brother now led.

However, the warmth of the evening was interrupted by [[Bakaris]], a wealthy and arrogant local who made a point of loudly complaining about the "boring" stories and the quality of the beer. Sensing the mood soured, [[Sylvia]] decided to intervene. With a subtle weave of arcane power, she cast a charm on the nobleman. The effect was instantaneous. [[Bakaris]]’s sneer vanished, replaced by a dreamy, helpful expression. He suddenly found [[Sylvia]]’s presence enchanting and, at her soft suggestion, decided that perhaps he should take his complaints elsewhere, leaving the party in peace.

As the evening wound down, [[Sergeant Sturm]] and his daughter [[Tika]] joined the table. The talk turned back to the "lizardfolk" raiders. [[Sturm]] remained dismissive, viewing them as mere bandits not worth disrupting the festival for, but the party knew better. These were no ordinary brigands.

Before they could retire, [[Elara]] was approached by [[Darret Highwater]], [[Becklin]]’s young and somewhat disorganized squire. Amidst a flurry of apologies and confusion over a sack of fish pies, [[Darret]] delivered a message: [[Becklin]] had a final gift from [[Ispin Greenshield]] to give to them, but it would have to wait until the morning. With the promise of a mysterious inheritance and the impending [[Battle of High Hill]] reenactment tomorrow, the party finally sought sleep, the shadows of the coming war looming just beyond the village walls.
