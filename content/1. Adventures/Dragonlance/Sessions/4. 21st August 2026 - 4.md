## 🧭 Summary
The morning’s festive air, once light with the scent of pine and river water, grew heavy as the village bell tolled across [[Vogler]]. It was a sound that signaled the transition from games to history, calling the local militia to gather for the annual reenactment of the [[Battle of High Hill]]. 

[[Caeden]] stood among the ranks, the weight of [[Ispin Greenshield]]’s green relic heavy on his arm. Beside him, [[Sylvia]] attempted to maintain her composure despite the lingering effects of the morning’s celebratory drinking, while [[Elara]] remained vigilant, her eyes scanning the treeline as if expecting the shadows to move. [[Silas]], ever the pragmatist, had initially sought to remain a spectator, though the promise of gold from [[Mayor Raven Uthvoglar]] to act as a "referee"—ensuring the mock battle remained bloodless—eventually drew him toward the vantage points overlooking the slope.

The march to [[High Hill]] was a study in contrasts. The [[Vogler]] militia was a ragtag assembly of fishermen and farmers in mismatched leather and crooked helmets, led by the boisterous [[Sergeant Sturm]] and his daughter [[Tika]]. Waiting for them at the base of the hill were the mercenaries of the [[Ironclad Regiment]]. Unlike the villagers, these were professionals; their armor was polished, their formations tight, and their presence radiated a cold, practiced efficiency.

As the sun hit its zenith, the signal was given. A trumpet blast cut through the air, and the two forces charged. It was meant to be a display of tradition—wooden swords clashing against wooden shields. But as the lines met, the sound was wrong. Instead of the dull thud of timber, the air was pierced by the sharp, rhythmic ring of steel on steel.

The pretense of the festival shattered in a heartbeat. A ribbon of red splashed across the green grass as a militia member fell, his chest opened by a mercenary’s blade. The "Astarian rats" weren't playing their part; they were hunting.

Chaos erupted. [[Caeden]] reacted with a soldier’s instinct, drawing his real longsword as he realized the mercenaries had swapped their practice gear for lethal steel. [[Sylvia]]’s intoxication vanished, replaced by the cold surge of arcane power as she threw aside her wooden props. However, the mercenaries were prepared. A crossbow bolt, fired with deadly precision, caught [[Sylvia]] squarely, nearly ending her life where she stood. She collapsed into the grass, blood pooling beneath her robes.

[[Elara]] screamed her sister-in-law’s name, her magic manifesting as a defensive wall. She called upon the earth, summoning a [[Protector Tree]] to sprout instantly before the wounded sorceress, its branches interposing themselves against the incoming fire. Beside her, the dog [[Atlas]] snarled, his fur bristling as he guarded the flank.

From the distant vantage point, [[Silas]] saw the carnage and began a desperate sprint. He was hundreds of feet away, his lungs burning as he raced toward the treeline, his bow ready. He arrived just as the tide seemed to turn for the worse.

The mercenaries weren't alone. A massive shadow detached itself from the wagon line—[[Gragonis]], a hulking half-ogre with a greatsword that looked like a jagged slab of iron. He moved with a terrifying, lumbering grace, his eyes fixed on [[Darret Highwater]], who was struggling to rally the terrified survivors.

"Taste my axe!" the mercenary captain roared, swinging at [[Caeden]]. The Guardian met the blow with Ispin’s shield, the impact rattling his teeth, but he held the line. [[Caeden]] taunted the brute, drawing his ire away from the vulnerable [[Elara]].

The battle became a desperate struggle for survival. [[Elara]] unleashed a [[Galeblast]], the localized cyclone buffeting the mercenaries, while [[Atlas]] darted in to tear at the hamstrings of the attackers. [[Silas]], finally reaching the edge of the fray, let loose a volley of arrows with a [[Hunted Shot]], his shafts finding the gaps in the mercenaries' armor.

The climax came when [[Gragonis]] lunged for [[Atlas]], his massive blade nearly cleaving the loyal hound in two. The tree summoned by [[Elara]] took the brunt of the blow, splinters flying like shrapnel. Seeing his opening, [[Caeden]] stepped into the half-ogre’s guard. With a roar that echoed the ancient Solamnic heroes, [[Caeden]] drove his blade home in a devastating strike, a flourish of steel that ended the brute’s reign of terror.

With their leader dead and the party's combined might turning against them, the remaining mercenaries broke. They fled toward the west, disappearing into the woods and leaving behind a battlefield littered with the wounded and the dead. 

As the adrenaline faded, the grim reality set in. This was no localized skirmish. [[Darret]], shaken and bloodied, looked toward the village. The smoke rising from the distance told the true story: [[Vogler]] was under siege.
