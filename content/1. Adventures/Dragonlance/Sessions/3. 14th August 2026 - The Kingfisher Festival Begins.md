## 🕒 Key Events
- The party reunites and visits Thornwall Keep to meet Becklin and Darret.
- Caeden receives Ispin Greenshield’s legacy—a round green shield—from Becklin.

## 🧭 Summary
The morning mist clung to the [[Vingard River]] like a damp shroud, blurring the lines between the water and the wooden docks of [[Vogler]]. Inside the [[Brass Crab]], the air was thick with the scent of stale ale and the lingering warmth of the previous night’s wake. [[Caeden]] was the first to stir, his soldier’s discipline pushing him out of bed and toward the door of his sister, [[Sylvia]]. His rhythmic pounding on the wood was met with a groan that spoke of a deep desire for five more minutes of oblivion. Despite her protests, the promise of tea eventually coaxed the sorceress into the waking world, though her mood remained as stormy as a mountain squall until the caffeine took hold.

Meanwhile, [[Elara]] spent her morning in the quiet company of the earth, tending to the small animals in the wooded area she had claimed as her sanctuary. For her, the festival felt like a thin veil over a growing darkness, but she found peace in the routine of nature. [[Silas]], ever the wanderer, drifted through the village center as the town began to buzz with the energy of the [[Kingfisher Festival]].

The party eventually converged on the road leading to [[Thornwall Keep]]. The ancient three-story stone tower loomed over the village, its walls choked with bronze-colored ivy. At the gates, they were met by [[Darret Highwater]], [[Becklin Uth-Vaharan]]'s squire. The young man, who only the night before had been a bumbling mess of fish pies and apologies, stood with a new sense of purpose. He led them into a grand hall where a long rectangular table was set with silverware and multiple brass candlesticks. After a brief breakfast—which included more of [[Darret]]'s ubiquitous fish pies—[[Becklin]] joined them, her expression a mix of somber duty and nostalgic warmth.

She presented them with a weathered wooden box. Inside lay a round green shield, a relic of [[Ispin Greenshield]]. It was a heavy thing, laden with history. [[Becklin]] spoke of Ispin’s peculiar tradition of "dying" in the most ridiculous ways during the festival reenactments. She offered the shield to [[Caeden]], who accepted it with a silent, stoic vow to honor his mentor’s legacy. 

As the sun climbed higher, the group descended back into [[Vogler]] to the sound of trumpets and cheers. [[Mayor Raven Uthvoglar]], a woman who commanded respect with every step, took the stage in the village circle. She spoke of courage, family, and the ancestors who had built [[Vogler]] from the ruins of the Cataclysm. With her blessing, the [[Kingfisher Festival]] was officially underway.

The party followed the crowd toward the wharf for the annual fishing competition. [[Silas]] and [[Caeden]] took their places on the docks, lines cast into the murky depths of the [[Vingard River]]. While [[Caeden]] struggled with the tug of the current, [[Silas]] felt a pull that nearly dragged him into the water. The crowd went silent as a massive, eight-foot-long carp broke the surface—[[Bennebog the Linebreaker]], a creature of legend. With a display of skill that left the locals gasping, [[Silas]] hauled the beast in. However, rather than claiming the trophy for the cook-fire, [[Silas]] chose to release the legendary fish back into the river. The act of mercy split the crowd; some cheered his nobility, while others lamented the lost feast. Despite the release, [[Silas]]’s haul over the subsequent rounds secured him first place, earning him a gold-plated trophy and a masterfully crafted fishing pole.

The victory led the group back to the [[Brass Crab]], where they encountered [[Joran]], a local fisherman who seemed to be more ale than man. He insisted on sharing a "fine drop"—a thick, viscous liquid that turned out to be fermented fish-egg liqueur mixed with whiskey. [[Caeden]], ever the diplomat, downed the sludge with a straight face, while [[Sylvia]] fought the urge to gag. 

While the others socialized, [[Elara]]’s keen eyes were drawn to a group of mercenaries from the [[Ironclad Regiment]]. She noticed [[Cudgel Iron-Smile]] and [[Becklin]] sharing a laugh, but her attention shifted to the rank-and-file soldiers. Slipping closer to their conversation, she overheard unsettling rumors. The mercenaries spoke of massive recruitment drives in the east, promising wealth and glory that hadn't been seen since before the Cataclysm. The tone was one of professional opportunism, but to [[Elara]], it sounded like the gathering of a storm.

The festive atmosphere was suddenly pierced by the deep, resonant tolling of the village’s large bell. The sound echoed off the cliffs and across the river, signaling the end of the morning’s games. It was the call to arms for the militia. The reenactment of the [[Battle of High Hill]] was about to begin, and the party, now carrying the weight of Ispin’s shield and the whispers of war, moved toward the village square to take their places in the coming "battle."
