## Overall Flow

This arrangement gives the chapter a natural rhythm:

1. **Arrival** – The party reunites with family, mentors, and friends in their familiar locations.
    
2. **Festival** – NPCs mingle, compete, tease one another, and reinforce existing relationships.
    
3. **Preparation** – Becklin and Sturm quietly involve trusted allies (including the PCs) in growing concerns.
    
4. **Invasion** – Every NPC is already somewhere the players expect them to be, making rescue decisions and emotional moments feel earned rather than scripted.
