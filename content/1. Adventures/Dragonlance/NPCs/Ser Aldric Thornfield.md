# Overview

Ser Aldric Thornfield serves as a veteran scout attached to the Knights of Solamnia operating along the eastern frontier.

Though not a noble knight himself, Aldric has earned respect through decades of service and possesses extensive knowledge of the roads, forests, and borderlands surrounding Vogler.

Recently he has become increasingly concerned about reports arriving from the east.

---

# Appearance

A broad-shouldered man with weathered skin and prematurely grey hair.

His armour is practical rather than ornate, showing signs of regular repair and use.

A faded blue cloak bearing the insignia of the Knights of Solamnia hangs from his shoulders.

A long scar crosses his left cheek.

---

# Personality

Aldric is cautious, dependable, and patient.

He prefers observation over action and often notices details others miss.

While loyal to the Knights, he is pragmatic enough to question orders that place civilians at risk.

---

# Goals

- Protect Vogler from outside threats.
- Gather intelligence on eastern military movements.
- Prove the Dragon Army threat is real.
- Prevent unnecessary bloodshed.

# GM Notes

Aldric can serve as an early warning system for the campaign.

Whenever the party needs information about:

- Estwilde
- Military movements
- Border activity
- Dragon Army scouts

Aldric is a reliable source.

He should survive the opening chapters if possible, allowing him to become a recurring ally throughout the campaign.
