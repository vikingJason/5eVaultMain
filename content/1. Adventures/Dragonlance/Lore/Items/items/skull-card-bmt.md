# Skull Card
*Wondrous item, legendary*  
![](content/1.%20Adventures/Dragonlance/Lore/Items/items/img/19-skull.webp#right)


As an action, you can brandish this card and use it to cast [Spirit of Death](CLI/spells/spirit-of-death-bmt.md) (see "chapter 7") as a 6th-level spell (spell attack bonus `+9`, save DC 17). Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 15*
