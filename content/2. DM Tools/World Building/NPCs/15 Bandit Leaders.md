
### Dru
Dru is an ex-preacher who has gone bad (or good, depending on your viewpoint). After watching rich people bribe their way to forgiveness and redemption while punishing the peasants for the same transgressions, Dru decided he needed to balance the tables. He gathered a band of followers willing to commit crimes to bring justice to an unjust situation (and keep a bit for themselves). They only target the coffers of religious institutions and wealthy religious leaders and never murder (unless it is necessary). Dru gives his crew twenty-five percent of the take to split amongst themselves and returns the other seventy-five percent to the local peasants and downtrodden. Dru still believes in the divine teachings and preaches whenever he can to try to save the souls of the truly deserving.

### Faznann
Faznann grew up in the slums and had a childhood accident that changed him. Like was hard and rough for everyone in the slums, but Faznazz had a streak of meanness in him. He would happily attack someone who looked at him wrong, even one of his loyal followers. His intensity was such that he collected several loyal followers who would follow him anywhere. Tired of the slums, he and his crew set off into the world to make their mark by terrorizing and robbing farms, small settlements, and caravans. His philosophy is that no one is ever left alive to tell of their attack. The only message he shares with those who discover the atrocity is the letter F carved into the chest of each of the deceased.

### AbbieGail
AbbieGail is the wife of a minor noble. She finds her public life stifling. Fortunately, he husband travels constantly, and she has used her free time constructively, training with various unsavory characters to develop a unique set of skills. Each encounter with these folks spurred her to even greater depths of depravity. She would do anything to feel alive for even a few moments. As her skills grew, she started collecting a small group of folks who also liked danger. She used her knowledge of what was happening in the region to plan daring robberies against the crown. She secretly smiles whenever she sees a wanted poster for her and her gang.

### Tenny
Tenny runs a tinkerer shop in town and is respected by the community as the go-to kobold for repairs. He lives on a prosperous small farm on the outskirts of town, run by a few family members and strong-looking “hirelings.” Tenny builds various gadgets in one of the barns on the property to help with his side job. Using the knowledge he gleans from loose lips in his tinkerer shop, he and his gang of “hirelings” set up daring robberies using his unusual inventions to make the robberies bloodless.

### Pru
Pru grew up in a martial family and even served as a member of the town guard for a few years. Unfortunately, her face became disfigured when trying to stop a crime. She felt like her life was destroyed and hid from the public for quite a while. Finally, she started going out wearing a veil to hide the scars and, while out one day, followed an urchin out of curiosity. The urchin led her to a local thief. He was also scarred, and they quickly formed a bond and then a gang of similarly
disfigured scofflaws. They became the Gilded Mask gang, and due to her martial background, Pru became the leader and chief tactician.

### Krantz
Krantz was a lead miner of a now closed iron mine. He learned that the mine was quietly sold to a competitor so that entity could reduce supply and charge more for iron. He discussed this injustice with his men and they decided to step up and do something about it. Under his leadership, they formed a strike team to sabotage the competitor’s operations and rob the owners. A bit of thievery, some sabotage, and a slice of revenge kept this gang of hardened men busy for many months. They were so effective that the owner sold all his operations and moved away with his tail between his legs.

### Warnec
Captain Warnac and his team of thirty elite soldiers were abandoned and left for dead when a mission failed. Working together, they fought their way to freedom. Many died. Those who survived formed a pact to take revenge against the government and military that abandoned them. Using their skills in infiltration and swift strikes, they attacked military supply lines and kidnapped and ransomed royals for many years. Every time they attacked, they left behind their calling card – a drawing of the royal crest with a skull and crossbones written across it. They are moving closer and closer to the kingdom’s heart, and those in power fear an attack against the crown will soon occur.

### Sybil
This gypsy maiden was born with the gift of future sight. She used its powers to twist her extended family to her will, which was relatively easy considering everyone already had a somewhat larcenous heart. She used her gift to plan many audacious robberies and escapes. She keeps most of the money from the robberies for a grand project. This apparent greed on her part creates friction between her and her family as they are greedy and want more. Thus far, she has convinced them the project is critical to their survival as a family, but it gets more difficult with each month that passes.

### Big Josh
Big Josh is a mountain of a man. He stands almost seven feet high and is a three-hundred-fifty-pound slab of slow-witted muscle. His first robbery was accidental, stumbling out of the woods in front of a merchant traveling down the road. The merchant was so afraid that he jumped off his wagon and ran away. It was so easy that he did it a few more times. Pretty soon, he caught the attention of a two-bit gang that approached him and offered to make him their leader so they could go after bigger targets. It worked well until Big Josh finally realized they were keeping most of the loot. He killed them all in a rage and ran off into the woods, a loner again.

### Karana
Karana is the daughter of the now-dead leader of a local gang of bandits. She is currently in a power struggle over the gang’s leadership against her childhood friend, Alphase. The entire gang voted and decided a contest would be the best way to determine the new leader. Everyone’s name was tossed into a hat, and Karana and Alphase took their names out in turns to build their teams. The rules
are simple. The team leader who collects the most loot in thirty days becomes the new gang leader.

### Olivia O’Hare
Olivia used to be a member of a traveling circus and ran a fortune telling con game on the visitors. It was profitable, but after a while, it became hard to remember all the lies as the circus revisited towns and cities, so she quit seeking easier pickings. While chatting with an entrepreneurial sort at a bar, they devised a simple yet elegant plan and formed a partnership. She would open a fortune telling shop and use her wiles to gather information from wealthy patrons that they could then use to rob them. The partners robbed the rich just enough times to keep suspicion from her business and the racket running.

### Mimsie
Always large for her age, Mimsie’s tomboyish ways led her to a career in the military. After serving for ten years and collecting an assortment of wounds, she decided it was time to retire from the military and use her skills to secure a comfortable life. She convinced a few of her fellow soldiers to join her as mercenaries. As a band, they were quite effective and secured many jobs guarding wealthy patrons. The pay was much better than the military, and the fringe benefit of being able to pick up some odds and ends from past customers was even more profitable. Using their legitimate business, they could easily launder their illicit income streams.

### K’See Har’view
Sometimes life does not live up to expectations. K’See was raised in a religious order and trained to become a prophet. When she was ten years old, the order decided she would never become a prophet and threw her into the streets. Using the skills they taught her, she became an effective beggar and was soon the leader of a band of street urchins. With her collection of street informants and spies, she built an information empire – selling her juicy tidbits to members of the town’s seedy underbelly for a cut of the spoils. By the time she was sixteen, she had bought a property on the edge of the poorest part of town and used it as her home and business headquarters. She chose the property because of the extensive tunnel network connected to its cellars.

### Raz’la
Raz’la is no stranger to crime. He was the first mate on a pirate ship that unfortunately met its demise while he was on shore, paying some bribes for some profitable shipping manifests. Using his remaining funds, he booked passage on a freighter to a big city where he could get lost in its slums. Over a few years, he learned to ride a horse and build a crew of loyal cutthroats who joined him in his quest to become the most successful land pirates in the land.

### Jaella
Jaella is a private tutor by day and the leader of a daring band of second-story thieves by night. As a tutor, she has been in the homes of most of the wealthy in this city. Her photographic memory has allowed her to amass a massive list of home layouts, valuables, and more. One day, her brother got in trouble with some seedy folks, and she needed quick money to save him. She worked a deal with the group, and they successfully robbed one of her prior customers. The arrangement worked so smoothly, and the pay was so good that they did it again and again. Jaella’s power in the group grew along with the successful robberies. Before long, she was planning the jobs and calling the shots.
