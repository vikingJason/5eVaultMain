# Best Monsters To Use For A Survival Campaign In DND

---
## Dungeons & Dragons: Best Monsters For A Survival Campaign

![[tomb_of_horrors.png]]

Survival-based campaigns can be some of the most fun you'll ever have in a Dungeons & Dragons adventure. For high-level players who may feel like they've seen it all before, testing their limits as a DM by forcing them to consider rations, water supply, illness, and more can bring an intriguing dose of reality into any campaign.


But, what's a survival campaign without monsters that test those same limits? If you plan on running a survival-based adventure that pushes your players to their absolute brink, consider using these monsters to keep the sense of danger alive and present throughout the campaign.

## 10 Larvae


![[larvae.png]]

| Stat  | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Fiend |
| HP | 9-22 |
| CR | 0-1 |

Larvae are great for a survival-based campaign as they evoke a sense of physical disgust and grotesqueness that is essential for capturing the mood of an adventure like this. You can either use a single Larva for lower-level parties or multiple swarms of Larvae (utilizing mob combat rules) for higher-levels of play.

## 9 Lich


| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Undead |
| HP | 315 |
| CR | 21 |

Liches are extremely powerful mages that can lay waste to even some of the most daring adventuring parties around. They consume souls and use them to feed their dark magics. If you're looking for a climactic boss battle for your survival campaign, consider using a Lich.

The reason Liches are great for these kinds of campaigns is that they have access to spells like Finger of Death, and Disrupt Life that can absolutely decimate mid to high-level parties, forcing players to think on their feet and perhaps even seek help post-battle.

## 8 Myconids

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Plant |
| HP | 3-45 |
| CR | 0-2 |

Myconids are, in essence, fantastical fungi that players can stumble across in dense forest environments. Fungi of any kind are great for a survival-based campaign, as you can tailor the abilities of these spores to evoke undeath or plague.

Plus, the 2025 Monster Manual has plenty of different options for Myconids that can be adjusted for any party level. They're probably best suited for lower levels though, unless you add in a lot of them. Look at Myconid Spore Servants for higher tiers of play.

## 7 Yetis

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Monstrostiy |
| HP | 51-137 |
| CR | 3-9 |

Yetis can make great boss battles for survival adventures if you're in the right type of biome. If your players are exploring a frostbitten mountain or tundra, consider using a Yeti to serve as the climax for their current arc. These creatures can be tailored for mid to high levels of play.

Though powerful, Yetis also have a critical weakness that players can exploit, which makes for fun roleplay in combat. If a Yeti takes fire damage, it triggers its Fear of Fire trait, which means they have disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.

## 6 Carrion Crawler

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Monstrosity |
| HP | 51 |
| CR | 2 |

If your campaign is set in a post-apocalyptic environment, Carrion Crawlers are great for setting tone and creating atmosphere. Have players see these creatures consuming corpses, knowing that if they fall to one of these creatures, their fate will likely be the same.

## 5 Blights

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Plant |
| HP | 7-264 |
| CR | 1/8-16 |

Plant blights are a great addition to any campaign that takes place in a barren wasteland, or a decaying forest. The best part about blights is that you can tailor them to any kind of party, as the 2025 Monster Manual has no shortage of options available to you.


## 4 Bandits

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Humanoid |
| HP | 11-169 |
| CR | 1/8-11 |

Bandits do a great job at making a survival environment feel grounded and real, while adding a real sense of stakes to the mix. The threat of the party losing valuable items may have them staying up through the night to guard their supplies, incurring points of Exhaustion.

As with many of the creatures on this list, there are tons of different Bandit options you can use, but they work best in groups. Sending in just one bandit will be pretty easy for even a beginner party to take down. Also, make sure to have fun and provide backstories, names, and a general motif for your group!

## 3 Giant Wolf Spider

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Beast |
| HP | 11 |
| CR | 1/4 |

Giant, mutated arachnids will always be right at home in any kind of survival or post-apocalyptic landscape. Flavor these creatures with acidic or biohazard-like poison, and you'll have a perfect creature for your campaign. You can also use a couple of these creatures in tandem with one another for better action economy in combat.

In addition to their standard abilities, consider using webs to make combat more interesting. Perhaps the Spider's lair is all difficult terrain thanks to its web-like encasement. Or, maybe party members can suffer from the Restrained condition if they're not careful.

## 2 Flesh Golem

| HP | 127 |
| CR | 5 |


Plus, the Flesh Golem is no pushover. Its Berserk feature, which essentially gives it a second wind in battle, can turn a long combat session on its head. Additionally, it has Magic Resistance and can absorb Lightning damage, meaning players may have to think on their feet to stay alive.

## 1 Invisible Stalker

| Stat | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Elemental |
| HP | 97 |
| CR | 6 |

For a great sense of suspense, have your party continuously hunted by an Invisible Stalker as they move through their campaign. They'll be sure to sleep with one eye open if they think they're being hunted, even if they can't see their target!
