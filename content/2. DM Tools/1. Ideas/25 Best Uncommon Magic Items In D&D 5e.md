## 23A Driftglobe Will Illuminate Any Dungeon

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2024), pg. 254

One of the most basic but pervasive obstacles in any dungeon complex or cave network is the sheer darkness found inside. Species such as tabaxi and deep gnomes can see in the dark just fine. However, it's best if the entire party can see, so communal light sources like the driftglobe are a must.

Characters often have torches in their inventory, but driftglobes are fancier. When used, **a driftglobe will give off light equal to either the Light or Daylight spells** and will last much longer than a torch. Such light can impose disadvantage on light-averse hostiles like drow, allowing the party to gain the upper hand while exploring hazardous realms like the Underdark.

## 22Gloves of Missile Snaring Are Ideal for Shutting Down Enemy Archers

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2014), pg. 172

![The Gloves of Missile Snaring magic item in the DnD 5e Dungeon Master's Guide](https://static1.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Dungeons-Dragons-Gloves-of-Missile-Snaring.jpg)

Image by Wizards of the Coast.

The Gloves of Missile Snaring is an iconic _D&D_ magic item that has saved many characters' lives. When wearing them, a character can use their reaction to deflect a ranged weapon attack sent their way. It reduces the damage by 1d10 plus a character's Dexterity modifier. This can negate the damage from an arrow or javelin, and even take the bite out of a ballista bolt. The Gloves of Missile Snaring provide consistent protection from ranged attacks.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

Even better, they don't require attunement or charges. A _D&D_ character can take this reaction any number of times in an adventuring day. Particularly in adventures against more mundane opponents, they can protect players from dozens or even hundreds of points of damage. They're one of _D&D 5e_'s best Uncommon magic items for defense. Players should note that the monk class has the Deflect Missiles class feature, making Gloves of Missile Snaring redundant when [building powerful _D&D_ monk characters](https://www.cbr.com/dungeons-dragons-5e-shadow-monk-guide/). So, it's usually other martial characters who would want these gloves.

The best Uncommon magic items in _D&D_ serve multiple purposes. Plenty of magic items exist to provide light, but none do it with the versatility of the Gem of Brightness. This small prism has 50 charges and can be used to cast light in a radius slightly greater than that of a torch. However, the Gem of Brightness's usefulness doesn't stop there.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

The bearer of the Gem of Brightness can expend charges to make attacks that will blind targets, either one at a time or in a wide cone. Blindness is one of _D&D 5e'_s most devastating status effects, especially against spellcasters. The Gem of Blindness cannot regain charges and will eventually run out. Before then, however, it's invaluable both in and out of combat.

## 18Cloak of Protection Is Ideal "Armor" for Squishy Spellcasters

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2014), pg. 159

![A blue cloak of protection in D&D](https://static1.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/a-blue-cloak-of-protection-in-d-d.jpg)

Image by Wizards of the Coast.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

A Cloak of Protection has a straightforward but very notable effect. It provides a +1 bonus to Armor Class and all saving throws. Every single _D&D 5e_ character will benefit from being harder to hit and more resistant to spells. In particular, the Cloak of Protection isn't armor, so even spellcasters and other fragile characters can wear it and be that much tougher in combat.

The Cloak of Protection continues to be beneficial throughout [an entire _D&D 5e_ campaign](http://www.cbr.com/dnd-prepping-one-shot-versus-campaign-session/), particularly as its Armor Class bonus can stack with improvements from other items. Its general defensive properties make the Cloak of Protection one of the best Uncommon magic items in _D&D 5e_, and since it's relatively cheap and easy to find, the party might end up having a Cloak of Protection on two or even three characters at once. At higher levels, though, different classes will opt for fancier robes to up their game, such as Robe of the Archmagi at the legendary rarity.

## 17Elemental Gems Summon a Meat Shield for Their Users

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2014), pg. 167

![A group of Fire Elemental Myrmidons in DnD](https://static1.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/ezgif-5-c03a28a068-Cropped.jpg)

Image by Wizards of the Coast.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

The Elemental Gems are a set of Uncommon magic items that offer a wildly powerful effect for their relatively low level. There are four variations, one for each different elemental type: fire, water, air, and earth. When shattered, they summon a _D&D_ elemental of their corresponding kind. This summoning follows the same rules as the Conjure Elemental spell, meaning the elemental is nominally under the summoner's control.

However, if the conjurer loses Concentration, the elemental turns on them. This makes an Elemental Gem a high-risk, high-reward item at lower levels best saved for emergencies. The Elemental Gems are best used during a tough fight if the party needs a disposable monster to serve as a bullet sponge or keep a dangerous enemy busy. It's especially good for squishy casters who need a meat shield to keep the heat off of them.

## 16Decanter of Endless Water Provides Water Whenever It's Needed

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2014), pg. 161

![A Decanter of Endless Water magic item in the DnD 5e Dungeon Master's Guide](https://static1.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/dnd-decanter-of-endless-water-an-adorned-horn-filled-with-water.jpg)

Image by Wizards of the Coast.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

The Decanter of Endless Water is one of the best Uncommon magic items in _D&D_ _5e_ due to its sheer versatility. This non-attunement item can produce near-infinite amounts of water. When the Decanter of Endless Water is uncorked, it can trickle out in a stream (about one gallon's worth per turn), a fountain (five gallons per turn), or a geyser (generating 30 gallons).

These effects aren't game-changing in their own right. However, they also aren't limited by charges or attunement. The Decanter of Endless Water trivializes one aspect of resource management. Furthermore, canny players can find other uses for that much water. One example is for survival's sake, such as if the party is trekking across a desert.

## 14+1 Weapons Provide Decent Stat Boosts and Can Harm Any Foe

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2014), pg. 213

![A Sword of steel and sinew in D&D art.](https://static1.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sword-of-steel-and-sinew.jpg)

Image by Wizards of the Coast.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

Weapons are some of the most prolific magic items in _D&D 5e_. The game has many swords, daggers, and more with spectacular additional effects. However, a plain +1 weapon is often all a character needs. It doesn't drain life from its enemies or roll extra damage dice; it does what a regular weapon does, but much more effectively.

A +1 weapon is the equivalent of an Ability Score Improvement in a character's primary stat. It also allows a character to exceed the typical caps on attack bonuses and damage. Its most valuable ability is letting a [martial _D&D_ player character](https://www.cbr.com/dnd-physical-classes-ranked/) ignore enemy resistances or immunities to non-physical damage. Although there are more exciting magic weapons, a +1 is reliable for parties at a low level until they are ready for +2—or even +3 weapons—later in the campaign.

## 11Keoghtom's Ointment is a Handy Healing Item

### Dungeon Master's Guide (2024), 324

![Keoghtom's ointment magical item in D&D 5e 2024 art.](https://static1.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/keoghtom-s-ointment-magical-item.jpg)

Image by Wizards of the Coast.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

Healing potions of varying strength are commonly found in _D&D_, using quite a few d4 to heal the drinker. If the party wants some variety, though, the DM can sprinkle in a few jars of Keoghtom's Ointment instead, which heals with different dice and can even rid the character of the poisoned condition. By contrast, healing potions only restore HP and do nothing else.

It will have 1d4 +1 doses, though a generous DM will probably set each one to the full five doses in a challenging dungeon if this item is found in the Tomb of Annihilation, for example.

The Gauntlets of Ogre Power don't provide a bonus in the conventional sense in _D&D 5e_. Instead, they set the wearer's Strength score to 19 if their Strength is normally below that. They have the same effect whether the character normally has 8 Strength or 18. This gives them two distinct uses.

[](https://www.cbr.com/premium/?ref=ad_removal)

Characters can use the Gauntlets of Ogre Power to enhance their Strength until they surpass 19 naturally and move onto a different magic item. Alternatively, a character can accept staying at 19 for the rest of the campaign and focus on other ability scores or feats. The Gauntlets of Ogre Power are one of the best Uncommon magic items in _D&D 5e_ for their character-building potential.
