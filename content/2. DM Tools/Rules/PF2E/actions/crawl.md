# Crawl (basic) [>](rules/core-rulebook/chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Single Action")
[move](rules/traits/move.md "Move Combat Trait")  
*Source: Core Rulebook p. 470*  


- **Requirements**: You are [prone](content/2.%20DM%20Tools/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Prone) and your Speed is at least 10 feet.
- **Activity**: Single Action

You move 5 feet by crawling and continue to stay [prone](content/2.%20DM%20Tools/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Prone).
