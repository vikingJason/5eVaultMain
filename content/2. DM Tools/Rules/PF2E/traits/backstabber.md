# Backstabber  
*Source: Core Rulebook p. 282*  

When you hit a [flat-footed](content/2.%20DM%20Tools/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Flat-footed) creature, this weapon deals 1 precision damage in addition to its normal damage. The precision damage increases to 2 if the weapon is a +3 weapon.
