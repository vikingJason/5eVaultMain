# Dragon  
*Source: Core Rulebook p. 631*  

Dragons are reptilian creatures, often winged or with the power of flight. Most are able to use a breath weapon and are immune to [sleep](rules/traits/sleep.md "Sleep Effect Trait") and [paralysis](content/2.%20DM%20Tools/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Paralyzed).

- **Categories**: Creature Type
