# Devil  
*Source: Core Rulebook p. 630*  

A family of fiends from Hell, most devils are irredeemably lawful evil. They typically have greater [darkvision](rules/abilities/darkvision.md), immunity to fire, and telepathy.

- **Categories**: Creature
