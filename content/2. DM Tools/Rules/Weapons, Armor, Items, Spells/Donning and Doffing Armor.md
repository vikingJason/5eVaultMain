# **Donning and Doffing Armor**

The time it takes to don or doff armor depends on the armor’s category.

**Don**: This column tells how long it takes a character to put the armor on. (One minute is 10 rounds.) Your Armor Class is only increased by armor that you have fully donned.

**Remove**: This column tells how long it takes to get the armor off. If you have help, reduce this time by half.

Table: Donning Armor

| Armor Type   | Don        | Doff      |
| ------------ | ---------- | --------- |
| Shield (any) | 1 action   | 1 action  |
| Light Armor  | 1 minute   | 1 minute  |
| Medium Armor | 5 minutes  | 1 minute  |
| Heavy Armor  | 10 minutes | 5 minutes |
