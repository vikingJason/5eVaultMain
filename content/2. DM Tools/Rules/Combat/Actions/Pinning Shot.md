### Pinning Shot

|    |
|----|
| Prerequisite: ranged weapon that uses arrows or bolts |

When you make an attack with this weapon, you can choose to shoot part of a Large or smaller creature's clothing and pin the clothing to the ground or nearby object or structure.

The target must be wearing clothing, such as boots or a cloak, and must be on the ground or within 5 feet of an object or structure.

Make an attack roll with this weapon. On a hit, the target can't move from its current space. A creature, including the target, can take its action to rip the cloth, pull out the arrow or bolt, unclasp or wriggle out of the clothing, or free itself in some other way by succeeding on a [[Strength]] or [[Dexterity]] check (target's choice).

The target must make only one check to free itself, using the highest DC of the characters performing this weapon option, regardless of the number of arrows or bolts holding its clothing in place.

#### Special

A [[dagger]], [[dart]], or [[javelin]] can be used to perform this weapon option.
