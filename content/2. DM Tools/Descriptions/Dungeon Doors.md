1. This old, waterlogged wooden door is swollen into its frame and impossible to open. The wood's integrity is so poor that pulling hard on the doorknob rips it right out of the door, leaving a big hole. While impossible to open, it is easy to break into pieces.
2. This odd stone door has a horizontal seam at approximately chest height. There is a lever at the side of the door in the down position. When the lever is raised, which takes quite a bit of force, the top and bottom parts of the door slide into the ceiling and floor, respectively.
3. This soot-covered iron door is circular and made of many petals. A button is in the frame to the right side of the door. When the button is pushed, the door irises open slowly and stops after it opens halfway. The soot is left over evidence of fire damage that has warped the panels slightly, preventing them from fully dilating.
4. The imposing brass door is covered with stylized designs of snakes. At the door's center is a large brass snake head with its mouth wide open protruding from the door. Its fangs are shiny, almost as though they are dripping poison. It is large enough for someone to reach in. Inside the open mouth is a pull ring that opens the door when pulled.
5. This simple wooden door has dozens of small indented shelves carved into it. Hanging in each space is a small bronze bell. Opening or closing the door causes the bells to start ringing in a loud, haunting melody.
6. This iron-plated door is shaped to look like a person trapped inside while the iron was pounded into place. You can see the outline of the front half of the body from the hallway. Both hands are sticking out into the corridor. The door opens when both hands are grasped and pushed inward towards the room. The back of the door shows the outline of the back half of a person.
7. This wooden door has a small 1'x1' window built into it at head height, showing a quiet study with a happily burning fire in a fireplace beyond. There are even refreshments set out on a table. The view is an illusion.
8. This door is jet black. Even light shone on it seems to be absorbed. When touched, it feels greasy, like it is made from a pool of oil suspended in the shape of a door.
9. This set of double doors is made of two giant sheets of quartz. The left door is smoky white quartz, and the right is smoky yellow. The center of each door contains a glyph carved into the material. Each door has a doorknob at approximately waist height.
10. This door is shaped like a giant ear. A sign above the door says, "Feed me." A player needs to share a bit of gossip to open the door.
11. Multiple powerful hammer blows have sorely damaged this wood door sometime in the past, but it is still intact. A few of the hammer blows broke through to the other side. If you stick a mirror through one of the small holes, you can see that the door is barricaded by several metal bars and cannot be opened short of destroying it.
12. This door is made of frosted glass painted with a scene showing a brave knight slaying an evil dragon. There is no obvious way to open it, but it can be shattered. The secret to unlocking it is to adopt the knight's pose in front of it.
13. This blacked steel door has a carving of the moon on it. The moon phase being shown is the one that will be active this evening. There is no mechanism to open it. If the door is revisited at moonrise that evening, a knob magically appears for ten minutes.
14. This brass door has a musical score engraved into it. There is no doorknob. The only way to open the door is to play the musical score on an instrument.
15. This iron door is cold enough to cause flesh to stick to it when touched. The space between the door and frame is covered with frost.
16. This wooden door has a wheel in its center. Turning the wheel releases baffles that keep the door firmly in place. The baffles are designed to keep the space beyond airtight.
17. This door's surface has a series of eight tiles in a frame. There is one blank space where a 9th tile could fit. The tiles can slide into that (or any subsequent) empty space. When they are aligned to show the picture etched into the tiles, the lock mechanism is disabled.
18. This wooden door starts to glow faintly when you approach. Up close, you can see that the light is caused by hot flames burning on the door's surface but do not seem to be consuming it. The doorknob is glowing a ruddy red from the heat radiating off it.
19. This metal door is so pockmarked with holes that it almost looks like a series of crisscrossing iron bars. Blood stains are apparent in the hallway near the door.
20. This solid copper-plated door has a series of five locks on one side. Each lock is more difficult to pick than the last, regardless of the order chosen.
21. This door is hollow. Knocking on it suggests it is filled with some liquid. If the door is opened forcefully, it breaks, and several pints of acid leak through the hole.
22. This rough wooden door is covered with many small spikes that wiggle slightly as you approach. Even the doorknob is covered with these spikes.
23. This door is covered with a series of interlinked gears and several handles that can turn them. Working the various handles allows the gears to be moved into the position needed to unlock the door.
24. This door is just a carving on the wall depicting a woodland scene. If each forest animal shown in this idyllic scene is touched in alphabetic order, the carving turns into an actual door.
25. This wooden door has a stiff wooden face carved into it. When approached, it animates and speaks very slowly. It asks for food (tree sap) for the right to pass through the doorway.
26. This door looks like a waterfall that has been captured in one instant of time. It is solid to the touch but radiates a feeling of suppressed energy. If it is pushed open, the time freeze breaks, and a torrent of water flows into the hallway and room beyond the now open space.
27. This doorway seems open, except for the smoke spewing from several holes in the frame, hiding what lies beyond it. The smoke obscures a portal that transports those who walk through to a different part of the dungeon.
28. There is a curtain of cheap bead strings in front of this wooden door. The beads are all somewhat sticky and make it difficult to interact with the door. The beads also jangle noisily when pushed aside, making a noise that attracts nearby creatures.
29. This solid-looking wooded door is made even more solid by three iron bands screwed into it. Each of the bands is covered with a series of runes. If the runes are read in order, the door unlocks. If not, it is impossible to open.
30. This solid metal door is locked with a very complex locking mechanism. Any thief examining it knows that it would be better to find the key rather than attempt to pick it. There is a two-inch space at the bottom of the door. If someone looks through this space, that person sees a key lying on the floor, tantalizingly close but just out of reach.
31. This long, thin doorway is shaped like a coffin cover. When the door is swung open, you are greeted by a skeleton that reaches out to shake your hand. If you accept the handshake, the back of the coffin swings aside to let you into the room beyond.
32. This door is made of a series of ivory tusks crisscrossing horizontally, effectively creating a solid door of tusks. Some of the tusks have rusty red stains that look like dried blood. The only way to open the door is to give the tusks a blood sacrifice; just a few drops will do. Once it is sated, the tusks retract into the wall to allow access to the room.
33. This wooden door has been carved into the shape of an orc and covered in an orc's hide to make it look more realistic. The door can be opened by pushing both eyes at the same time. They are buttons that release the door's locking mechanism.
34. This tricky door doesn't know if it wants to be open or closed. Every time you approach, it starts closing. When you walk away, it starts to open again. With some study, you can learn the pattern and dash safely through the opening before it closes.
35. This door is a large flat slab of stone that rests in tracks on the wall. It is pushed to the side to reveal the entrance to the space beyond. The slab is quite heavy and hasn't been opened for a very long time. It takes two very strong people and a bit of luck to be able to push it aside. Greasing the track beforehand helps.
36. This door is tiny, just one foot tall. It is set in the wall approximately four feet above the floor. It is made of copper and shines brightly in the torchlight. It has a very complex lock mechanism that is difficult to pick, but if successful, it opens and reveals a button in the wall. If the button is pushed, seams suddenly appear, and a full-sized door slides into the ceiling.
37. This door is made of not-yet-set clay barely held together by some magical force. You can push through the door as though you were walking through difficult terrain, but if you hesitate at any point in your journey through the door, it will harden and trap you within.
38. This wooden door is crisscrossed with copper strips. In the background, you feel the vibrations of hidden machinery. If you are using darkvision and not a torch or similar light source, you see faint sparks of lightning arcing across the wire strips.
39. This wooden door has a decorative clock carved into it, complete with the hour and minute arms—the arms act as the key to opening the door. The face of the clock is decorated with a princess wearing just one shoe. Like Cinderella, setting the clock to 12 is the key to this door.
40. This imposing iron door looks like a bank vault. It has a massive ships-wheel-like device right in the center. A small slot at the side of the vault is just big enough to fit a coin. The wheel can be turned when a coin is placed in the slot. It takes a total of 20 GP worth of coins to turn the wheel enough to unlock the door.
41. This door is lying on the floor. There is a vast pile of rubble filling a door-spaced opening nearby. The debris magically vanishes if the door is lifted, set back on the hinges in this space, and then closed and opened.
42. This door is a perfectly blank white surface with a handle to allow it to be slid open. However, the player does not see the door. They each see a vision of their greatest fear. They will see the door beyond if they make a successful wisdom saving throw. Otherwise, they will believe that their greatest fear has come to life and is right in front of them.
43. This marble door has a riddle engraved upon it (the second riddle of the Sphinx from mythology.) It reads, "There are two sisters: one gives birth to the other, and she, in turn, gives
birth to the first. Who are the two sisters?" The door opens if the correct answer (night and day) is given.
44. This iron door has a shelf sticking out from it approximately waist high. The shelf has a chess set that shows a game in progress. The door opens if you can figure out the move that gives you checkmate against the other side. (Use an intelligence save or set up an actual chess example.)
45. This door is a tapestry hanging down over the open space to the room. The tapestry is quite ugly and dirty. It portrays a broken-down room with several (level appropriate) monsters on it, all seemingly passed out drunk. When you sweep the tapestry aside, you see the broken-down room just as pictured on the tapestry, but the monsters are awake and alert.
46. This door seems to be made of wood. (It is a wood veneer hiding a mechanism wedged between the two thin panels.) When the doorknob is turned, the mechanism activates and becomes a powerful magnet, dragging every bit of metal in a 20' radius to the door—the mechanism powers down after ten minutes.
47. This doorway is a sheet of cloudy ice. It is cold enough to cause damage if touched. There is no doorknob or any other obvious way to open the door. Fire will eventually melt the ice enough to show that it is not really an ice door but part of a room completely filled with a massive chunk of glacial ice.
48. This door is elegantly carved in a scene of angels and devils and quite shiny when torchlight lands on it. The doorknob feels quite strange. The entire door structure is one giant waxwork, elegantly carved and coated in varnish to preserve it. It is pretty thick, but a determined group can hack through it in a few minutes.
49. This giant-sized oak door has dozens of keyholes around its outer edge. An inspection shows that they all are associated with locks that penetrate the doorframe. Only one of the dozens of keyholes is the actual lock for the door. When it is unlocked, the entire door plus the "fake" doorframe can swing open. Given the number of locks, it can take the party quite a while before being able to open the door.
50. A wooden door depicts a scene of a dining area of a children's home. It shows several half-starving children eating their meager bowls of food and one boy in front of the cook holding a dirty bowl up for food. The door is magically locked and opens if someone says, "More please!" or something similar.
