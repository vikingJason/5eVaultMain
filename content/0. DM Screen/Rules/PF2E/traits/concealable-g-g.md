# Concealable  
*Source: Guns & Gears p. 231*  

This weapon is designed to be inconspicuous or easily [concealed](content/0.%20DM%20Screen/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Concealed). You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [Stealth](compendium/skills.md#Stealth) checks and DCs to hide or conceal a weapon with this trait.
