# Vital Shot [>>](rules/core-rulebook/chapter-9-playing-the-game.md#Actions "Two-Action")
[gunslinger](rules/traits/gunslinger-g-g.md "Gunslinger Class Trait")  
*Source: Guns & Gears p. 110*  

**Class** gunslinger; **Subclass** Sniper
- **Activity**: Two-Action

Your careful shot against an unsuspecting opponent pierces a vital artery or organ. Make a ranged [Strike](rules/actions/strike.md). If the target is [flat-footed](content/0.%20DM%20Screen/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Flat-footed), the [Strike](rules/actions/strike.md) deals an extra die of weapon damage, and the foe takes [persistent bleed damage](content/0.%20DM%20Screen/Rules/Conditions/Conditions.md#Persistent%20Damage) equal to the amount of precision damage from your [One Shot, One Kill](rules/actions/one-shot-one-kill-g-g.md).
